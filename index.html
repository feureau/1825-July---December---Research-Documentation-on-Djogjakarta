Archival Sources & Collection Scope: Systematic transcriptions of 178 archival notes and typed extracts compiled by historian Peter Carey from Dutch colonial and Javanese collections, covering the outbreak and opening campaign of the Java War (July–December 1825, plus retrospective 1816–1824 records and early 1826 continuations). Principal collections include: ARA Collectie De Kock (Nos. 18, 179, 180, 181, 182, 183, 197), Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Leiden University Library B.P.L. 616 (Collectie Nahuys van Burgst), ANRI Archief van Jogja / Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81, KITLV H 698 b, and Governor-General Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary (Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië, 1860).
