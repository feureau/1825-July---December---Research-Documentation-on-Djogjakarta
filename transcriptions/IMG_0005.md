* **People:**
  * **Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock** (`De K / de Kock`): Listed under *Military Commanders & Officers* (line 361). Commander of the Netherlands Indies Army and Government Commissioner during the Java War.
  * **Godert Alexander Gerard Philip Baron van der Capellen** (`Van der C`): Governor-General of the Netherlands Indies (1816–1826).
  * **Captain Kumsius** (`Kumsius`): Commander of the military column sent from Semarang whose rearguard of 40 men under Lt. Boogert was destroyed near Magelang/Yogyakarta in late July 1825.
  * **Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier** (`P.F.H. Chevallier`): Assistant Resident of Yogyakarta under A.H. Smissaert.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja IV** (`Danu Redjo`): Listed under *Patih of Yogyakarta* (line 184).
  * **Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry** (`MacGillavry`): Listed under *Residents of Surakarta / Yogyakarta* (lines 319, 346). Acting Resident of Yogyakarta (1825) and Resident of Surakarta.
* **Places:**
  * **Cianjur** (`Tjianjor / Ciancur`): Priangan Regency, West Java.
  * **Bandung**: West Java.
  * **Semarang**: North coast headquarters of the Dutch forces.
  * **Magelang**: Capital of Kedu residency.
  * **Djogja / Yogya**: Yogyakarta Sultanate.
  * **Kedu** (`Kedu`): Key residency south of Semarang.
  * **Solo**: Surakarta Sunanate.
