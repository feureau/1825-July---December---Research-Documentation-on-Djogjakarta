# Transcription of IMG_0048.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0048.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, *Geheim en Kabinetsarchief* / Causes of War Dossier, continuation)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-15 to 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of the anonymous courtier's letter detailing the final countdown to war; describes the construction of the Yogyakarta–Pasar Gede highway violating ancestral customs and sparking outrage; details intelligence received at Tegalrejo that Patih Danureja IV and Resident Smissaert planned to capture Pangéran Dipanagara; notes that Danureja IV was recalled from Pajang where he had been inspecting road projects; records Resident Smissaert issuing an ultimatum on 4 Besar (20 July 1825) demanding Dipanagara meet him alone at Fort Vredenburg; recounts Dipanagara's younger brother (Sendinegara) carrying the summons to Tegalrejo, where Dipanagara flatly refused on grounds of premeditated capture; describes Pangéran Mangkubumi siding with Dipanagara and joining him at Tegalrejo; records Dipanagara and Mangkubumi sending a joint letter demanding the immediate dismissal of Patih Danureja IV.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Sendinegara (youngest brother / royal messenger).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Pasar Gede (Kota Gede), Tegalrejo (TR), Pajang (Padjang), Kraton Yogyakarta, Fort Vredenburg.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Month Besar 4th (20 July 1825), Summons to Fort, Patih dismissal demand, Old customs (adat), Armed mobilization.
