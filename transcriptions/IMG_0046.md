- **Date Range:** 1825-08-03 (Context: 1825-05 to 1825-07-25)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's analytical extraction of Radèn Mas Alip's deposition continued; records scout Moedo warning Pangéran Dipanagara that the Europeans were actively preparing to capture him; highlights Demang Buro Resso of Pajang raising the largest contingent of troops for Dipanagara's army; details Alip's two-month separation from his father living at the residence of his great-grandmother Ratu Ageng; recounts Ratu Ageng sending Alip to Tegalrejo with a conciliation letter two days before the Dutch attack, attempting to persuade Dipanagara that Europeans mean no harm; records Dipanagara furiously tearing up the letter with the historic rebuke: *"And does my grandmother want me to give myself up? A chicken has more fight in him."*
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Mas Alip, Moedo, Demang Buro Resso, Ratu Ageng, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pajang (Padjang), Tegalrejo (TR), Yogyakarta (Djogja).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Procès-Verbal, Rural troop mobilization, Defiance of matriarchal intervention, Arrest plot.
