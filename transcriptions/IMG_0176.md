# Transcription of IMG_0176.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0176.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-11-01 to 1825-11-20`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Military operations, administrative purges, and regional political shocks across Java in November 1825; Dipanagara is driven from his fortified stronghold at Selarong by General Van Geen's offensive and takes position in the valley of the Progo River; Lieutenant-Colonel Cleerens advances along the South Coast near the Progo rivermouth, temporarily threatening to trap Dipanagara between his detachment and Van Geen; Cleerens is subsequently pulled back through Kedu toward Pekalongan to defend the vulnerable Jabarangkah enclaves against new insurgent thrusts; Dipanagara adopts the exalted royal title of Sultan (*Sultan Ngabdulkamid Erucakra*) and issues a general proclamation ordering the annihilation of Europeans and Chinese, which Van der Capellen identifies as the ideological core of the war; Resident Anthony Hendrik Smissaert is summarily suspended from office for gross negligence and tactless governance and ordered to Semarang; Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, recently returned from administrative duties in Palembang, is appointed acting Resident of Yogyakarta with instructions to investigate the root origins of the revolt; Pangéran Paku Alam I is judged by colonial leadership to offer limited effectiveness due to advanced age and equivocal loyalties; Kedu, lamented by Van der Capellen as the *"beautiful garden of Java"*, is ravaged by insurgents, with both regents (Magelang and Menoreh) irrevocably lost to Dutch authority, necessitating urgent reinforcements from Colonel Cochius to Resident Le Clercq; western lands (Priangan, Cirebon, Banten) remain pacified; in Grobogan, private estate owner Johannes Augustinus Dezentjé and Assistant Resident of Salatiga Van Rheede van Oudtshoorn assist in organizing defense against Pangéran Sèrang; defection of the eastern *mancanagara*: the Regent of Kertasana, despite recent lavish professions of loyalty to Resident Beijer of Surabaya, defects to the rebellion; facing an acute absence of military drafts from the Netherlands, the colonial government charters merchant vessels of the *Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij* (NHM) to serve as naval troop transports.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Pangéran Dipanagara, Lieutenant-Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens, Major-General Joseph van Geen, Anthony Hendrik Smissaert [suspended Resident of Yogyakarta], Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven [acting Resident of Yogyakarta], Pangéran Paku Alam I, Colonel Frans David Cochius, Resident Le Clercq [Resident of Kedu], Johannes Augustinus Dezentjé [landholder / estate owner], J.F. van Rheede van Oudtshoorn [Assistant Resident of Salatiga], Resident Beijer [Resident of Surabaya], Regent of Kertasana.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Selarong, Progo River (Kali Praga), South Coast of Java, Kedu (Kedhu), Pekalongan, Jabarangkah enclaves, Yogyakarta (Djokja), Semarang, Palembang, Priangan, Cirebon, Banten, Grobogan, Salatiga, Eastern Mancanagara, Kertasana, Surabaya.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sultan Title (*Sultan Ngabdulkamid Erucakra*), Administrative Suspension, Acting Resident, *Nederlandsche Handel-Maatschappij* (NHM) Troop Transports, Mancanagara Bupatis.
