## Contextual Notes & Analysis
- **Contract Background:** Following Governor-General Van der Capellen's decree of 6 May 1823 abolishing European land leases (*landhuur*) in the Principalities, Javanese nobles were obligated to repay substantial indemnity sums to European leaseholders for improvements and advances.
- **Parties:** 
  - **Joseph Donatiën Boutet** (1788–1832): A prominent French coffee planter in the Vorstenlanden (Pajang/Klaten region).
  - **Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II**: Patih (Grand Vizier) of Surakarta (1812–1846).
- **Estates Involved:** The estates of **Tranjasan** (or Trañjan), **Madu Moyang**, and **Sempu** comprised 14 *jonks* with roughly 400,000 coffee trees.
- **Financial Terms:** The debt was calculated at 90,000 Spanish dollars, equivalent to 198,000 Dutch guilders (at the standard conversion rate of 2.2 guilders per Spanish dollar), with an interest rate of 3/4% per month (9% per annum), backdated to 1 May 1824. Sasradiningrat could not resume direct control of his lands until the entire debt plus accrued interest was liquidated, leaving European planters effectively in possession.
