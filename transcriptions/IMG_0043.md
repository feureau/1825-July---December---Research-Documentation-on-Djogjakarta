# Transcription of IMG_0043.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0043.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Printed Reports (H.G. Nahuys van Burgst, *Verzameling van Officiële Rapporten betreffende den Oorlog op Java in de Jaren 1825–1830*, Deventer: M. Ballée, vol. I, 1835, p. 14)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-03 (Context: 1825-07-18 to 1825-08-03; Published 1835)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Climax of Radèn Mas Alip's deposition (3 August 1825); records Ratu Ageng sending Alip to Tegalrejo with a letter two days before the Dutch attack, attempting to dissuade Dipanagara and reassuring him Europeans mean no harm; Dipanagara's furious reaction upon reading the letter, tearing it to pieces and declaring: *"Verlangt uwe grootmoeder, dat ik mij gewillig zal laten vangen? Neen! een kip laat zich zoo gemakkelijk niet vatten... en mij laat loopen."* ("Does your grandmother want me to let myself be caught willingly? No! Even a chicken does not let itself be caught so easily... and leave me alone."); Nahuys van Burgst adds critical commentary, pointing out that Alip was well known to be mentally unstable (*zeer zwak van hoofd*) and shortly thereafter defected back to Dipanagara; Nahuys finds the deposition's claims improbable, noting that Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV was devoted to the Dutch and openly reported family disagreements (such as with Pangeran Panular and Pangeran Blitar), yet never mentioned any hostility from Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Mas Alip, Ratu Ageng, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Panular, Pangéran Blitar.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Yogyakarta, Kraton.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Hoofd-muiteling (chief rebel leader), Zwak van hoofd (mentally defective / erratic), Defection, Matriarchal conciliation letter.
