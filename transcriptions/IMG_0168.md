# Transcription of IMG_0168.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0168.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Aanteekeningen van den G.G. Baron van der Capellen over den opstand van Dipo Negoro in 1825*, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], new series, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-24 to 1825-07-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official journal entries of Governor-General Van der Capellen recording the worsening military and political crisis across Central Java during the final days of July 1825. Insurgent bands multiply rapidly, pillaging and destroying colonial installations throughout the countryside. In Yogyakarta, Assistant Resident Chevallier takes up permanent residence within the Kraton alongside the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V and the Queen Mother to preserve the dynastic order, while Patih Danureja IV and Paku Alam I maintain their allegiance amidst acute terror and paralysis in the palace. Meanwhile, a colonial relief detachment of 120 men under Captain Kümpel escorting a government chest of silver money from Magelang to Yogyakarta is ambushed by rebel forces seven miles outside Yogyakarta; 40 men of the rearguard are slain and the silver captured. The neighboring Residency of Kedu is left completely undefended, compelling Resident Le Clercq to terminate his leave in Batavia and rush back to his post. In Yogyakarta, Resident Smissaert succumbs to extreme terror, completely abandoning executive initiative to Assistant Resident Chevallier. Confronted by acute garrison depletion caused by Dutch overseas expeditions in Celebes (South Sulawesi) and Sumatra, the government musters 150 men from the 700-man European garrison at Weltevreden (Batavia) to sail in haste to Semarang. Van der Capellen records grave reflections on the crisis: *"Never was the number of troops in the various garrisons so low, and this Rebellion, perhaps prepared long ago, can easily have serious ramifications in other parts of Java. Every one knows how easy it is for Javanese Princes, especially when they are under the influence of fanatical priests, to stir up the Javanese people to Rebellion and Robbery."*
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, P.F.H. Chevallier, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Ratu Ibu, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Captain Kümpel, P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Kraton Yogyakarta, Magelang, Kedu, Batavia (Weltevreden), Semarang, Celebes (Sulawesi), Sumatra, Java.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kraton Security, Silver Specie Escort, Rearguard Slaughter, Garrison Depletion, External Expeditions (Celebes / Sumatra), Administrative Paralysis, Royal Alliances with Religious Teachers (*"fanatical priests"* / santri).
