# Transcription of IMG_0101.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0101.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 181 / ANRI *Djogja Letters* [1825], Smissaert to De Kock, 13–14 August 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-13 to 1825-08-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Pangéran Mangkubumi inquires about Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing), asking how long he had led Dipanagara's *prajurit* troops (5 to 6 years), prompting Resident Smissaert to remark to General De Kock that Secadiningrat would be highly unlikely to join given his pro-European outlook and the virulent anti-Chinese character of the Java War, though Smissaert vows to keep him under close surveillance (Carey cross-references Leiden MS Babad LOr 2114 re: *magérasari* / *kapêdhak* troops performing Chinese-style martial arts, *"mêncak cara cina"*). Dipanagara's personal estates of Sukama and Seguwu are reported transformed into major rebel military mobilization depots. Temporary bridges are constructed across the Kali Progo (Praga) River for rebel infantry movements: north over Prawanang and Sendhang Pitu, south over Jodok and Mangir, and east over Tangkilan. On 13 August at 11:00 AM, rebels launch an assault on Dalem Pakualaman, driven back by Dutch artillery fire. Major insurgent strongholds along the post-road to Magelang are identified across Desa Bener, Tegalreja, Kuncen, Panggang, and Kasihan under the command of the former Demang of Kemlaka, Nitikrama, supported by field artillery. Smissaert demands reinforcements for Kedu. On 14 August, the Chinese camp in Yogyakarta is attacked and burnt to the ground, and Dalem Pakualaman is torched. A foraging and reconnaissance rebel detachment is spotted consisting of 40 royal Hussars of the Sultan's bodyguard with mounts and weapons, accompanied by 90 other rebels possessing good cavalry horses but poor armaments (surplus weapons from the Napoleonic Wars). Urgent supplies demanded by the garrison include ammunition, rice, salt, and arrack.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing), Pangéran Dipanagara, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Demang Nitikrama, Paku Alam I, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Sukama, Seguwu, Kali Progo (Praga), Sendhang Pitu, Prawanang, Jodok, Mangir, Tangkilan, Dalem Pakualaman, Magelang road, Desa Bener, Tegalreja, Kuncen, Panggang, Kemlaka (Quemlou), Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Prajurit*, *magérasari*, *kapêdhak*, *mêncak cara cina* (Chinese martial arts / Babad LOr 2114), Progo infantry bridges, Chinese camp burnt, Royal Hussars bodyguard, cavalry mounts, arrack, ammunition requisitions.
