# Transcription of IMG_0072.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0072.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Kopie No. 12 cont. [Part 2])
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Eyewitness combat report detailing the battle of Tegalrejo on the late afternoon of Wednesday, 20 July 1825; when envoy Nitimarta approaches, he is assaulted and fired upon with stones and musket balls; hundreds of armed villagers surge from the surrounding settlements to defend Dipanagara; colonial troops deploy into the flooded paddy fields (*sawahs*) behind the dalem amidst a torrential tropical downpour; Assistant Resident Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier identifies Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi in the field: Dipanagara is dramatically distinguished by his dress—wearing *"an Arabian and extremely distinctive priestly garment"* (white turban and Islamic robes) and surrounded by a dense phalanx of spearmen brandishing lances; Sub-Lt. Thierry, Chevallier, and Lt. Wiesemann lead a combined cavalry charge of Hussars and royal dragoons into the sawahs attempting to capture the rebel princes; the horses become immobilized in deep mud, and David of the Sultan's bodyguard is killed by pikes while several Dutch troopers are wounded; Chevallier withdraws the cavalry and orders 2nd Lt. Van der Plas to fire grape/canister shot into the villagers, killing 15 to 20 defenders; facing encirclement, Thierry orders a tactical retreat to Yogyakarta; Peter Carey highlights that Smissaert's official report deliberately suppresses all mention of the arson and total burning of Tegalrejo.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier (Assistant Resident), Sub-Lt. Thierry (7th Hussars), 2nd Lt. Van der Plas, 2nd Lt. Wiesemann, David (Sultan's bodyguard dragoon, casualty), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Surrounding sawahs (paddy fields), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 Kopie No. 12 pt. 2), Battle of Tegalrejo, Priestly Islamic vestments (*Arabian priestly garment / jubah*), Phalanx of spearmen (*ringed by men armed with lances*), Mud-trapped cavalry charge, Bodyguard casualties (killed by pikes), Artillery canister bombardment, Concealment of arson (*no mention made of burning of TR*).
