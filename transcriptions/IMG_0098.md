## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0098.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *Prince Abu Bakar's Defection, Arab Commanders, and Kraton Conspiracies, 7–9 August 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-07 to 1825-08-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles critical intelligence reports from *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54 dated 7 to 9 August 1825 uncovering palace defections, religious insurgent leadership, and the hollow shell of the Kraton. Smissaert clarifies that the anticipated attack on Yogyakarta on 6 August was postponed because Dipanagara convoked an emergency war summit of all allied princes and field commanders at Selarong. Simultaneously, Prince Abu Bakar—the father-in-law of Major Tumenggung Wiranagara—openly defects to Dipanagara. Abu Bakar, a haji, religious sayyid (*sheriff man*), and underworld organizer who had attempted to detonate the powder magazine in Fort Vredeburg during the British storming of 1812, casts off non-Javanese customs, cuts his hair (*cuts pigtail*), dons traditional Javanese garb, and deals in opium with bandit followers. On 9 August, reports alert the garrison that an insurgent column under an Arab commander (identified by Carey as **Sèh Ahmad**, son-in-law of al-Ansari) is marching from Klaten to storm the Dalem Pakualaman, supported by 2,000 to 6,000 insurgents massed in Rejawinangun and Sampins. Inside the deserted Kraton, where only women and intimate retainers remain, court loyalty collapses completely: Ratu Kencana (widow of HB IV and mother of the child Sultan) secretly conspires to defect to Dipanagara, while the trusted personal retainer (*panakawan*) of Ratu Ageng is caught and arrested in the act of packing up palace rice reserves to smuggle to Selarong.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Prince Abu Bakar, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Paku Alam I, Sèh Ahmad (Ansar Sharif), Ratu Kencana (Ratu Ibu), Ratu Ageng Tegalreja, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Selarong (Vilarong), Klaten, Dalem Pakualaman, Rejawinangun, Sampins, Fort Vredeburg, Kraton Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, British assault of 1812 (powder magazine plot), Arab military leadership (Sèh Ahmad), panakawan retainers, smuggling of rice reserves, palace conspiracies, opium trafficking, cutting pigtail / cultural renunciation.
