# Transcription of IMG_0056.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0056.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698 b; ANRI 'Archief van Jogja' [AvJ] 1825 / *Translaat Brieven: Beginning of the War*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-05-14 to 1825-08-27 (Retrospective sigillographic records: 1818 to 1825)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Intercepted intelligence report dated 27 August 1825 sent to Patih Danureja IV (*DR IV*) detailing Pangéran Dipanagara's military mobilization order requiring every single *jonk* of land to supply two fully equipped soldiers for the Holy War; first official archival mention of **Kyai Maja** (*Kyai Modjo*), Dipanagara's supreme religious advisor, noting that his village (Maja) is located close to Boyolali; forensic analysis of administrative seals (*cap / pratandha*): Patih Danureja IV transitions from a simple seal (used on 14 Puasa Bé 1752 / May 1825) to a Large Official Seal on Thursday, 3 Sura Wawu 1753 (18 August 1825); details the oval red wax seal of Wedana Gedhé Prajurit Radèn Tumenggung Mayor Wiranagara bearing the Javanese inscription *"1819 pratandha Radèn Mayor Tumenggung Wiranêgara"*; records the octagonal red-wax seal of Paku Alam I inscribed *"1746 [AJ] pratandha... Kangjeng Adipati Paku Alam"*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Kyai Maja, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Boyolali, Desa Maja (Pajang / Surakarta borderlands), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Jonk military quota (2 soldiers per jonk), Kyai (Islamic religious scholar), Wedana Gedhé Prajurit (Senior Royal Guard Commander), Sigillography / Official seals (cap, pratandha, red sealing wax), Javanese dates (Sura Wawu 1753, Puasa Bé 1752).
