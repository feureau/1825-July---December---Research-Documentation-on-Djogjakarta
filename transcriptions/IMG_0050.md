# Transcription of IMG_0050.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0050.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, *Geheim en Kabinetsarchief* / Causes of War Dossier: Letter from Queen Mother)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08 (Context: Circa August 1825)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's analytical notes on an intercepted Javanese royal letter from the Queen Mothers of Yogyakarta preserved in the Dutch Secret and Cabinet Archives; records the profound grief and humiliation (*isin*) felt by the royal matriarchs (Ratu Ageng and Ratu Kencono / Raden Ayu Maduretno) over colonial threats or rumors to exile the young Sultan Hamengkubuwana V to distant outer islands; laments the severe mistreatment and suffering inflicted upon the Javanese population by European officials; denounces court collaborators who cooperate with infidel Europeans to suppress and undermine the Islamic religion; concludes with the Queen Mothers formally endorsing and assisting the elevation of Pangéran Dipanagara as Sultan (*Sultan Abdulhamid Erucakra*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Ratu Ageng, Ratu Kencono (Ratu Ibu), Raden Ayu Maduretno, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Outlying islands (penal exile destinations), Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Geheim en Kabinetsarchief, Causes of War dossier, Queen Mother (Ratu Ageng), Royal exile threat, Collaboration against Islam, Elevation of Dipanagara as Sultan.
