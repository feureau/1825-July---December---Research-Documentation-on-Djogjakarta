# Transcription of IMG_0160.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0160.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Kedu Letters, September 1825; Collectie De Kock No. 182, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-09-27
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatch concerning the ongoing insurgent threat to Kedu posed by Yogyakarta rebel commanders and princes in late September 1825, specifically under the command of Tumenggung Abu Bakar. Defensive deployments are established to safeguard the southern frontier of Kedu: at Kali Djintung, a defensive garrison of 70 men is stationed supported by a 3-pounder artillery piece and indigenous auxiliary troops (*bantuans*) provided by the Regent of Magelang (Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I). Simultaneously, Assistant Resident Holmbergh van Beckfelt and three mobile columns commanded by Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens are tasked with advancing along the strategic mountain passes and river bridges on the direct approach road toward Dipanagara's stronghold at Selarong.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Tumenggung Abu Bakar, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Bupati of Magelang / Kedu), Assistant Resident Holmbergh van Beckfelt, Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens, Pangéran Dipanagara, Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu, Kali Djintung, Magelang, Selarong, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Southern Frontier Defense, 3-pounder Artillery, Indigenous Auxiliary Levies (*bantuans*), Mobile Columns (3 columns under Cleerens), Strategic Mountain Passes / Bridges, Assistant Resident (*Assistent-Resident*).
