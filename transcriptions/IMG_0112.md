# Transcription of IMG_0112.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0112.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Yogya Letters* No. 22, 31 December 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1822-07-17 to 1825-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official retrospective dispatch to the Minister of Marine and Colonies in The Hague summarizing the land lease crisis (*landverhuur in de vorstenlanden*). The Governor-General's correspondence (Decrees No. 154 of 17 July 1822, No. 100 of 20 May 1823, and No. 115a of 4 June 1823; Ministerial dispatch No. 47/116 of 23 June 1824) condemned European rural land leases as a grave political evil, ordering their immediate abolition. This abrupt edict imposed severe financial distress upon the Sunan of Surakarta, the Sultan of Yogyakarta, and high-ranking court princes, who were forced to immediately indemnify European planters for heavy unamortized capital investments and rent advances. By government resolution of 3 February 1824, a special Commission on the Vorstenlanden was formed consisting of Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (Resident of Surakarta), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), and Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (Second Resident of Surakarta) to audit coffee plantations and negotiate repayment agreements. The Commission submitted its final report from Surakarta on 30 August 1824 (forwarded to Batavia on 13 October 1824), concluding that the majority of compensation claims had been settled, with notable exceptions among private estates including Ulu Wallen, Gitangan, and Ketangan Pangor, leased out by prominent Surakarta grandees such as Pangéran Buminata and Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. van der Capellen, Minister of Marine and Colonies, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Pangéran Buminata (Surakarta), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih of Surakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Vorstenlanden (Principalities), Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, The Hague (Den Haag), Batavia, Ulu Wallen, Gitangan, Ketangan Pangor.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Landverhuur* (land rent / plantation leases), Commission on the Vorstenlanden, coffee plantations (*koffietuinen*), indemnity reimbursements (*restituties*), Ministerial decrees.
