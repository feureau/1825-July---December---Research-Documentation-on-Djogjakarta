# Transcription of IMG_0047.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0047.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, *Geheim en Kabinetsarchief* / Causes of War Dossier .1.)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08 (Context: 1824-05 to 1825-07)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's analytical notes on an intercepted anonymous letter from a Yogyakarta court official to his mother preserved in the Dutch Secret and Cabinet Archives; recounts the arrival of a dispatch from Governor-General Van der Capellen during the month of Puasa (Year Bé, c. May 1824/1825) summoning all nobility to the Kraton; describes the evening ball at the European Residency house following the Garebeg Mulud celebrations, where Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier openly humiliated Pangéran Dipanagara, treating him as though he were insane; notes that European guests became intoxicated at table and compounded Dipanagara's mortification; details the subsequent flashpoint when Resident Smissaert ordered the construction of a new road passing directly through Dipanagara's estate at Tegalrejo without permission, prompting Dipanagara to pull the boundary stakes out of the ground and causing indigenous workers to refuse to return to the site.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Governor-General G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Dessa Jetjang, Surambi mosque, Residency House (Fort Vredenburg), Tegalrejo (TR).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Geheim en Kabinetsarchief, Puasa Year Bé, Mulud Festival (Garebeg Mulud), Tegalrejo road markers (patok dispute), Mantri overseer.
