# Transcription of IMG_0104.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0104.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54, July–September 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-29 to 1825-09-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On 29 August (the 40th day since the outbreak of the Java War), Smissaert reports to the commandant at Klaten that Yogyakarta is completely surrounded by rebel forces who have severed all principal arteries; the garrison is stripped of basic provisions (unhusked rice /*padi*, meat, and fruits). An insurgent column of 1,000 men, featuring numerous *prajurit* and religious clerics (*madjis*) clad in white robes, advances toward Kedu, while the road to Pacitan remains impassable. On 2 September, Smissaert orders Captain Wiesemann (commander of the Sultan's Bodyguard) to tighten market security due to townspeople violently mobbing *warung* food vendors out of sheer desperation; entry and exit from the Chinese quarter (*Kampung Cina*) are barred. On 4 September, severe famine conditions inside the Kraton spark internal unrest and market riots, prompting Wiesemann and Captain Achenbach (garrison commander) to submit emergency reports. On 8 September, Smissaert writes to the Resident of Semarang reviewing two major assaults on Fort Vredeburg occurring on successive Mondays (8 and 15 August) and recounts hyperinflation where a wagon-load of rice reaches 400 guilders. Smissaert anticipates a personal assault by Dipanagara himself on 22 August (*mencak Surya*), which culminates in heavy fighting directed against the Dalem Pakualaman. A subsequent assault on Dalem Pakualaman on 7 September is repulsed.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Captain Wiesemann (Commandant of the Sultan's Guard), Captain Achenbach (Military Garrison Commander), Lieutenant Thierry (Hussars), Paku Alam I, Pangéran Dipanagara, Resident of Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Fort Vredeburg, Klaten, Pacitan, Kedu, Kampung Cina, Kraton Yogyakarta, Dalem Pakualaman, Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** 40th day of the war, white-robed clerics (*madjis*), *prajurit*, market looting (*warung* vendors), food famine, rice prices (400 guilders per wagon), Sultan's Guard, Hussars.
