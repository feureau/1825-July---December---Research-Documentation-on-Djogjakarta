# Transcription of IMG_0060.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0060.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Bijlage Smissaert No. 10; ANRI 'Archief van Jogja' [AvJ] 1825 / Intercepted Reports)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-14 to 1825-07-24 (Retrospective note citing De Kock: 1825-08-13)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation and conclusion of Ngabèhi Wiraprana's secret intelligence dispatch to Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*DR IV*); confirms that royal tiger hunters (*Tuwa Buru*) were ordered to ready their firearms and edged weapons, while social outcasts, bandits, and recalcitrant elements south of Yogyakarta (*het slecht volk*) from Karanguda and Mangir were summoned to Tegalrejo by Pangéran Dipanagara; reveals that on Thursday, 14 July 1825, Pangéran Mangkubumi visited Dipanagara to counsel moderation, but Dipanagara refused to listen because his thoughts were completely consumed and agitated (*zijne gedachten verwardt zijn*); details the testimony of Demang Nitikrama of Sampar (? Nampu; Carey cross-references General De Kock's journal entry of 13 August 1825 / dK 181) who brought tax revenues to the capital; records that Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert summoned officials on Thursday seeking intelligence, but spies had not yet reported back; Smissaert was absent on a weekend holiday at his country retreat at Bedoyo (*Bèdaya*) from Friday 15 July to Monday 18 July 1825, receiving the grave news only upon his return on 18 July, while the full catastrophic scale of the rebellion was not officially digested until 24 July.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Demang Nitikrama (of Sampar / Nampu), General Hendrik Merkus de Kock (referenced in Carey annotation).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Tegalrejo, Karanguda, Mangir (south of Yogya), Sampar / Nampu, Bedoyo (*Bèdaya*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Banditry / Underclass mobilization (*het slecht volk*), Dutch administrative negligence (weekend absence at Bedoyo), De Kock papers (*dK 181*), Tax collection in kind (*padi*), Secret intelligence summons.

* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Mangir & Karanguda**: Traditional bandit-inhabited districts south of Yogyakarta along the Progo.
  * **Bedoyo (Bèdaya)**: Smissaert's country estate where he spent the critical weekend of 15–18 July 1825.
  * **Tegalrejo**: Dipanagara's estate and epicenter of rebellion.
* **Terms:**
  * **Het Slecht Volk (Bandits / Highwaymen)**: Marginalized rural groups recruited by Dipanagara to mount initial ambushes against Dutch supply trains and communication lines.
  * **Padi (Paddy)**: Unhusked rice delivered as land-rent or tax tribute.
  * **De Kock Papers (dK)**: Military diary and dossier series compiled by General De Kock during the Java War.
