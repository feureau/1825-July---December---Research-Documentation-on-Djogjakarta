# Transcription of IMG_0173.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0173.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-08-22 to 1825-08-31`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Insurgent forces launch repeated ferocious attacks on Yogyakarta on 22 and 24 August 1825 following Lieutenant-Colonel Achenbach's arrival, with 8 coordinated assaults repulsed with heavy colonial casualties in wounded; in Kedu, Resident Le Clercq faces desperate siege conditions with attacks from all sides and the Regent (Bupati) of Menoreh missing; ironical financial dilemma in Kedu, the sole residency with a surplus in hard silver specie, where local cash requests complicate Van der Capellen's fiscal management; border skirmishes destabilize the southern frontiers of Cirebon, Tegal, and Pekalongan while provincial capitals remain tranquil; rebellion flares across the fragmented princely enclaves of Jabarangkah, severing the Great Post Road between Semarang and Pekalongan; Van der Capellen dispatches his personal military adjutant Lieutenant-Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens as military commander of Pekalongan to assist Resident Van der Poel; Cleerens pacifies key Jabarangkah sectors with reinforcements from Batavia to secure vital links to Kedu; northern rebellion erupts across Demak and Grobogan led by Raden Ayu Serang (erroneously recorded in Dutch files as the daughter of Sultan Sepuh, refuted by Carey); Raden Ayu Serang leads rebel columns in person; Resident of Japara Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak evacuates his family and Jayèng-Sekar auxiliaries to Semarang; insurgents storm and incinerate the estate of colonial Land Tax Inspector Nagel, who barely escapes the conflagration.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Lieutenant-Colonel H.G. Achenbach, Resident Le Clercq [Resident of Kedu], Bupati of Menoreh [missing native regent], Lieutenant-Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens [military adjutant / commander in Pekalongan], Resident F.H. van de Poel [Resident of Pekalongan], Radèn Ayu Sèrang (Nyai Ageng Sèrang) [celebrated female warrior and commander], Pangéran Sèrang, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Diederik Willem Pinket van Haak [Resident of Japara], Inspector Nagel [colonial Inspector of Land Revenues / Land Tax].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djokja), Kedu (Kedhu), Menorèh, Cirebon (Cheribon), Tegal (Tagal), Pekalongan, Jabarangkah enclaves, Semarang, Batavia, Demak, Grobogan, Japara.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Silver Specie Surplus, Jabarangkah enclaves, Jayèng-Sekar [armed cavalry auxiliaries], Land Tax Inspector (*Inspecteur der Landelijke Inkomsten*).
