# Transcription of IMG_0102.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0102.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54 [July–Sept 1825], dispatches of 16 August 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-13 to 1825-08-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On the morning of 15 August at 7:00 AM, rebel forces launch a coordinated attack on Fort Vredeburg, engaging Paku Alam I's troops in sustained firefights and attempting to seize the bridge adjoining the Chinese quarter (*Kampung Cina*). Due to the defection of Pangéran Abu Bakar, villages adjacent to the Dalem Pakualaman become openly hostile. Rebels vandalize and destroy the Residency gardens, firing into the "Red House" of Van den Berg (the newly appointed government translator) and shattering windows. Captain Bouwens van der Boyen drives off rebel scaling parties carrying ladders prepared for storming the Kraton walls. Smissaert reports that on 13 August another flight / detachment departed Yogyakarta due to acute food shortages caused by rebel occupation of the fertile surrounding agricultural lowlands. High desertion rates among garrison troops attributed to lack of pay. Smissaert notes the capture outside Yogyakarta of three armed Javanese child soldiers under 15 years old carrying rifles, annotated by Peter Carey in red as early *"Pemudas"*. Districts are openly assaulted by rebels operating under a warrant/commission from Pangéran Mangkubumi and Surya Adimanggala. An intelligence spy from Kedu reports that local insurgents wish to replace Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat as leader. Auxiliary troops of the Mangkunagaran Legion (*MN*) are forced to retreat to Surakarta (Solo), while additional taxation and appanage lands fall under the direct control of Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Paku Alam I, Pangéran Abu Bakar (Dipawiyana I), Van den Berg (translator), Captain P.M.M. Bouwens van der Boyen, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Surya Adimanggala, Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing), Pangéran Dipanagara, Mangkunagara II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Fort Vredeburg, Dalem Pakualaman, Chinese Camp (*Kampung Cina*), Residency House / "Red House", Kraton Yogyakarta, Klaten, Surakarta (Solo), Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Scaling ladders, child soldiers (*pemudas*), Mangkunagaran Legion (*MN*), tax lands, Residency garden destruction, troop desertions, Dutch translator.
