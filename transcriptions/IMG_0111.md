# Transcription of IMG_0111.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0111.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 181 / ANRI *Djogja Letters* [1825–1826], October 1825 – January 1826).
- **Date Range:** 1825-10-28 to 1826-01-21
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On 28 October 1825, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven formally takes over the Residency of Yogyakarta from Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry. On 12 November, official notification is sent regarding the death of Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier, who passed away on 11 November at 3:00 PM from an abscess of the liver (*abces in de lever*) while preparing to seek medical care at Buitenzorg; the administration mourns that "the government loses a very able official." On 7 December, Radèn Mas Yudakusuma takes part in insurgent military operations surrounding Pasar Gèdhé (Kota Gede) and the sacred royal cemetery complex of Imogiri. On 12 December, Lieutenant-General De Kock departs Yogyakarta for Batavia; Coertzee remains official translator; rebel forces establish outposts at Gamping just three leagues west of Yogyakarta, while Dipanagara takes up personal military headquarters at Desa Dèkså along the border of the Kali Progo (Praga); Major-General Joseph van Geen arrives to take overall field command, accompanied by A.M.Th. Baron de Salis arriving from Tuban. On 7 January 1826 (arriving 21 January), extreme distress and financial destitution grip Yogyakarta, where colonial and Javanese officials receive no salaries and are forced to sell their private jewelry to purchase hyper-expensive food provisions; wet monsoon rains halt Dutch offensive maneuvers; a royalist counter-offensive led by loyalist Javanese princes against Dipanagara ends in failure; Dipanagara establishes positions at Pisangan, severing the high-road to Magelang; Surakarta auxiliary troops deploy under Tumenggung Suradilaga.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, P.F.H. Chevallier, Radèn Mas Yudakusuma, Coertzee (translator), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Major-General Joseph van Geen, Adriaan Mauritz Theodorus Baron de Salis, Tumenggung Suradilaga.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Buitenzorg, Pasar Gèdhé (Kota Gede), Imogiri, Batavia, Gamping, Desa Dèkså, Kali Progo (Praga), Tuban, Pisangan, Magelang road, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Succession of Residents, death of Assistant Resident (liver abscess), royal tombs of Imogiri, relocation of DP headquarters to Dèkså, monsoon paralysis of Dutch army, sale of personal jewels, loyalist princely offensive, highway blockade.
