# Transcription of IMG_0154.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0154.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Semarang Letters, July–December 1825; Collectie De Kock No. 180, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-22 to 1825-07-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatches from Resident of Semarang H.J. Domis to the Secretary of State (*Algemeene Secretarie*) of Governor-General Van der Capellen reporting the first repercussions of the outbreak of the Java War. On 22 July, Domis—while at Kopeng inspecting the government opium monopoly and planning to travel along the Tuntang River to Wirasari to inspect government ricefields—receives reports from the Residents of Surakarta and Yogyakarta that Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi have risen in rebellion. Domis immediately institutes internal security controls, ordering all Javanese to carry passes when moving from village to village (*désa ke désa*), while Colonel von Jett marches south towards Yogyakarta. On 24 July, following news via Resident Nahuys of the murder of Dutch emissaries dispatched from Kedu to Yogyakarta, Domis mobilizes northern coastal defenses: regents of Demak and Kendal are ordered to assemble armed levies; the Assistant Resident and inspector of land revenues (*Opziener der Landelijke Inkomsten*) at Grobogan are placed on high alert; forward observation posts are set up along the southern frontier; artillery is concentrated at Salatiga; Jayèng-Sekar mounted police are mobilized; and the neighboring Residencies of Japara, Juwana, and Pekalongan are warned. By 26 July, with an assault on Magelang reported as imminent and news that Dipanagara has assumed the sovereign religious title *Susuhunan Ing Alaga Panatagama*, the Regent of Semarang is stationed at Salatiga to organize and head a defensive regional police force.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** H.J. Domis, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Colonel von Jett, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Regent of Semarang, Regent of Demak, Regent of Kendal.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Kopeng, Kali Tuntang, Wirasari, Kedu, Demak, Kendal, Grobogan, Salatiga, Japara, Juwana, Pekalongan, Magelang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Resident, Assistant Resident (*Assistent-Resident*), Secretary of State (*Secretaris van Staat* / *Algemeene Secretarie*), Opium Monopoly (*Candhu / Madat*), Ricefield Inspections, Travel Passes (*passen* / *surat jalan*), *Opziener der Landelijke Inkomsten*, *Susuhunan Ing Alaga Panatagama*, Jayèng-Sekar (cavalry/armed police), Artillery, Frontier Observation Posts.
