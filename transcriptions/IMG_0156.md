# Transcription of IMG_0156.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0156.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Kedu Letters, July–September 1825; Collectie De Kock No. 182, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-24 to 1825-07-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatches from Kedu Resident P. le Clercq and Residency Secretary Breton van Groll to Governor-General Van der Capellen, reporting the military collapse and socio-economic distress in Kedu during the opening week of the Java War. On 24 July 1825, Breton van Groll reports the total disaster of a colonial specie remittance: a treasury shipment of f. 28,600 (Dutch guilders) dispatched from Magelang to Yogyakarta under an armed escort of 123 men and officers commanded by Captain Kümpel is ambushed by insurgent forces between 21 and 23 July (the attack occurring on 22 July). The entire remittance is captured and looted, and 40 colonial soldiers are killed. On 29 July, Resident Le Clercq returns to Magelang from Batavia, having encountered severe unrest along his route, and transmits an urgent situation report to General De Kock. Le Clercq praises Captain Duperron's skillful defense for preventing the total overrun of Kedu, but warns that unless immediate reinforcements are dispatched, Kedu will succumb entirely to the rebel princes, leaving Pekalongan and Semarang exposed to attack. Overland communication lines between Surakarta, Yogyakarta, and Kedu have been severed. Meanwhile, native auxiliary pikemen are retained at a daily wage of 3 duits, and severe harvest failure causes rampant rice price inflation, reaching 10 guilders per picul. Concurrently, Tumenggung Ranu Singa (pensioned former bupati in Kedu) is uncovered conducting clandestine correspondence with the rebel princes; Le Clercq orders his immediate arrest and exile to Semarang. While Magelang itself is temporarily quiet, the district of Probolinggo (Prabalingga) is reported completely fallen to the rebels.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Breton van Groll (Secretary of Kedu Residency), Captain Kümpel, Captain Duperron, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Tumenggung Ranu Singa (pensioned Bupati in Kedu / Ranuwidjaya), Pangéran Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu, Magelang, Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Pekalongan, Semarang, Batavia, Probolinggo (Prabalingga), Western Mancanagara (*W. Manca Negara*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Resident, Secretary of Residency, Specie Remittance (f. 28,600), Military Escort, Ambush / Treasury Capture, Overland Communications Severed, Auxiliary Pikemen (wages: 3 duits/day), Harvest Failure, Rice Inflation (10 guilders/picul), Subversion / Treason, Political Arrest / Deportation.
