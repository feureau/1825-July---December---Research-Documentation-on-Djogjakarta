# Transcription of IMG_0074.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0074.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 2789 / Ministerie van Koloniën; *Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, 26 July 1825 No. 1)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-19 to 1825-07-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historic resolution of Governor-General Godert van der Capellen in Council (*G.G. in Rade*) dated 26 July 1825 No. 1, responding to the emergency dispatches from Yogyakarta (19/20 July No. 12a), Semarang (21 July), and Surakarta (21/22 July); the colonial government officially confirms that Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, and five other Yogyakarta princes have entered into open insurrection, supported by massive uprisings of village populations; notes that the colonial cavalry and artillery sent to Tegalrejo were thwarted by flooded paddy fields rendered impassable by torrential rain, forcing an ignominious retreat to the city; records mobilization orders directing the 2nd Military District (Semarang) and the Surakarta Mangkunagaran Legion towards Klaten to relieve Yogyakarta; characterizing the uprising as grave and alarming "unexpected news", the Governor-General issues a supreme martial directive appointing Lieutenant Governor-General and Commander-in-Chief Hendrik Merkus de Kock to proceed post-haste to Semarang and Central Java invested with absolute, unconstrained civil and military powers (*volle civiele en militaire volmachten*) to crush the rebellion.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (Governor-General), Hendrik Merkus de Kock (Lieutenant Governor-General & Commander-in-Chief), Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Buitenzorg / Batavia, Yogyakarta, Semarang, Surakarta, Klaten, Tegalrejo.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Ministerie van Koloniën (ARA 2789), Besluit van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade (26 July 1825 No. 1), Full civil and military powers (*full civil / military powers*), 2nd Military District Semarang, Mangkunagaran Legion mobilization to Klaten, Dynastic revolt (7 Yogya princes).
