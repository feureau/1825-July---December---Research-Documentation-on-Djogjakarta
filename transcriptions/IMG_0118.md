# Transcription of IMG_0118.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0118.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 54)
- **Date Range:** 1826-02-04 to 1826-03-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Administrative and military logistical correspondence of acting Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*) from February to March 1826 preserved in ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 54. Shipments of monopoly opium arranged through burghers Baumgarten and Van Ghent (comprising 98 kattis of Bengal black opium and 61 kattis of Levant red opium, specifically packaged for smaller retail weights); diversion of all structural timber previously reserved for Residency house repairs to construct emergency defensive palisades safeguarding the Chinese and European quarters; receipt of four casks of silver specie totaling 10,160 guilders dispatched via Semarang; critical failures in hospital supply lines, with fresh vegetables sent from Surakarta (*Solo*) routinely spoiling in transit; complete exhaustion of financial liquidity in Yogyakarta blocking an urgent consignment of 800 piculs of rice from Surakarta, forcing Van Sevenhoven to plead with the Resident of Surakarta to release grain despite unpaid accounts; appointment under *procès-verbal* of a two-man civic rationing committee (Baumgarten and Driessen) to control salt distributions from government warehouses (*pakhuizen*); reported abuse and mishandling of Chinese opium tax-farmers at Klaten; and strategic administrative reorganization in the eastern theater transferring civil control of Pajang (*Padjang*) to Pangéran Adipati Arya Mangkunagara II, with orders recalling two Yogyakarta *tumenggungs* to the capital while Radèn Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat remains stationed in Klaten.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*), Baumgarten, Van Ghent, Driessen, Pangéran Adipati Arya Mangkunagara II (*Mangku Negara*), Radèn Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat (*Wiryodinegara*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Jogja / Djogja*), Surakarta (*Solo*), Semarang, Cirebon (*Cheribon*), Klaten, Pajang (*Padjang*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Opium (*Bengal black opium, Levant red opium, kattis*), Defensive Palisades (*Chinese / European camps*), Silver Specie (*casks, guilders*), Military Hospital Supplies, Rice Logistics (*picols*), Procès-Verbal (*Salt rationing commission, packhouses*), Opium Tax-Farmers (*Klaten abuses*), Administration of Pajang, Tumenggung.
