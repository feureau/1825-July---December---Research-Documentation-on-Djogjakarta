# Transcription of IMG_0066.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0066.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Kopie No. 4)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-19 (Javanese date: Tuesday evening, 4 Besar AJ 1752)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official Dutch translation of Pangéran Dipanagara's celebrated final letter addressed to Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (*"to his brother the Resident"*), dispatched from Tegalrejo on the evening of Tuesday, 4 Besar AJ 1752 (19 July 1825), hours before the outbreak of open warfare; Dipanagara formally acknowledges receipt of Smissaert's dispatch of 18 July assuring him of Dutch esteem and reminding him of his status as royal co-guardian; in phrasing marked by refined Javanese diplomatic irony (*samudana*), Dipanagara states that he is deeply touched (*zeer gevoelig*) and grateful for the Dutch government's past trust; however, addressing Smissaert's request that he come forward frankly with any grievances, Dipanagara coolly and unequivocally declines: *"Now I have no plans to do this [Thans ben ik niet van voornemens], and I have always entertained the Best regard for Your Exc. and for the Dutch Government"*; this letter represented the definitive rupture of personal diplomatic relations, leading directly to the dispatch of colonial troops to surround Tegalrejo the following morning.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara (*Prince Aria Dipanagara*), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Yogyakarta Residency.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 Kopie No. 4), Javanese diplomatic irony (*samudana*), Brotherly epistolary protocol (*to his brother the Resident*), Refusal of grievance consultation (*thans ben ik niet van voornemens*), Javanese calendar (Tuesday evening, 4 Besar Year Bé AJ 1752).
