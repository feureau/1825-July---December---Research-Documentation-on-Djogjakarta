# Transcription of IMG_0094.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0094.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA [Nationaal Archief, The Hague] *Collectie De Kock* / ANRI: *Resident Smissaert to General De Kock on the Total Encirclement of Yogyakarta, 30 July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey transcribes Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert's urgent strategic situation report dispatched to Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock on 30 July 1825, detailing the total siege of Yogyakarta, acute famine, and the destruction of colonial administrative infrastructure. All overland postal couriers to Kedu and Surakarta are severed. Relief troops under Colonel Frans David Cochius fight their way through from Surakarta with 100 European combat engineers (*genietroepen* / sappers), Mangkunagaran artillery, 100 remounts, and emergency rice rations, while Colonel von Jett holds Klaten with 200 Jägers and Mangkunagaran cavalry. Smissaert reports an accelerating wave of aristocratic defection to Selarong, noting that Pangéran Mangkubumi has formally assumed the royal title of Panembahan, while **"Dipanagara behaves like a Prophet"** (*DP behaves like a PROPHET*), exercising spiritual and political hegemony over his followers. Two high court bupatis have openly defected, including Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya, the veteran commander famous for hunting down Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja in 1810. With Yogyakarta under total blockade, earlier offensive sorties ended in disaster (confirming the capture of the f. 28,600 silver treasure convoy). Rice, paddy, and salt are unobtainable on the empty city markets, worsened by an extreme coin shortage. Carey highlights the devastating popular fury unleashed against colonial revenue posts: **every single tollgate (*bandar*) in the vicinity of Yogyakarta is burnt to the ground, Chinese tax-farmers are systematically massacred, and all commercial travelers and traders (*passers*) have fled in terror.** Finally, four artillery pieces and a dragoon detachment are captured near Klaten, while panic spreads toward Madiun.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi (Panembahan), Colonel Frans David Cochius, Colonel von Jett, Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja, Second Lieutenant Boogaard.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Selarong, Surakarta (Solo), Kedu, Klaten, Madiun, Fort Vredeburg.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ARA Collectie De Kock / ANRI, Panembahan title, Dipanagara as Prophet (*profeet* / spiritual leader), European sappers (Geniemannen), Mangkunagaran Legion, destruction of tollgates (*bandar*), massacre of Chinese tax-farmers, market paralysis, captured artillery.
