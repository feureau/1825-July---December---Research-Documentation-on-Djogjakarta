- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 54)
- **Date Range:** 1826-05-01 to 1826-06-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival research notes from ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 54 detailing military casualties, fiscal policy, profound administrative paralysis, and emergency food rationing in Yogyakarta during May–June 1826. Peter Carey records the combat record and fatal injury of former government Javanese translator Johannes Godlieb Dietrée: repeatedly accompanying Dutch flying columns (*colonne volante*), his deep linguistic and cultural knowledge rendered him invaluable until he sustained a mortal bullet/wound in the arm during the heavy assault storming the Kraton of Plered (*Pléréd*) in May 1826. On 12 June 1826, acting Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven consults H.J. van de Graaff concerning policies governing tollgate and bazaar tax collections (*tolhekken en bazaren*) amidst wartime disruption. On the same date, Van Sevenhoven submits a damning dispatch to the General Secretariat (*Gouvernements-Secretarie*) in Batavia on the total collapse of the Yogyakarta Residency secretariat since his takeover in late 1825: official correspondence is four months in arrears and nearly a full year behind schedule, directly attributable to the dereliction and chronic inefficiency of suspended Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert; secretarial staff are chronically incapacitated by illness attributed to Yogyakarta's unhealthy climate (*"someone always sick"*); Van Sevenhoven admits his own inability to manage detailed secretarial paperwork amidst military governance; and the untimely demise of Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier in November 1825 has plunged the secretariat into complete chaos. Finally, emergency rationing tariffs ordered by Jean Chrétien Baud to Van Sevenhoven are recorded, pricing daily rations of grain at 25 cents, meat at 20 cents, and candles at 8 cents per pound (*pond*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Godlieb Dietrée, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*), H.J. van de Graaff, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, P.F.H. Chevallier, Jean Chrétien Baud.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Djogja*), Plered (*Kraton of Plered / Paléréd*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Flying Columns (*colonne volante*), Assault on Kraton of Plered (May 1826), Tollgates & Bazaar Taxes, Secretariat Breakdown & Arrears (4 months backlog, 1 year behind), Smissaert's Dereliction, Death of Chevallier, Climate & Staff Illness, Emergency Rationing Tariffs (grain, meat, candles).
