- **Date Range:** 1825-07-26 to 1825-07-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles critical intelligence reports from *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54 revealing the extreme psychological panic, defensive isolation, and burgeoning messianic mythos surrounding Dipanagara during late July 1825. On 26 July, Resident Smissaert informs the General Secretariat in Batavia that insurgent bands have advanced within half a league (2.5 km) of Fort Vredeburg, plundering and killing with impunity. Entirely severed from communications with Kedu and Surakarta, Smissaert confesses that he cannot determine the true causes of the revolt and expresses complete alienation from the court: *"UNABLE TO PLACE any trust in the VAST MAJORITY of members of the Sultan's family."* On 29 July, relief forces under Colonel Frans David Cochius arrive, comprising European combat engineers (*genietroepen* / sappers) and Madurese auxiliary troops from the Mangkunagaran Legion, successfully resupplying the garrison with desperately needed rice, salt, and arrack after fighting through demolished bridges and caltrop-strewn roads. Smissaert tracks insurgent mobilization across rural border enclaves: Demang Nitikrama of Bulus, Demang Wirajentika of Jatisalen/Tempur, and Demang Sadirdja of Tangaten march toward the capital, while the border territories of Samper and Dalian channel extensive manpower to Selarong, where Dipanagara and Mangkubumi are constructing a rival *kraton* protected by flooded sawahs and spiked earthworks. Carey highlights the emergence of supernatural lore among the peasantry: reports spread that Dipanagara possesses the ability to fly (*"News that DP can FLY — whether in RAIN or DRY"*), accompanied by conflicting rumors of his physical illness. Concurrently, Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara takes military command at Kalasan to confront incoming royalist forces from Madiun.

  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Fort Vredeburg, Bulus, Jatisalen (Tempur), Tangaten, Samper, Dalian, Selarong, Kalasan, Madiun, Surakarta (Solo), Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, European sappers (Geniemannen), Madurese Mangkunagaran troops, Fort Vredeburg siege, caltrops (bamboo spikes / ranjau), Kraton Selarong, messianic flight / levitation lore (*kesaktian*), distrust of royal family.
