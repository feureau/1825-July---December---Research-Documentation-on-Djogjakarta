# Transcription of IMG_0114.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0114.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Yogya Letters* No. 22 / Secretary-General to Minister of Colonies No. 70, Batavia, 31 December 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1824-12-01 to 1825-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatch transmitting the financial balance sheet for the indemnification of European landholders (*landhuurders*) and the long-term state lease of Jabarangkah and Karangkobar. Total capital outlay owed to European planters stood at f. 1,109,932.15, with pre-war accumulated interest through December 1824 of f. 105,671.20, projected administrative overhead (1824–1831) of f. 492,058.24, and capital interest (1824–1831) of f. 538,025.55, bringing total government outlay to f. 2,245,688.03; offset against estimated revenues from the confiscated estates (1824–1831) calculated at f. 2,446,568.40. The dispatch details terms of the 30-year treaty lease (1 January 1825 – 1 January 1855) for Karangkobar and Jabarangkah: the colonial government leases these territories from the Sunan of Surakarta for f. 100,000 annually and from the Sultan of Yogyakarta for f. 26,000 annually, payable biannually in May/June and November/December. Direct compensation for lost court customs and tax revenues for Garebeg Mulud 1824 is fixed at f. 50,000 to the Sunan and f. 13,000 to the Sultan. In return, the Sunan's debt obligations for plantation capital improvements (reckoned at f. 400,000) are scheduled for liquidation in six-monthly installments.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Secretary-General of the Colonial Government, Minister of Colonies in The Hague, Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Batavia, Karangkobar, Jabarangkah, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Landverhuur* financial balance sheet, capital indemnity (*restituties*), 30-year state treaty lease (1825–1855), Garebeg Mulud 1824 tax compensation, colonial estate revenues.
