# Transcription of IMG_0061.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0061.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert No. 5; Yogya Letters 1825 / Intercepted & Official Dispatches)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-16 to 1825-07-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatch from Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert of Yogyakarta to Acting Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry of Surakarta dated 18 July 1825, acknowledging receipt of MacGillavry's secret warning of 16 July regarding insurgent troop movements; confirms intelligence that Pangéran Dipanagara was covertly massing a large armed force at Sepikul (*Sêpikul*), news corroborated by Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Rijksbestuurder*) and other senior regents; Smissaert authorizes MacGillavry and Surakarta forces to cross into Yogyakarta administrative borderlands and take military action against rebels as if operating in Surakarta territory, an inter-residency jurisdictional concession fully endorsed by Danureja IV; notes the prevailing administrative perplexity surrounding Dipanagara's motives, observing that the Javanese Patih and court regents possessed far deeper insight than Smissaert himself, while condescendingly diagnosing Dipanagara as mentally deranged (*"someone who is not well in his senses"*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (Acting Resident of Surakarta), Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Rijksbestuurder / Patih*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala / Solo), Gunung Sepikul (*Sêpikul*), Yogya-Surakarta borderlands.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 No. 5), Inter-Residency military cooperation, Cross-border hot pursuit authorization, Rijksbestuurder (Patih / Chief Minister), Feigned insanity diagnosis (*not well in his senses*).
