## Metadata

- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *Outbreak of the Java War, 29 July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey analyzes Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert's urgent dispatches to Batavia and Surakarta on 29 July 1825, highlighting the formal political constitution of Dipanagara's court and the decisive role of the Islamic religious leadership. At Selarong, senior prince Pangéran Mangkubumi is solemnly invested with the sovereign title of Panembahan. Smissaert warns Governor-General Van der Capellen that conditions have worsened drastically since 26 July: rebel standards are raised in every district under the active direction of religious scholars (*santri*, ulama, and village *kyai* / "fanatical priests") who are preaching holy war (*prang sabil*) and mobilizing the rural population against colonial rule. The insurgent leadership is so firmly established at Selarong that Dutch military commanders consider it impossible to dislodge them, particularly as disaffected princes defect to them at steady intervals. Within the encircled capital, loyalties remain divided: Major Tumenggung Wiranagara acts as the "truest servant of the Dutch," Patih Danureja IV labors industriously, and Paku Alam I sustains the besieged European garrison with grain and fodder while deploying cavalry toward Klaten. Nevertheless, the royal family firmly blocks attempts to appoint Paku Alam I as sole regent for the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V. Surakarta delivers 600 packets of sharp bayonets and 10,000 guilders in silver coin, even as intelligence uncovers the insurgents' strategic plan to storm Fort Vredeburg and seize the boy Sultan.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Mangkubumi (Panembahan), Pangéran Dipanagara, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Paku Alam I, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Radèn Tumenggung Radjaniti, Prince Blitar.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Selarong (Vilarong), Klaten, Surakarta (Solo), Fort Vredeburg, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, investiture of Panembahan, holy war / Islamic clergy mobilization (*fanatical priests*), rebel war flags, bayonets and silver subsidy (f. 10,000), Regency dispute (*wakil-dalem*), storming the fort.
