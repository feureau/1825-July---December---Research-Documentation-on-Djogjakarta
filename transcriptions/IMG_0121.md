## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0121.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 55)
- **Date Range:** 1826-08-11 to 1826-10-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival research notes and statistical ledgers from ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 55 recording labor crises, transport expenditures, illicit commerce, and colonial finances in Yogyakarta from August to October 1826. On 11 August 1826, acting Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*) informs the Director-General of Finances that recruitment of indigenous transport coolies (*koelies*) has broken down entirely because many laborers have joined Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*) while the rest demand higher wages, rendering the standard tariff of 2½ to 3 copper *duits* completely obsolete. Carey transcribes comparative residency transport ledgers showing coolie costs soaring from 1,010 guilders in 1823 to 2,495 guilders in 1824, itemizing expenditures for horse relocation (f. 147), specie convoys (f. 561), arrack (f. 149), coffee (f. 126), edible birds' nests (f. 145), smallpox vaccines and medical vaccinators (f. 54), military munitions and supplies (f. 991), special commissioners (f. 36), and official mail (f. 12.9). On 12 August 1826, Van Sevenhoven reports to the commander at Klaten regarding rampant Chinese opium smuggling, facilitated by the complete refusal of the rural population to cooperate with colonial authorities. Additional entries document the celebration of the King of the Netherlands' birthday (25 August 1826); a large colonial government relief loan of 22,000 guilders at 6% annual interest granted to planter Bouwens van der Boijen (6 October 1826); and an intelligence notice to the military outpost at Pisangan (28 August 1826) revealing that Chinese sub-agents of licensed opium tax-farmers are actively selling monopoly opium directly to rebel forces (*Rebels*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*), King Willem I (*King of the Netherlands*), Bouwens van der Boijen, Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Jogja / Djogja*), Klaten, Pisangan, Priangan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Coolie Labor Crisis (*koelies*), Wartime Wage Inflation (*2½ - 3 duits*), Government Transport Accounts (1823–1824), Opium Smuggling & Chinese Sub-Agents, Royal Birthday Festivities, Planter Debt & State Loans (*Bouwens van der Boijen, 22,000 guilders at 6%*), Smallpox Vaccination (*vaccinators*), Edible Birds' Nests (*vogelnestjes*).
