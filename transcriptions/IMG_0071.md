# Transcription of IMG_0071.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0071.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Kopie No. 12 cont.)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official report from Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert detailing the military mobilization and battle order assembled to march on Tegalrejo on the afternoon of Wednesday, 20 July 1825; after Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara and Radèn Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat return from Tegalrejo with Dipanagara's defiant refusal, Smissaert orders the military commander of the Dutch garrison fortress (*Kommandant van het Fort*) to deploy a mixed expeditionary column; the force comprises 25 infantrymen under 2nd Lieutenant Van der Plas, 25 cavalrymen of the 7th Hussar Regiment under Sub-Lieutenant Thierry, and two 2-pounder field guns under 2nd Lieutenant Roussel, reinforced by 24 dragoons of the Sultan's European Lifeguard under 2nd Lieutenant Wiesemann, Kraton prajurit troops under Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, and Pakualaman cavalry led by Princes Suryaningprang, Suryaningrat, and Suryaputra; the column is placed under the political direction of Assistant Resident Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier; halting 50 to 70 roods from Tegalrejo, Chevallier sends forward a Javanese envoy, Nitimarta, offering to negotiate if Dipanagara and Mangkubumi come out unarmed, which Carey notes was contradicted by Dutch forces opening fire while Dipanagara was still composing his written reply.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier (Assistant Resident), Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Sub-Lt. Thierry (7th Hussars), 2nd Lt. Van der Plas, 2nd Lt. Roussel (Artillery), 2nd Lt. Wiesemann (Lifeguards), Pangéran Suryaningprang, Pangéran Suryaputra, Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara, Radèn Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat, Nitimarta (emissary).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta Fortress (Fort Vredenburg), Tegalrejo.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Military Expedition Order of Battle, 7th Hussar Regiment (*7e Regiment Huzaren*), Sultan's European Lifeguards (*Dragoonders der Lijfwacht*), Kraton Prajurit infantry, Pakualaman cavalry detachment, Field artillery (2-pounder guns), Ultimatum negotiation distance (50–70 roods).
