# Transcription of IMG_0068.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0068.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / No. 6)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official Dutch archival translation of Pangéran Dipanagara's historic final reply to Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, penned at Tegalrejo on the morning of Wednesday, 20 July 1825 and carried back by royal emissaries Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara and Radèn Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat immediately prior to the colonial military assault; Dipanagara systematically rebuts Smissaert's accusations, declaring that the masses of armed villagers assembled around Tegalrejo had not been illegally coerced, but had flocked to him entirely of their own free will; refutes the allegation that he had stockpiled rice supplies; categorically refuses Smissaert's summons to enter the Residency fortress on the transparent grounds that he fears assassination or capture (*"I am not in a position to come Because I am afeared [afraid], and therefore I cannot appear"*); addresses his outstanding political ultimatum regarding the dismissal of Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*DR IV*), observing that because Danureja IV is simultaneously an official employee and instrument of the Netherlands Indies government, Smissaert cannot resolve the issue locally and must submit the petition directly to Governor-General Van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara (*Prince Baria / Aria Dipanagara*), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (Governor-General).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Yogyakarta Residency.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 No. 6), Voluntary peasant mobilization (*people have come out of their own free will*), Princely refusal to surrender (*cannot appear because I am afraid*), Demand for dismissal of Patih Danureja IV, Referral of royal dispute to Governor-General.
