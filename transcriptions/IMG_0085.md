# Transcription of IMG_0085.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0085.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: *Collectie Nahuys van Burgst* Port. 9 No. 2: *Letters of Resident Smissaert to Governor-General Van der Capellen*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20 to 1825-07-21
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey transcribes Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert's direct operational dispatch to Governor-General Godert van der Capellen (Letter No. 2, continuation) drafted on the night of 20 July 1825 following the clash at Tegalrejo. Smissaert diagnoses Dipanagara's armed uprising as stemming from *"fateful fanaticism"* (*noodlottige dweepzucht*) triggered by personal grievances and religious delusion, abetted by Pangéran Mangkubumi. Intelligence confirms that Dipanagara and Mangkubumi have retreated southward to Desa Secang (*Dese Sitjang*), adjacent to the former royal pleasure retreat (*pesanggrahan*) of Toyatumumpang (*Temekenpang*) near the limestone hill of Selarong, roughly six leagues (30 km) south of Yogyakarta. To counter the insurgents, the colonial administration and loyalist regents order a Kraton punitive force of 1,700 Javanese troops under Tumenggung Sepanegara, Tumenggung Ranadiningrat, and Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya—the veteran commander celebrated for crushing Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja's 1810 rebellion in Madiun. Smissaert urgently solicits mobile reinforcements from the commander of the Second Military Division, the Surakarta court, and the elite Mangkunagaran Legion, concluding with the political announcement that on the following day a new guardian (*wakil-dalem*) will be formally appointed for the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V to replace the rebel Mangkubumi.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Tumenggung Sepanegara, Tumenggung Ranadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya (Mertologo), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Mangkunagara II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Tegalrejo, Desa Secang (Sitjang), Toyatumumpang (Temekenpang), Selarong, Surakarta (Solo), Madiun.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Leiden Univ. BPL 616 Port. 9 No. 2, Second Military Division (2e Groot Militair Commando), Mangkunagaran Legion, pesanggrahan Toyatumumpang, fateful fanaticism (*dweepzucht*), guardianship (*wakil-dalem*).
