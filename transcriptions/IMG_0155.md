# Transcription of IMG_0155.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0155.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Kedu Letters, July 1825; Collectie De Kock No. 182, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-26 to 1825-07-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival report from Breton van Groll, Secretary of the Kedu Residency, dated 28 July 1825, documenting the violent outbreak of the Java War in Kedu and the desperate defense of Magelang. On 26 July, an insurgent army of approximately 800 rebels launches an assault across the frontier into the district of Probolinggo (Prabalingga), burning to the ground the colonial land tax government post (*Landrente post*) of Inspector Janssen, along with the residences and offices of Head Demang Radèn Truna Widjaya and Tax Collector Danukusuma. In response, colonial and indigenous forces are gathered under Captain Duperron and the Regent of Magelang, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I. On 27 July, the insurgents encamp near Magelang and launch a fierce assault on a patrolling detachment of Jayèng-Sekars (mounted indigenous armed police / volunteer citizen force), which repulses the attackers. Bupati Danuningrat dispatches a full battalion of indigenous pikemen to defend the perimeter. As renewed insurgent assaults are mounted against Magelang, Breton van Groll urgently warns that the garrison cannot hold out much longer without immediate reinforcements and relief from Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Breton van Groll, Inspector Janssen, Radèn Truna Widjaya (Head Demang), Danukusuma (Tax Collector), Captain Duperron, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Sayyid Alwi / Kyai Ngabèhi Danukrama, Bupati of Kedu/Magelang), Pangéran Dipanagara, Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu, Probolinggo (Prabalingga), Magelang, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Secretary of Residency (*Secretaris der Residentie*), Land Tax Government Post (*Landrente post*), Head Demang (*Demang*), Tax Collector (*Collecteur*), Jayèng-Sekar (mounted armed police / citizen volunteer force), Pikemen (*Battalion of Pikemen*), Military Encampment, Frontier Incursion.
