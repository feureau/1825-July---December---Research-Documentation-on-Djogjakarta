## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0088.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *Outbreak of the Java War in Yogyakarta, July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-11 to 1830-06-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles a tight chronological ledger from *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54 tracing the rapid escalation of defensive preparations, logistical panic, and administrative paralysis in Yogyakarta throughout July 1825. On 11 July, Resident Smissaert bans the sale of wild jungle coffee plants on the Yogyakarta market. On 14 July, he authorizes a 25,000-guilder subsidy for the Pakualam corps before departing for his weekend retreat, leaving Assistant Petrus Chevallier to inspect urban bridges and arterial roads on 19 July and urgently demand 400 ball cartridges (*scherpe patronen*) and flints for the Sultan's Bodyguard on 20 July. Immediately upon Smissaert's return to the Residency on 21 July, emergency armaments requisitions multiply: 10 rifles and 100 live cartridges for a European civilian home guard (*schutterij*), 400 rounds for Pakualaman troops, and 600 bayonets with 500 flints for Kraton prajurits, alongside a fateful request to the Kedu Residency for 30,000 guilders in cash from the Magelang treasury under armed escort. Even as Smissaert naively grants travel passes toward Pacitan on 22 July, the crisis explodes: on 24 July, he concedes that the initial strike column against Dipanagara and Mangkubumi collapsed as all villages along the route rose in arms, and by 25 July, the imperial highway to Surakarta is completely severed following the ambush and murder of a Mangkunagaran cavalry dragoon just one staging post outside Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, Paku Alam I, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Mangkunagara II, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Magelang (Kedu), Surakarta (Solo), Pacitan, Fort of Djogja (Benteng Vredebug).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, Paku Alam Legion (Pakualaman corps), Sultan's Bodyguard, civilian home guard, scherpe patronen (live ball cartridges), treasury convoy (f. 30,000 / Magelang), Mangkunagaran Legion dragoons, coffee plant trade ban.
