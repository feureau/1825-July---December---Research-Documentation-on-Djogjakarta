# Transcription of IMG_0133.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0133.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Solo Letters 1825–1826)
- **Date Range:** `1825-07-25` to `1825-07-27`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatches from Resident of Surakarta Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry to Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. baron van der Capellen describing the explosive outbreak of hostilities in central Java. Following the inconclusive colonial expedition of 24 July 1825, Surakarta dispatches f. 10,000 in silver specie and 200 of Sunan Pakubuwana VI's royal troops to reinforce Yogyakarta. MacGillavry reports widespread anti-colonial sentiment and warns that the oppressed rural peasantry is poised to rally behind Pangéran Dipanagara, with Chinese tax-farmers becoming the primary targets of popular slaughter. Spies are posted to surveil provincial *bupatis*, particularly the son of the late rebel Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III in Madiun. Meanwhile, logistic supplies forwarded to Klaten encounter violent disruption, a Mangkunagaran Legion dragoon carrying dispatches is ambushed and killed, and MacGillavry urgently confers with Patih Sasradiningrat II and Pangéran Buminata, advising Batavia to immediately abolish oppressive transit tollgates (*bandar*) and redress grievances over the annexed Jabarangkah territories.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, G.A.G.Ph. baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Pangéran Buminata, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), First Lieutenant Thierry
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Klaten, Madiun, Jabarangkah, Mataram
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock, Algemeen Rijksarchief (ARA), Resident of Surakarta, Governor-General, Mangkunagaran Legion (Dragoons), Sunan's Troops, Tollgates (*Bandar*), Jabarangkah Lands, Chinese Tax-Farmers, Silver Specie (*Guilders*)

### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[25 July 1825]**<br>*(MacGillavry to GG)* | **Dispatch from Resident H.M. MacGillavry to Governor-General Van der Capellen:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Expedition of 24 July:* Reports that the military expedition of 24 July 1825 has yielded no decisive result in quelling the insurrection.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Relief Sent to Yogyakarta:* MacGillavry has dispatched f. 10,000 in specie (silver guilders) to Yogyakarta along with 200 infantry from the Sunan's royal troops to relieve the besieged residency.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Danger of General Rebellion:* Notes acute fears of a general insurrection erupting across Mataram and the Surakarta realm: *"the oppressed people will be ready immediately to follow them [Dipanagara's forces], & the Chinese are their first victims."*<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Surveillance of Mancanagara Bupatis:* Secret spies and surveillance agents have been posted to watch suspect *bupatis*, especially Radèn Rongga's son [Radèn Rongga Prawiradiningrat] in Madiun. |
| **[27 July 1825]**<br>*(MacGillavry to GG)* | **Dispatch from Resident H.M. MacGillavry to Governor-General Van der Capellen:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Klaten Supply Line:* Emergency food supplies and live cattle have been dispatched to Klaten to provision advancing detachments.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Interception of Couriers:* A dragoon of the Legion van Mangkoe-Negoro [Mangkunagaran Legion] carrying urgent dispatches and letters to Yogyakarta has been ambushed and killed on the high road.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *High Council with Kraton Dignitaries:* MacGillavry holds an urgent conference with Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II and Pangéran Buminata (uncle of the Sunan); reports intense court and popular resentment regarding the arbitrary annexation and financial disputes surrounding the Jabarangkah lands.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Abolition of Tollgates Urged:* Urges the immediate abolition of transit tollgates (*bandar*) to pacify the excited rural population, citing horrific atrocities and indiscriminate massacres committed against Chinese tollgate keepers and their families. |
