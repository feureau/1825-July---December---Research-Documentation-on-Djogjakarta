# Transcription of IMG_0110.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0110.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ARA 4132 & ANRI *Djogja Letters* [Oct–Dec 1825], October–November 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-10-20 to 1825-11-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On 20 October, Radèn Ngabéhi Sumajaya and Mas Ngabéhi Sumatirta (confidants of Pangéran Adipati Purbaya / Sunan Pakubuwana VII) travel to Setas but suffer deliberate humiliation from the Dutch Inspector of Coffee, who refuses them even a stool to sit upon. On 23 October, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven officially assumes office as Resident of Yogyakarta, relieving Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry. Following the major engagement of 24–25 October where De Kock attacks Selarong and forces Dipanagara's withdrawal across the Progo River, a general public notice is posted on 27 October urging displaced villagers to return to their desas. On 31 October, Patih Danureja IV attempts to dispatch two Yogyakarta tumenggungs to stabilize Pajang, but both Yogyakarta and Surakarta tumenggungs defect en masse to Dipanagara, alongside the Tumenggung of Mataram. On 12 November, Van Sevenhoven reports to the Secretary-General at Buitenzorg that Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier has died of acute liver inflammation ("HEATING in his LIVER"). On 16 November, the formal coronation festivities for the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V take place at the Kraton, attended by the Resident. On 30 November, Van Sevenhoven reports to the Resident of Semarang concerning Colonel Hoon's punitive military expedition against Radèn Ayu Sérang in Demak/Grobagan; meanwhile, Dipanagara remains relatively unmolested along the banks of the Kali Progo (Praga), with his primary insurgent vanguard stationed at Gamping.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Ngabéhi Sumajaya, Mas Ngabéhi Sumatirta, Pangéran Adipati Purbaya (Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Pangéran Dipanagara, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, P.F.H. Chevallier, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Radèn Ayu Sérang, Colonel Hoon.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Setas, Pajang, Mataram, Selarong, Kali Progo (Praga), Gamping, Demak / Sérang, Buitenzorg (Bogor), Semarang, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Coffee inspection slight, succession of Residents, mass defection of Tumenggungs, Battle of 24/25 October, death of Chevallier (liver disease), coronation festivities of HB V, military expedition against Radèn Ayu Sérang.
