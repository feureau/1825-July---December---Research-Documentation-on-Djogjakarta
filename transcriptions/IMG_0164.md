# Transcription of IMG_0164.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0164.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Diary of Van der Capellen, July 1825 – Jan 1826*; *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], 1860, vol. 11, p. 367).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-24 to 1825-07-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official diary entries and high-level correspondence of Governor-General Van der Capellen recording the escalating political-military emergency across Central Java between 24 and 26 July 1825. Surakarta Resident H.M. MacGillavry secures solemn assurances of Surakarta court loyalty: Pangéran Mangkunagara II and the *Rijksbestuurder* (Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II) dispatch a detachment of mounted Mangkunagaran cavalry to protect colonial communications. On 26 July, following an emergency Council of the Indies meeting in Batavia on 24 July, Governor-General Van der Capellen entrusts all extraordinary dictatorial civil and military powers to Lieutenant-Governor-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock to restore order in the Vorstenlanden. Van der Capellen records his profound astonishment in his journal: *"Such a rebellion, in the middle of the calm and peaceful condition of Java was an occurrence, which had not occurred to me, and whose consequences are incalculable. The more so because the prime motives for the Rebellion are unknown to me."* Van der Capellen deems it vital that De Kock proceed immediately to the Princely Courts. Reports from Yogyakarta deteriorate sharply: Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier remains continuously inside the Kraton guarding the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V and his mother, while Paku Alam I remains staunchly loyal amidst pervasive panic. Meanwhile, an armed escort under Captain Kümpel dispatched to Magelang to secure government silver is ambushed and wiped out by Dipanagara's forces. In Kedu, the town of Magelang lies entirely undefended while Resident Le Clercq hastens back from leave in Batavia amidst rumors of his assassination. Smissaert's paralyzing fear leaves effective authority entirely in the hands of his assistant.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Pangéran Mangkunagara II (Prangwedana), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Rijksbestuurder / Patih of Surakarta), P.F.H. Chevallier, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Ratu Ibu, Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Captain Kümpel, P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta, Yogyakarta, Batavia, Magelang, Kedu, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Council of the Indies (*Raad van Indië*), Extraordinary Powers (*Volmacht* / Supreme Military Command), Mounted Cavalry (*Mangkunagaran Legion*), *Rijksbestuurder*, Specie Remittance Ambush, Kraton Defense, Child Sultan, Administrative Incompetence / Panic.
