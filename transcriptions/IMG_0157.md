# Transcription of IMG_0157.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0157.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Kedu Letters & Mémoire of Lt-Gen De Kock, July 1825; Collectie De Kock No. 182, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-26 to 1825-07-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival notes on dispatches from Kedu Resident P. le Clercq to Lieutenant-General De Kock and entries from De Kock's official military *mémoire*, detailing the spread of the rebellion into southern Kedu. In Probolinggo, the European estate lessee/planter Janssens loses all his property in the uprising, prompting colonial leaders to stress the imperative return of the expeditionary force from Celebes (Sulawesi) to Java. Although most of Kedu was initially quiet, Dipanagara's clandestine emissaries accompanied by 100 Yogyakarta troops rapidly stir the populace to insurrection. The entire district of Probolinggo (with 35,000 inhabitants) south of Magelang defects to the rebels, although the district police remain loyal. An intercepted rebel courier carries a summons from Dipanagara (translated into Malay; see Appendix). A sworn interrogation (*procès-verbal*) of courier Madan Besari (Hasan Besari) reveals that Dipanagara's principal grievances against the Dutch and Patih Danureja IV centered on bad treatment, the pegging of roads, and bridge work encroaching upon Tegalrejo, underscoring Dipanagara's personal hatred for Danureja IV (despite Dutch confidence in the Patih's fidelity). On 29 July, insurgent forces brandishing two red and yellow *payung* (royal parasols) appear outside Magelang near Mount Tidar. Captain Duperron organizes the defense of Magelang, leading a daring sortie composed of a small body of European soldiers alongside local forces assembled by the Regents of Menoreh and Magelang, totaling 1,200 pikemen.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Janssens (European planter in Probolinggo), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Madan Besari (Hasan Besari, Dipanagara's courier), Captain Duperron, Regent of Menoreh, Regent of Magelang (Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu (Southern Kedu), Probolinggo (Prabalingga), Celebes (Sulawesi), Magelang, Tegalrejo, Mount Tidar, Menoreh, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** European Planter (*huurder*), Celebes Expedition, *Mémoire of Lt-Gen De Kock*, Intercepted Rebel Summons, *Procès-Verbal* (sworn deposition), *Payung* (royal status parasol: red and yellow), Sortie, Pikemen (*Prajurit Watang* / 1,200 men).
