- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (A.D. Cornets de Groot Letters / Dispatches to Coastal Residents of Rembang, Gresik, and Surabaya)
- **Date Range:** `1825-09-26` to `1825-09-26`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official circular dispatches from Acting Resident of Surakarta A.D. Cornets de Groot dated 26 September 1825 to the colonial Residents of Rembang, Gresik (*Grissee*), and Surabaya, alerting them to the insurgent capture of Ngawi and the imminent disruption of northern river transport. Cornets de Groot emphasizes that Ngawi must be recaptured immediately by government forces, as it forms the vital strategic choke-point and river transshipment hub for the entire rice supply (*beras*) flowing from the fertile inland plains of Madiun and Magetan down the Solo River (*Bengawan Solo*). The loyalty of the Surakarta bupatis of Dungarasa and Taruban, as well as the joint Yogyakarta *bupati wedanas* of Madiun (Radèn Mas Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat and Radèn Tumenggung Rangga Prawirasentika), is viewed with intense concern. Carey adds prominent biographical annotations regarding the rebel commander at Ngawi: Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma was a royal sister of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh); despite her imperial royal blood, she was married off to an ordinary official (*orang cilik*), namely the Bupati of Munèng, Radèn Tumenggung Yudakusuma—a social slight and deep grievance later highlighted in colonial post-war memoirs (*Waterloo mémorie*). Meanwhile, the Yogyakarta regents of Kalangbret and Rawa openly defy General De Kock's summons to report to Surakarta, with only the Bupati of Munèng and the two senior Madiun *bupati wedanas* answering the summons.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** A.D. Cornets de Groot, Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Radèn Tumenggung Yudakusuma (Bupati of Munèng), Radèn Mas Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Rangga Prawirasentika, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Residents of Rembang, Gresik, and Surabaya
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Ngawi, Bengawan Solo (Solo River), Madiun, Magetan, Dungarasa, Taruban, Munèng, Kalangbret, Rawa (Tulungagung), Surakarta (Solo), Rembang, Gresik (Grissee), Surabaya
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Strategic Rice Supply (*Beras* / Logistics), Bengawan Solo River Trade, Waterloo Mémorie, Commoner (*Orang Cilik*), Bupati Wedana of Madiun, Coastal Residencies, Regency Defiance
