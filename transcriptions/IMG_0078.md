# Transcription of IMG_0078.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0078.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81 [Dj. Br. 81]; *Letters concerning DN – 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-18 to 1825-07-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Exhaustive archival register from ANRI Dj. Br. 81 recording colonial and court dispatches during the first week of the Java War; Smissaert authorizes Surakarta forces to cross into Yogyakarta territory to suppress the rebel gathering at Mount Sepikul, while dismissing Dipanagara as an irrational "superstitious prince" declared mad by Patih Danureja IV and court regents; on 20 July 1825, following the debacle at Tegalrejo, Smissaert dispatches frantic appeals for military reinforcements to Colonel Von Teer in Semarang (2nd Military Division), the garrison commander at Klaten, the colonial supervisor in Pacitan, and Resident of Kedu; appeals urgently to Surakarta for the deployment of Mangkunagara II's Legion; on 21 July, Assistant Resident Chevallier intercepts a letter from Van Vlissingen, administrator of the European-leased Kembang Arum estate (belonging to Beyens and Van den Berg), reporting that Surakarta peasants and villagers have spontaneously crossed the border to join Dipanagara and Mangkubumi; Smissaert orders the Mangkunagaran Legion detachment at Klaten to march immediately to relieve Yogyakarta; on 22 July 1825, the Regency government issues the sensational **Proclamation of Sultan Hamengkubuwana V**, formally condemning Dipanagara, Mangkubumi, Pangéran Adinagara, and Radèn Tumenggung Suryadipura as traitors subverting royal authority with fraudulent decrees, commanding all princes, regents, and subjects to hunt them down and deliver them **"Alive or Dead"** (*dood of levend*), with promises of official rank and rewards for loyal service.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Adinagara, Radèn Tumenggung Suryadipura, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, Colonel Von Teer (Semarang), Mangkunagara II (Pangéran Prangwedana), Van Vlissingen (estate administrator), Beyens & Van den Berg (plantation lessees).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta, Klaten, Semarang, Kedu, Pacitan, Kembang Arum estate, Mount Sepikul.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81, Emergency military requisitions, Mangkunagaran Legion intervention, Land-rent plantations (*Kembang Arum estate*), Spontaneous peasant rebellion (*Salanese inhabitants join DN*), **Proclamation of HB V (22 July 1825)**, Bounty decree (*delivered up alive or dead*), Collaborationist court regime.

* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Klaten**: Frontier garrison post between Surakarta and Yogyakarta, listed under line 434.
  * **Semarang, Kedu, Pacitan, Surakarta**: Administrative seats of Central Java, listed under lines 424, 427, 431.
  * **Kembang Arum Estate**: Agricultural plantation leased to European planters (Beyens and Van den Berg).
* **Terms:**
  * **Proclamation of 22 July 1825 ("Alive or Dead / Dood of Levend")**: Royal edict issued under Danureja IV placing a bounty on Dipanagara and rebel princes.
  * **Wandering from Desa to Desa**: Guerrilla mobile tactics adopted by Dipanagara and Mangkubumi immediately following the destruction of Tegalrejo.
  * **Mangkunagaran Legion**: Colonial auxiliary strike force dispatched to break the siege of Yogyakarta.
