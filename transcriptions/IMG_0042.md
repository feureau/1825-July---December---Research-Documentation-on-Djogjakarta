  - **Key Events:** Deposition of Radèn Mas Alip (3 August 1825) continued; records Dipanagara's alleged exclamation upon the death of Hamengkubuwana IV ("God dank!"); solicitation of court princes to join the planned rebellion, with Pangéran Adisuryo consenting and Pangéran Suryobronto refusing; Dipanagara's designation of P.F.H. Chevallier, J.G. van den Berg, and D.W.J. Bouwens as the first European officials to be assassinated or captured; identification of Dipanagara's inner council, including Demang Buro Resso of Poonghoongan (Pajang), two Arab religious scholars (Sheikh Achmat and his son-in-law Achmat), and intimate confidants Moedo, Wiriodikromo, and Djomohamad; Moedo's explicit warning to Dipanagara of colonial plots to arrest him (*opligten*); Alip's account of his two-month banishment to live with his great-grandmother Ratu Ageng.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Mas Alip, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Pangéran Adisuryo, Pangéran Suryobronto, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, J.G. van den Berg, D.W.J.   - **Key Entities (Places):** Pajang (Padjang), Poonghoongan (Pungungan), Kraton Yogyakarta, Tegalrejo.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Oproer (insurrection / rebellion), Opligten (arrest / capture), Demang (district chief), Raadslieden (spiritual councillors), Arab ulama network.
