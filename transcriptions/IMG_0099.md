# Transcription of IMG_0099.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0099.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54, 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-10 to 1825-08-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resident Smissaert issues general defense proclamations ordering all food supplies to be gathered into Fort Vredeburg, cutting down fruit trees and bamboo groves around the fort to deny rebel cover, and planting sharpened bamboo stakes (*ranjau*) at Kraton gates. Smissaert reports 500 rebel troops under Pangéran Jayakusuma stationed at the sugar mills of Jonsong/Jenar in Kedu. Strict security order bans all armed Javanese from Fort Vredeburg except Major Tumenggung Wiranagara. A colonial intelligence messenger dispatched to contact Pangéran Dipanagara reports failing to reach him, meeting only Pangéran Mangkubumi at Sampar Dalian in Pacitan; rebel intelligence warns of a major coordinated assault on Yogyakarta scheduled for 6 Sura (20 August 1825). Rebel construction of defensive stone walls at Selarong to enclose Dipanagara's garden retreat; presence of 10 Chinese fighters/converts in Dipanagara's insurgent ranks; widespread troop concentrations along the Kedu road under Demang Nitikrama across Desa Bener, Tegalreja, Kundèn, and Panggang. Courier tariffs recorded for Paku Alam's messengers (20 duits per day, 50 duits per league). On 15 August, a three-column rebel offensive launches against Dalem Pakualaman led jointly by Prince Blitar, Prince Adi Surya, and Prince Adi Negara.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Pangéran Jayakusuma (Pangéran Ngabéhi), Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Blitar, Pangéran Adisurya, Pangéran Adinagara (Suryéngalaga), Demang Nitikrama, Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Fort Vredeburg, Kraton Yogyakarta, Jonsong / Jenar (Kedu), Selarong, Sampar Dalian (Pacitan), Desa Bener, Tegalreja, Kundèn, Panggang, Dalem Pakualaman.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** General Proclamation, *bamboe ranjau* (sharpened stakes), sugar mills, kantoor ambtenaar / wadana, courier payments, duits, Sura (Muharram), Chinese combatants, three-column assault.
