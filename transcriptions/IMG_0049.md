# Transcription of IMG_0049.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0049.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, *Geheim en Kabinetsarchief* / Causes of War Dossier .2.)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20 to 1825-08
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's analytical notes concluding the anonymous court letter describing the outbreak of hostilities; details Resident Smissaert sending a final letter carried by messengers Sinounagara and Mundara; records Dutch and Kraton troops opening fire on Tegalrejo before any reply could be returned; identifies the assault force as led by Major Tumenggung Wiranagara and Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier, supported by 9 mantri of the Sepuh and Ketanggung palace regiments; recounts Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi escaping west through a gap in the encirclement lines while Tegalrejo was put to the torch; details their arrival on Sunday (24 July 1825) at the cave complex of Selarong, where they were joined by blood relations, bupatis, and palace mantris; records the solemn oath taken at Selarong to wage a Holy War (*Prang Sabil*) in the path of the Prophet; concludes with the writer's personal sign-off asking his mother whether to inform his father.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Sinounagara, Mundara, The Prophet Muhammad.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo (TR), Selarong, Yogyakarta (Djogja).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Geheim en Kabinetsarchief, Prajurit Keparak Sepuh, Prajurit Ketanggung, Holy War (Prang Sabil), Selarong headquarters, Bupatis and Mantris.
