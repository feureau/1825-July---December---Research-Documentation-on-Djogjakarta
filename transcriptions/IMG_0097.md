- **Date Range:** 1825-08-04 to 1825-08-07
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles intelligence dispatches from *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54 between 4 and 7 August 1825 detailing the systemic disintegration of court fidelity in Yogyakarta and the violent ignition of the western front in Bagelèn. On 4 August, over 200 insurgents mass at Krapyak, with intelligence indicating that Dipanagara, Panembahan Mangkubumi, and Adinegara plan a major assault on the capital on 6 August to coincide with the birthday of the exiled Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II), currently in transit from Ambon to Surabaya; Dipawidjaya is placed under house arrest. On 6 August, Sindunegara reports rebel assemblies north of the city, while Smissaert notifies Kedu that the Kraton has been hollowed out by desertion: most royal retainers (*abdi-dalem*) have fled, 30 religious scholars (*madjis*) of the palace **Suryagama** regiment have defected directly to Dipanagara, and virtually no royal prajurits remain. On 7 August, the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V and his immediate court are transferred inside Fort Vredeburg for sanctuary, where the terrified boy Sultan is traumatized by the European garrison. While 6 August passes with only light skirmishing, a massive peasant and elite revolt erupts in Bagelèn led by Tumenggung Gandasudirdja (Gandoeng-Saling)—a formidable warlord notorious for enlisting brigands—who brings the sub-districts of Moldo, Bringkelan, Loano, and Jana into open rebellion with the aim of severing Kedu. The Bagelèn insurrection is accompanied by brutal racial violence, featuring the systematic slaughter of Chinese women and children, while Major De Cavelairer's coffee plantation is burned to the ground.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara, Tumenggung Gandasudirdja (Gandoeng-Saling), Radèn Tumenggung Sindunegara, Dipawidjaya, Major De Cavelairer.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Krapyak, Surabaya, Ambon, Fort Vredeburg, Kedu, Bagelèn, Moldo, Bringkelan, Loano, Jana, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, Suryagama religious regiment (madjis), abdi-dalem flight, refuge of HB V in Fort Vredeburg, Bagelèn insurrection, slaughter of Chinese civilians, coffee plantation destruction, birthday of Sultan Sepuh.
