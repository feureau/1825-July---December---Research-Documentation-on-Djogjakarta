# Transcription of IMG_0096.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0096.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Jogja Letters* No. 54: *Siege Operations, Currency Collapse, and the Sura Offensive Plans, 1–3 August 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-01 to 1825-08-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles crisis dispatches from *Algemeen Verslag Jogja Letters* No. 54 dated 3 August 1825 documenting the tightening encirclement of Yogyakarta, high-level administrative defections, and severe financial collapse. All supply corridors into the capital are cut. Dipanagara's intelligence operatives sent into the Chinese camp are intercepted, revealing that the urban mosque (*masjid*) community is organized and armed with slings and stones, intensifying fears that both the European cantonment and Chinese settlement will be set ablaze. Insurgent forces shift from the Klaten road to concentrate at **Tegalsari**—the royal hunting lodge on the Pacitan road—preparing for an assault on Fort Vredeburg under Surakarta rebel Tumenggung Rajadipura. In a major shock to court loyalists, the Patih of Prince Paku Alam I, **Daéng Aria Nataredja**, abruptly abandons his post and flees back to his native village of Payaman in Kedu, while Tumenggung Judawijaya defects to the insurgents on 2 August. Writing to Surakarta, Resident Smissaert expresses relief that Lieutenant-General De Kock has arrived in Solo, while reporting the burning of peripheral villages on 1 August. In a verbal reply to De Kock's diplomatic letter, Dipanagara insolently dismisses Dutch threats regarding the safety of royal family members held in the fort, proclaiming plans for a massive general offensive during the sacred Islamic month of **Sura (Muharram)**. Meanwhile, the capital experiences monetary paralysis: a total dearth of copper small change causes silver guilders to plummet to an exchange rate of only 8 duits, leaving the colonial treasury bankrupt and compelling Smissaert to solicit an emergency private loan from Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Paku Alam I, Daéng Aria Nataredja, Radèn Tumenggung Rajadipura (Redja Dipura), Tumenggung Judawijaya, Radèn Tumenggung Radjaniti.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Selarong (Vilarong), Klaten, Tegalsari (Tegal Sari), Pacitan road, Payaman (Kedu), Fort Vredeburg, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Jogja Letters No. 54, Sura (Muharram) general offensive, defection of Pakualaman Patih, mosque slinger units (*bandring*), arson of Chinese/European camps, acute monetary deflation (f. 1 = 8 duits), emergency private loan from Paku Alam I, royal hunting lodge.
