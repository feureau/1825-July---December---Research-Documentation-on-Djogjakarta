# Transcription of IMG_0122.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0122.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 55)
- **Date Range:** 1826-08-29 to 1826-10-16
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatches, security circulars, and annual fiscal summaries of acting Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*) from late August to October 1826 preserved in ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 55. On 29 August 1826, Van Sevenhoven circulates a military directive to Dutch redoubt commanders across Mataram (Tempel, Pisangan, Bentulan, Kalasan, and Klaten) reporting an offensive sortie: over 250 men muster in Yogyakarta under Mas Tumenggung Reksapraja (*Mas Tum Reksa Pradja*), advancing under both the Sultan's royal flag and the Dutch tricolor in an effort to pacify rural districts; however, the rural peasantry (*desa people*) completely refuse cooperation, abandoning their villages to flee toward Pasar Gedé (Kota Gede), Imogiri, Banarang, Samping, Bantul Karang, and Trangkong. On 2 October 1826, Van Sevenhoven proposes a new municipal tax on residential houses to raise revenue. On 5 October 1826, he reports to Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock that Jaga Resa, the *bekel* of Desa Banyumeneng who actively collaborated with the flying column commanded by Major Sollécuijer, was forced to evacuate, after which rebel forces systematically burned and destroyed all his village property. Carey also notes a colonial insertion from Batavia requesting charitable contributions for European flood victims in East Friesland (*Oost-Friesland*). On 16 October 1826, Van Sevenhoven reports to the Commissioner-General on the lavish court celebration of Garebeg Mulud and ceremonial toasts honoring the restored 70-year-old Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), who displayed remarkable vitality throughout days of exhausting ceremonies; following the Sultan's formal installation, Government Commissioners Herman Warner Muntinghe and Nicolaus Engelhard prepare to depart Yogyakarta. Finally, the page transcribes the annual balance sheet of Yogyakarta's tax farms for 1826: arrack (f. 552), tollgates and market bazaars (f. 77,166), and opium (f. 36,000), totaling f. 113,718.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*), Mas Tumenggung Reksapraja (*Mas Tum Reksa Pradja*), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Major Sollécuijer, Jaga Resa (*bekel of Desa Banyumeneng*), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (*Sultan Sepuh*), Herman Warner Muntinghe, Nicolaus Engelhard.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Jogja*), Mataram, Tempel, Pisangan, Bentulan, Kalasan, Klaten, Pasar Gedé (Kota Gede), Imogiri, Banarang, Samping, Bantul Karang, Trangkong, Desa Banyumeneng, Batavia, East Friesland (*E. Friesland*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Benteng Network (*bentengs in Mataram*), Royal Sultanate Flag & Dutch Tricolor, Rural Defiance & Non-Cooperation (*desa people*), House Tax (*tax on houses*), Flying Columns (*flying column*), Bekel (*village headman*), Rebel Retaliation, East Friesland Floods, Garebeg Mulud, Restoration of Sultan Sepuh, Government Commissioners (*Muntinghe & Engelhard*), 1826 Tax Farm Revenues (arrack, tollgates, opium).
