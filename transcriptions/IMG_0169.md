# Transcription of IMG_0169.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0169.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Diary of Van der Capellen, July 1825 – Jan 1826*; *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], new series, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-26 to 1825-07-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official journal entries of Governor-General Van der Capellen detailing the critical military and diplomatic deterioration across Central Java between 26 and 28 July 1825. On 26 July, overland communications between Surakarta and Yogyakarta are severed; Acting Resident MacGillavry relies on trusted Javanese runners to obtain news from Yogya. Skirmishes extend to the outskirts of Klaten, where Fort Klaten and Fort Vredenburg face critical shortages of ammunition and provisions. In Semarang, Lieutenant-Colonel of Engineers Frans David Cochius mobilizes troops to convoy provisions to Yogyakarta and Klaten, but is forced to retreat to Solo while being attacked at Magelang by 800 Javanese insurgents. Captain Duperron mounts a defense of Magelang with 50 European soldiers and native pikemen provided by the Regents of Magelang and Menoreh. On 27 July, a massive insurgent force of 5,000 to 6,000 men launches a renewed assault on Magelang. As all communications from Semarang are severed, panic spreads that Kedu will fall and European power in Java will collapse completely. On 28 July, dispatches from Surakarta warn that Lieutenant-Governor-General De Kock must arrive immediately to control the wavering Surakarta Court: Sunan Pakubuwana VI vacillates openly and secretly communicates sympathy for Dipanagara and Mangkubumi, while only Patih Sasradiningrat II and Pangéran Buminata maintain unequivocal loyalty to the Dutch. In Yogyakarta, complete administrative breakdown reigns as the military district commandant breaks into open conflict with Resident Smissaert.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Lieutenant-Colonel Frans David Cochius, Captain Duperron, Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I (Regent of Magelang), Regent of Menoreh, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Patih of Surakarta), Panembahan Buminata, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Klaten (Fort Klaten), Fort Vredenburg, Semarang, Magelang, Menoreh, Kedu, Java.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Communications Interruption, Garrison Resupply, Military Engineering Detachment, Mass Insurgent Attacks (5,000–6,000 rebels), Royal Double-Dealing / Vacillation (*Sala Court*), Patih and Buminata Loyalty, Civil-Military Conflict (*Commandant vs. Resident*).
