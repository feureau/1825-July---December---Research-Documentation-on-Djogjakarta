## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0127.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Leiden University Library, B.P.L. 616, Portfolio 9, No. 2: *Expedition to Tegalrejo, 20 July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Eye-witness tactical field account preserved in the Nahuys van Burgst Archive (B.P.L. 616, Portf. 9, No. 2) detailing the colonial military expedition dispatched against Pangéran Dipanagara's estate at Tegalrejo (*Tegalredja*, located two leagues from Yogyakarta) on 20 July 1825. Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier mobilizes a mixed force; halting immediately before the estate boundary, he dispatches an emissary to reassure Dipanagara that no hostile action is intended and requesting that Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi (*MB*) come forward for immediate conference. The overture is violently rebuffed: the messenger is handled roughly, and the column is fired upon from the estate and pelted with slingstones (*bandring*). The colonial command decides to encircle the compound peacefully, stationing the majority of troops in the wet rice fields (*sawahs*) behind the house. Dipanagara and Mangkubumi are clearly spotted surrounded by pikemen bearing lances; Dipanagara is mounted on an Arab horse and dressed in white priestly attire (*"very distinguished priestly garb"*). An attempt by the Sultan's Bodyguard cavalry detachment under First Lieutenant Thierry and Captain Wiesemann to seize Dipanagara and Mangkubumi fails when surrounding villagers swarm and attack the troops, wounding several royal lifeguards with pikes. Field artillery is quickly brought up to fire upon the crowds, dispersing the villagers; the government detachment sustains no further casualties, while 15 to 20 defenders are killed on Dipanagara's side. At dusk, Lieutenant Thierry leads the troops in a tactical withdrawal back to Yogyakarta in an effort to secure the city and raise the sagging morale of the Kraton.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** P.F.H. Chevallier, Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*), Pangéran Mangkubumi (*MB*), First Lieutenant Thierry, Captain Wiesemann.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo (*Tegalredja*), Yogyakarta (*Djogja*), Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Military Expedition to Tegalrejo (20 July 1825), Mixed Force, Slings & Stones (*bandring*), Sawahs, Priestly Dress (*white garments, Arab horse*), Sultan's Bodyguard (Cavalry Detachment), Pike Assaults, Field Artillery, Casualties (15–20 dead), Tactical Withdrawal at Dusk, Morale of the Kraton.
