# Transcription of IMG_0106.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0106.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ARA 4132 [*Jan's Bijlage*], *Yogya Letters*, Smissaert to De Kock, 12 September 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-09-09 to 1825-09-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** De Kock notifies Colonel Achenbach on 9 September of his impending arrival in Yogyakarta on 18 September. In his 12 September dispatch, Smissaert describes the conditions in the besieged capital as exceedingly bleak: rampant starvation, disease, and total depletion of food provisions, noting that for three leagues surrounding Yogyakarta not a scrap of food can be procured. Female petty market traders selling *gula Jawa* (Javanese palm sugar) and sundry produce are systematically intercepted and robbed outside Dalem Pakualaman. Smissaert warns that so long as the rebel triumvirate of Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, and their allied princes maintain their headquarters at Selarong, there is no hope of peace. The rebellion spreads north into Demak; all communications with Kedu remain severed. A colonial intelligence spy from Kota Gede (*Kotha Gédhé*) reports spotting Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya fleeing there alongside other court bupatis intending to join Dipanagara at Selarong. The surrounding roads are extensively destroyed and studded with sharpened bamboo stakes (*ranjau*); near Reksanegaran, massive felled tree trunks form impassable road barricades. Smissaert explicitly identifies Dipanagara's strategic objective as starving Yogyakarta into total capitulation. Meanwhile, a high-level flight from the Kraton is uncovered: Mas Ajeng Jarawati (royal attendant in charge of rearing the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V) flees the palace alongside Radèn Mas Jadiman (a 14-year-old royal son of HB IV) and a detachment of soldiers.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Colonel Achenbach, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Paku Alam I, Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya, Mas Ajeng Jarawati, Radèn Mas Jadiman, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Selarong, Demak, Kedu, Kota Gede (Kotha Gédhé), Dalem Pakualaman, Reksanegaran, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Siege famine, *gula Jawa* (palm sugar), road barricades (felled trees), *ranjau* (sharpened bamboo caltrops), siege warfare, palace defections, child princes.
