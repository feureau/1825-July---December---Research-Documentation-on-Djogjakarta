# Transcription of IMG_0063.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0063.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825; H.E. Ankes / *Legioen van Mangkoe Nagoro*, 1935)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical intercepted intelligence dispatch dated 19 July 1825 sent by Acting Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry of Surakarta to Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert in Yogyakarta on the eve of the Tegalrejo assault; MacGillavry alerts Smissaert that the Demang of Desa Grojokan (*Grojogan* in the Surakarta enclave) had mobilized on Pangéran Dipanagara's express orders and departed towards Yogyakarta at the head of 100 armed men; Surakarta colonial spies returning from reconnaissance reveal an insurgent war plan to assault Pacitan on the southern coast before pivoting to attack the royal capital of Yogyakarta; intelligence gathered from the Demang's concubine confirms the troop march towards Yogyakarta to receive immediate tactical orders; citing H.E. Ankes' monograph *De levensgeschiedenis van Mangkunegoro VII en het Legioen van Mangkoe Nagoro* (1935, pp. 62–63), Carey transcribes MacGillavry's urgent warning: *"Amice — De Démang of Grojogan is op last van den Pangeran Dipanagora met 100 man van zijn gevolg naar Jogja vertrokken."*
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (Acting Resident of Surakarta), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Pangéran Dipanagara, Demang of Desa Grojokan (insurgent field commander), H.E. Ankes (historian).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Desa Grojokan (*Grojogan*), Pacitan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Inter-residency military warning, Insurgent troop mobilization (100-man levy), Planned assault on Pacitan, Informant intelligence (concubine testimony), Mangkunagaran Legion history (*Ankes pp. 62–63*).
