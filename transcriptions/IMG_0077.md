# Transcription of IMG_0077.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0077.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81 [Dj. Br. 81]; *Letters concerning Guardians 1822–1826* & *Letters concerning DN – 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-16 to 1825-07-18 (Retrospective administrative context: 1822-01-03)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official correspondence from the Yogyakarta Residency archives (ANRI Dj. Br. 81) preserving diplomatic warnings and administrative proceedings during July 1825; Section 1 documents loyalty oaths imposed upon Yogyakarta regents (*bupatis*) through Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, where Dutch authorities assure the court of Government benevolence and General De Kock's personal sacrifices to maintain order, followed by unanimous acclamations of *"Inggih"* [Yes] and decrees ordering palace officials to offer public prayers at the Grand Mosque for divine protection of the realm; describes an official audience with Queen Mother Ratu Ageng to commemorate Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV's accession birthday on 3 January (Year Alip AJ 1749); Section 2 details Acting Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry's urgent intelligence dispatch from Surakarta dated 16 July 1825, alerting Resident Smissaert that the Demang of Desa Grojogan (7–8 leagues from Solo) had received secret instructions from Pangéran Dipanagara to mobilize a major armed force at Mount Sepikul at the beginning of the month of Besar; reveals that Dipanagara had laid long-range insurrectionary plans by remitting land taxes to the Demang of Grojogan across 3 to 4 fasting months (*Puasas*) to enable the purchase of firearms and payment of road taxes; notes that the Bèkèl of Desa Babadan likewise joined the mobilization, with both headmen commanding followings of local outlaws and brigands (*wong durjana*); MacGillavry cautions that the ultimate designs of Dipanagara's rebel faction (*kraman party*) remain unclarified.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (Acting Resident of Surakarta), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Ratu Ageng, Demang of Desa Grojogan, Bèkèl of Desa Babadan.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Grand Mosque (*Mesjid Ageng*), Surakarta, Desa Grojogan, Mount Sepikul (*Sêpikul*), Desa Babadan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81 (Dj. Br. 81), Bupatis' loyalty oath (*oath for Yogya bupatis*), Long-term tax remission for armaments, Wong durjana (rural outlaws / bandit followers), Kraman party (rebel faction), Mount Sepikul mobilization, Javanese month of Besar.
