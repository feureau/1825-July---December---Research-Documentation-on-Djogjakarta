# Transcription of IMG_0093.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0093.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *Isolation of Yogyakarta, Adinegara's Proclamation, and Selarong War Decrees, 1–3 August 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-01 to 1825-08-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey documents the absolute encirclement of Yogyakarta, severe urban starvation, and the dramatic escalation of insurgent sovereignty in early August 1825. Following the catastrophic defeat of Second Lieutenant Boogaard's convoy from Kedu carrying 28,600 silver guilders, only a single shipment of 10,000 guilders from Surakarta penetrates the blockade, leaving the European garrison and court loyalists deprived of essential foodstuffs—rice, paddy, cooking oil, salt, and horse fodder. At Krapyak, south of the capital, rebel leader Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara adopts supreme dynastic pretensions, styling himself **Adipati Anom Surya Ingalaga Sénapati ing Ngalaga / Sénapati ing Prang** (Crown Prince and Commander-in-Chief in War) in preparation for a direct assault on Yogyakarta. On 2 August, Resident Smissaert issues frantic security alerts: Radèn Mangkuwidjaya and Radèn Mangkudirdja rally large irregular forces (*wong durjana*) at Desa Tajem; Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya holds Ketitang; Tumenggung Semarawidjaya occupies Pratak on the Klaten road; and Tumenggung Jayaningrat organizes armed resistance on the Kedu road. Paku Alam I warns that Desa Semaki, bordering his own princely dalem, serves as a rebel staging area. Major De Cavelairer's government plantation at Jenu (Salak) is sacked, and on 3 August, intelligence from Selarong reveals that Dipanagara and Panembahan Mangkubumi are granting formal administrative titles, investing commanders with ceremonial rank umbrellas (*payung*), and ordering the extermination of Chinese toll-farmers (*bandar*) alongside the destruction of colonial leaseholder property.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara (Adipati Anom Surya Ingalaga), Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Paku Alam I, Second Lieutenant Boogaard, Radèn Mangkuwidjaya, Radèn Mangkudirdja, Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya, Tumenggung Semarawidjaya, Tumenggung Jayaningrat, Major De Cavelairer.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Krapyak, Desa Tajem, Ketitang, Pratak, Desa Semaki, Jenu (Salak), Selarong (Vilarong), Klaten, Kedu, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, investiture of Adipati Anom Surya Ingalaga, state umbrellas (*payung*), slaughter of Chinese tax-farmers, acute provisioning blockade, rural guerrilla mobilization (*wong durjana*), Pakualaman defenses.
