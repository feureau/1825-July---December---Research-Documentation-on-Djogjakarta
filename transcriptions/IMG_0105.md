# Transcription of IMG_0105.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0105.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54, September 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-09-17 to 1825-09-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Renewed rebel assaults on the Kraton force the urgent evacuation of the boy Sultan Hamengkubuwana V and Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton) into the security of Fort Vredeburg. Surrounding village populations are compelled to join the insurgent ranks. An intelligence spy warns that Dalem Pakualaman is targeted for a four-pronged concentric attack. The palace gates of the Kraton are locked shut to prevent the infiltration of subversive elements. On 17 September, Smissaert issues a general proclamation to the population of Yogyakarta announcing the imminent arrival of Lieutenant-General De Kock on 18 September, commanding all senior Dutch and Javanese officials to assemble at Baturono to escort him into the city. Smissaert enacts strict anti-defection regulations prohibiting any Chinese resident from departing Yogyakarta without the explicit written authorization of the *Capitan Cina*; the families and personal property of any fleeing Chinese are ordered immediately confiscated and impounded. On 22 September, Smissaert reports receiving remittance of 1,529 guilders from bazaar and customs tollgate (*bandar*) leases in the eastern *mancanagara* regencies of Kertasana, Kalangbrèt, and Rawa. On 23 September, Smissaert circularizes the Residents of Semarang, Surakarta, and Kedu regarding four runaway estate laborers registered as enslaved persons (*slaves*)—Kambing (9-year-old girl), Rami (23-year-old woman), Dul (18-year-old youth), and Rade (27-year-old man)—who escaped from the private estate of Laumers toward Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Ratu Ageng (Ratu Kedhaton), Paku Alam I, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, *Capitan Cina* (Qué Wi Kong), Laumers (private estate owner), Kambing, Rami, Dul, Rade.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kraton Yogyakarta, Fort Vredeburg, Dalem Pakualaman, Baturono, Kertasana, Kalangbrèt, Rawa, Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sultan's evacuation, Kraton gate closure, general proclamation, Chinese travel restrictions, property impoundment, *Capitan Cina*, bazaar / tollgate leases (*bandar*), enslaved estate laborers (*slaves*).
