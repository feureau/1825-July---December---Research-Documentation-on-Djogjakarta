- **Date Range:** `1825-07-25` to `1825-07-29`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical strategic and intelligence assessments following the arrival of Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock at Semarang on 29 July 1825, with his advance to Surakarta scheduled for the next morning (30 July). Colonial intelligence reports that Dipanagara's rebellion is spreading rapidly westward into Kedhu and Bagelen. Pangéran Mangkubumi has moved into Bagelen to rally the local population and mobilize insurgent recruits, while Pangéran Adinagara is dispatched toward Roma (on the Bagelen–Banyumas border) to foment rebellion. Dispatches highlight the acute strategic vulnerability of the western *mancanagara*, emphasizing the utter lack of Dutch garrisons, military centers, or redoubts throughout Banyumas and Bagelen. Reports also note that rebel detachments (*brandals*) are coercing rural communities and burning or destroying villages that refuse to tender their submission to Dipanagara, while Resident MacGillavry reiterates his urgent plea for the immediate payment of government rents and arrears for the annexed Jabarangkah lands to prevent further popular defection.

  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Surakarta (Solo), Kedhu, Bagelen, Banyumas, Roma (Romakamal), Jabarangkah
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Commander-in-Chief, Algemeen Rijksarchief (ARA), Western Mancanagara, Jabarangkah Rent Payments, Village Coercion, Brandals (Rebels)
