# Transcription of IMG_0076.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0076.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters, July 1825 Synthesis / Verso Leaf)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-14 to 1825-07-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding notes from Mantri Wiraprana's intelligence report to Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*DR IV*); confirms that Demang Nitikrama of Sampar attempted to deliver territorial taxes to Pangéran Dipanagara at Tegalrejo, but Dipanagara returned the cash payment with explicit orders that the funds be used immediately to purchase unhusked rice (*padi*) for war provisions; records that on Thursday (14 July 1825), Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert summoned informants seeking intelligence on rural unrest, but Javanese officials refused to report directly to the Resident out of fear, insisting on awaiting the return of Patih Danureja IV to the capital; lower portion of the sheet comprises the blank ruled leaf.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Demang Nitikrama of Sampar, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Sampar, Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Tax remission for war logistics, Rice purchasing orders (*buy padi*), Intelligence protocol breakdown, Official fear of colonial reporting.
