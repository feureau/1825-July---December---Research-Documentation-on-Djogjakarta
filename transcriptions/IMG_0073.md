# Transcription of IMG_0073.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0073.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Kopie No. 12 cont. [Part 3])
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding section of Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert's evening report of 20 July 1825 to Governor-General Godert van der Capellen following the battle of Tegalrejo; Smissaert assures Batavia that the Kraton and the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V remain securely guarded and on the best terms with the colonial regime; diagnoses Dipanagara's rebellion as the product of "fatal fanaticism", rationalizing that not all insurgent followers are religious zealots and arguing that senior prince Pangéran Mangkubumi and Radèn Tumenggung Suryadipura were merely led astray by Dipanagara's charisma; tracks the flight of Dipanagara and Mangkubumi south towards Desa Secang (*Guwa Secang / Selarong*) near the Sultan's royal country retreat (*pesanggrahan*) at Toyatumumpang (*Banyutumumpang / Nusa Tenumpang*), 6 to 7 leagues from Yogyakarta; details the deployment of a loyalist Kraton punitive expedition consisting of 1,700 Javanese troops under royal regents Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya (noted by Carey for defeating Raden Rongga in 1810), Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara, and Radèn Tumenggung Ranadiningrat; requests urgent military reinforcements from Acting Resident MacGillavry in Surakarta, calling for the deployment of the elite Mangkunagaran Legion and regular troops of the 2nd Military Division under Major De Teer.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (Governor-General), Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Tumenggung Suryadipura, Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya, Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara, Radèn Tumenggung Ranadiningrat, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Major De Teer (Commander, 2nd Military Division), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III (historical context 1810).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kraton Yogyakarta, Desa Secang (Selarong), Pesanggrahan Toyatumumpang (*Nusa Tenumpang*), Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 Kopie No. 12 pt. 3), "Fatal Fanaticism", Loyalist Kraton counter-insurgency column (1,700 troops), Mangkunagaran Legion requisition, 2nd Military Division (*2de Militaire Afdeeling*).
