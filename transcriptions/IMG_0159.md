# Transcription of IMG_0159.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0159.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Kedu Letters, July–September 1825; Collectie De Kock No. 182, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-13 to 1825-08-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Secret intelligence and situation reports from Kedu Resident P. le Clercq to General De Kock concerning rebel movements in Western Mancanagara and Kedu in mid-August 1825. Insurgents mount destructive assaults on Ambar Ketawang (the administrative centre for Probolinggo / Prabalingga), burning down the residences and offices of Tax Inspector Janssens and local native officials because they refused to surrender or heed the rebel summons to arms. Despite these localized raids, Le Clercq emphasizes that there has been no widespread desertion among colonial-appointed Javanese bupatis and government officials in Kedu, who continue to show staunch cooperation. News received from Paku Alam I reports that Colonel von Jett is operating in southern Yogyakarta and that the strategic bridge over Kranggan has been destroyed by fire. On 15 August, rebels launch an attack north of Magelang and take prisoner the Demang of Jetis, who had remained loyal to the government, while the general populace of Kedu offers passive, non-violent resistance. The Regent of Menoreh takes the offensive against the rebels, deploying his forces in three coordinated tactical columns. Meanwhile, a secret duplicate dispatch of 13 August reveals that a colonial spy dispatched to Selarong failed to encounter Pangéran Dipanagara because the prince had already ridden south along the Pacitan road towards Gambar Palian (Girimulyo / Gambarpalian) to recruit fresh levies for an imminent general assault on Yogyakarta, strategically scheduled for 6 Sura (21 August 1825).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Janssens (Tax Inspector / Planter), Pangéran Natakusuma (Paku Alam I), Colonel von Jett, Demang of Jetis, Regent of Menoreh, Pangéran Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu, Ambar Ketawang, Probolinggo (Prabalingga), Magelang, Kranggan, Jetis, Menoreh, Selarong, Pacitan, Gambar Palian (Girimulyo / Gambarpalian), Yogyakarta, Western Mancanagara (*W. MN*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Western Mancanagara, Administrative Centre, Tax Inspector, Arson / Destruction of Government Posts, Pakualaman Troops, Bridge Destruction, Demang, Tactical Columns (3 columns), Colonial Espionage / Spies, Troop Recruitment, Sura Assault Schedule (6 Sura / 21 August 1825).
