# Transcription of IMG_0059.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0059.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Bijlage Smissaert No. 10; ANRI 'Archief van Jogja' [AvJ] 1825 / Intercepted Reports)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-14 to 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Two critical intelligence dispatches documenting the immediate outbreak of hostilities in Yogyakarta and the tactical movements of Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi; first, an eyewitness field report from Ngabèhi Mintadiwirya of Desa Rumusuh (Kamusuh) dated Wednesday, 3 Besar AJ 1752 (20 July 1825), tracking the flight of Dipanagara and Mangkubumi following the Dutch-court assault on Tegalrejo; the princes bypassed Mlangi via Desa Ngréjêk, reached Desa Gancahan (west of Tangkilan) at 7:00 PM to spend the night, and departed at 8:00 AM on 21 July for Desa Mutian (Putian / Kembang) in the Gamplong district near Selarong to recruit armed peasant followers, before moving across the Progo River (*Kali Praga*) to establish headquarters at Jèlègong under the protection of the chief of the royal tiger hunters (*Tuwa Buru*); second, an earlier intercepted intelligence dispatch dated 15 July 1825 (written between 14 and 20 July) from Ngabèhi Wiraprana to Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*DR IV*), reporting that Danureja IV had requested urgent intelligence from the countryside regarding Dipanagara; Wiraprana observes that Dipanagara's demeanor had become radically altered and erratic, and that spies infiltrating Tegalrejo found his followers preparing for war by binding up their long sarongs as combat loincloths and alerting royal tiger hunters to take up arms.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Ngabèhi Mintadiwirya (village headman / spy of Kumusuh), Ngabèhi Wiraprana (intelligence mantri), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Desa Rumusuh (Kamusuh), Desa Ngréjêk, Mlangi (*Mêlangi*), Desa Gancahan, Tangkilan, Desa Mutian (Putian / Kembang), Gamplong, Selarong, Jèlègong (*Téligong*), Kali Praga (Progo River).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bijlage Smissaert (ARA 4132), Procès-verbal / Field intelligence report, Flight from Tegalrejo, Tiger hunters (*Tuwa Buru / Mantri Head of Tiger Capturers*), War dress (binding sarongs as loincloths / *pinjung*), Military recruitment (*gathering people*).

  * **Tuwa Buru (Tiger Hunters / Capturers)**: Professional royal trackers and game wardens possessing firearms and deep jungle navigation skills, mobilized by Dipanagara as elite skirmishers.
  * **War Dress (Loincloths / Pinjung)**: Hitching traditional long sarongs above the knee to permit rapid overland running and jungle combat.
