# Transcription of IMG_0084.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0084.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: *Collectie Nahuys van Burgst* Port. 9 No. 9 cont.: *Djogja Correspondence, July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey transcribes the decisive final message exchanged between Pangéran Dipanagara and Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert on the afternoon of 20 July 1825, delivered through Kraton envoy Radèn Tumenggung Sindunegara just hours before colonial forces attacked Tegalrejo. In response to Smissaert's peremptory summons, Dipanagara formally refutes the Resident's accusations point-by-point: he denies orchestrating an armed muster at his estate, asserting that rural villagers converged on Tegalrejo spontaneously of their own free choice, and refutes charges of hoarding grain. Explaining his refusal to attend the Residency conference, Dipanagara openly admits his apprehension of treachery and arrest (*"cannot come to Resident because of fact that: because afraid"*). Nevertheless, he maintains diplomatic decorum, closing with the ironic assertion: *"Rest assured that I am a trusty servant of the Dutch government."* The lower half of the page constitutes a blank ruled leaf.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Radèn Tumenggung Sindunegara (Sindunagara).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo (Tegalredja), Yogyakarta (Djogja).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Leiden Univ. BPL 616 No. 9, formal ultimatum reply, voluntary rural assembly, fear of arrest (*bevreesd*), trusty servant formula (*getrouw dienaar*), blank verso leaf.
