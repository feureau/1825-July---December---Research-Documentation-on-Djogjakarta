## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0041.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Printed Reports (H.G. Nahuys van Burgst, *Verzameling van Officiële Rapporten betreffende den Oorlog op Java in de Jaren 1825–1830*, Deventer: M. Ballée, vols. I & II, 1835; vols. III & IV, 1836, pp. 10–11)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-03 (Context: 1822 to 1825-08-03; Published 1835)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Deposition (*verklaring*) of Radèn Mas Alip (estranged son of Pangéran Dipanagara) taken by Lt.-Gen. Hendrik Merkus de Kock on 3 August 1825; Nahuys van Burgst reviews claims of Dipanagara's premeditated hostility toward the colonial government, noting that during the British period and under Dutch Residents Nahuys and De Salis, Dipanagara's conduct was irreproachable and he was recommended as co-guardian (*medevoogd*) for young Sultan Hamengkubuwana V; Alip testifies that Dipanagara repeatedly urged the late Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV to expel the Europeans and take back alienated lands such as Kedu; Hamengkubuwana IV sharply rejected the proposal, praising Resident Nahuys as an upright friend; Dipanagara subsequently avoided visiting the Kraton unless accompanying the Resident.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Mas Alip, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Baron A.M.Th. de Salis, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djocjokarta), Kedu (Kadoe), Kraton Yogyakarta, Deventer.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Verklaring (procès-verbal / deposition), Medevoogd (co-guardian), Hooge Indische Regering, British Residents, Colonial land alienation.

### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Dipanagara, Prince (Pangéran Dipanagara)** (`DIPO NEGORO`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 10). Central leader of the Java War.
  * **Radèn Mas Alip** (`Radeen Maas ALEP`): Listed under *Sons of Dipanagara* (line 48). Eldest son of Dipanagara, declarant before General De Kock on 3 August 1825.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV** (`HAMANGKOBOANO den 4den`): Listed under line 14. Younger half-brother of Dipanagara; died suddenly December 1822.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana V**: Listed under line 15. Child Sultan placed under the joint guardianship of Dipanagara, Mangkubumi, Ratu Ageng, and Ratu Kencono.
  * **Nahuys van Burgst, Huibert Gerard**: Listed under *Dutch Officials* (line 125). Resident of Yogyakarta (1816–1822).
  * **De Salis, Baron A.M.Th.**: Listed under line 133. Resident of Yogyakarta (1822–1823) who proposed Dipanagara as co-guardian.
  * **De Kock, Hendrik Merkus**: Listed under line 124. Lieutenant-General and Commander-in-Chief of the Netherlands Indies Army.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Djogjokarta [Yogyakarta]**: Listed under line 427. Sultanate capital.
  * **Kedu [Kadoe]**: Listed under lines 435, 461. Rich agrarian province annexed by Raffles in 1812.
  * **Kraton**: Listed under lines 430, 482. Royal Palace of Yogyakarta.
* **Terms:**
  * **Medevoogd**: Co-guardian of the underage Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  * **Verklaring**: Sworn official deposition / procès-verbal.
