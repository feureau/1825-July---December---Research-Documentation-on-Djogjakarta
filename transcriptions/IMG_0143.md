## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0143.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Surakarta Residency Letters / War in Pacitan 1825)
- **Date Range:** `1825-08-11` to `1825-08-11`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official report from Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry to Governor-General Van der Capellen dated 11 August 1825, incorporating a dramatic firsthand dispatch from Wörmer (*Leerling in de Javaansche Taal* and Deputy Inspector / *opziener* of Pacitan) recounting the collapse of colonial authority across the southwestern coastal regency of Pacitan. Following the arrival of Ahmad Daris, an emissary sent by Pangéran Dipanagara, the colonial government-appointed Regent of Pacitan, Radèn Tumenggung Djasa Karia (known as Bupati Djimat), defects to Dipanagara, violating his solemn pledge of loyalty made to Inspector Wörmer. The colonial administration had attempted to mobilize the retainers and villages belonging to Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), who held extensive appanage estates across Pacitan. Ahmad Daris (invested by Dipanagara as Demang of Land Bureng) brings news that the neighboring district of Sembuyang has rallied to the rebellion. When Wörmer orders Demang Tirta Winangoon of Madas to take up arms and block Ahmad Daris, an insurgent force led by Jama Widjaya (son of the pensioned former Bupati of Pacitan, Setra Widjaya) launches an attack. Bupati Djasa Karia prevents Wörmer from leaving the regency, having already opened secret communications with Ahmad Daris. Only one village chief, Demang Ahmad Kaimat of Pacitan, remains loyal to the colonial government. Djasa Karia, himself an Islamic religious scholar (*priest*), openly reads the mobilization letter sent by Ahmad Daris.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Wörmer (Dutch Student of Javanese / Inspector of Pacitan), Pangéran Dipanagara, Ahmad Daris, Radèn Tumenggung Djasa Karia (Bupati Djimat), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, G.A.G.Ph. baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Jama Widjaya, Setra Widjaya (Pensioned Bupati of Pacitan), Tirta Winangoon (Demang of Madas), Demang Ahmad Kaimat
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan (Patjitan), Sembuyang, Land Bureng, Madas, Surakarta (Solo)
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Deputy Inspector of Pacitan (*Opziener*), Student of Javanese Language (*Leerling in de Javaans Taal*), Mangkunagaran Appanages in Pacitan, Rebel Emissary, Defection of Regents, Brandals (Rebels)
