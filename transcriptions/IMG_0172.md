# Transcription of IMG_0172.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0172.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-08-12 to 1825-08-20`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical situation updates across Central Java in mid-August 1825; Kedu reports that Menoreh has been utterly plundered by insurgents while the three-way strategic junction toward Semarang remains held; 500 colonial reinforcements from Surabaya expected at Semarang; Semarang Jayèng-Sekar irregular cavalry commended for excellent operations; Panembahan Buminata of Surakarta directly outlines the structural grievances fueling the rebellion, citing the sudden abolition of agricultural land rent contracts (*landverhuur*), unfulfilled colonial promises regarding the Panembahan title for himself and the Adipati title for Pangeran Purbaya, court impoverishment, and the despotic governance of former Resident De Vries; Van der Capellen observes with anxiety that the Surakarta court perceives colonial dependence on its arms and may leverage this leverage for high political demands; in Yogyakarta, the garrison faces mortal peril with most princes and the child Sultan's personal retinue defecting to Dipanagara, leaving all European and loyalist survivors barricaded inside Fort Vredenburg; Colonel Cochius and Lieutenant-Colonel Achenbach arrive with emergency relief supplies, boosting Yogyakarta's defenders to 1,300 men (principally Surakarta and Mangkunagaran forces); heavy insurgent assaults on the Kraton are repulsed by Paku Alam's troops.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Panembahan Buminata [influential Surakarta prince / uncle of Sunan PB VI], Pangéran Purbaya [Surakarta royal prince], Resident Pieter de Vriese (De Vries) [former Resident of Surakarta, 1823–1824], Colonel Frans David Cochius [military engineer / commander], Lieutenant-Colonel H.G. Achenbach [colonial relief force commander], Pangéran Paku Alam I [ruler of the Pakualaman], Sultan Hamengkubuwana V [child Sultan of Yogyakarta], Pangéran Dipanagara [leader of the rebellion].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu (Kedhu), Menorèh, Semarang, Surabaya, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Djokja), Fort Vredenburg (Rustenburg), Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Jayèng-Sekar [armed cavalry auxiliaries], Land Rent (*Landverhuur*) Abolition, *Panembahan* [exalted prince title], *Adipati* [high prince/viceroy rank], Fort Vredenburg, Kraton, Mangkunagaran Legion.
