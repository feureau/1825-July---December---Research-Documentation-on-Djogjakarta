- **Date Range:** `1825-07-28` to `1825-08-09`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's detailed archival research notes continuing the intelligence debriefing of Radèn Mas Suwangsa (Mangkunagaran Legion officer) from Collectie De Kock No. 183. Suwangsa documents intimate details of life, discipline, and military layout within Dipanagara's rebel encampment at Selarong during late July 1825. Rebel princes lodge informally in the village Demang's house, guarded by only 150 to 200 armed retainers. Mobile rural fighting units (*bandung*) arrive at Selarong daily to receive operational orders and disperse immediately back to regional frontlines. The princes typically sleep late until 9:00 or 10:00 AM, and several smoke opium. Selarong possesses no defensive entrenchments or earthworks except along the direct approach road from Yogyakarta, which is obstructed with sharpened bamboo stakes (*belang-belang* / *ranjau*). The camp holds no heavy artillery, fielding only around 30 muskets/rifles and large quantities of traditional pikes. Suwangsa reports that the primary catalyst driving the princes to abandon Yogyakarta was the intolerable insolence of Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, who treated the royal guardians of the young Sultan with open contempt rather than as princes of the blood, coupled with similar arrogance from Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier. Carey adds a critical marginal note referencing the *Kraton Kedhaton Babad* (Babad Dipanagara), noting Dipanagara's admission that the insurrection was originally planned to erupt during the sacred month of Sura (Muharram), but Danureja's aggressive staking of the road across Tegalrejo precipitated the premature rupture.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Suwangsa, Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, P.F.H. Chevallier, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Selarong, Yogyakarta (Yogya), Tegalrejo
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Demang's House, Mobile Guerilla Bands (*Bandung* Troops), Sharpened Bamboo Stakes (*Belang-belang* / *Ranjau*), Kraton Kedhaton Babad (Babad Dipanagara), Sacred Month of Sura (Muharram), Tegalrejo Road Stakes, Opium Smoking
