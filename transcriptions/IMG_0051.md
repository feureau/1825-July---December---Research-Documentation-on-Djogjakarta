# Transcription of IMG_0051.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0051.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, Collectie Smissaert, ARA 4132: *Smissaert Letters*)
- **Date Range:** 1823-01-04 to 1825-07-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's research notes extracting confidential dispatches from the Smissaert collection; records Resident Smissaert's dispatch to Governor-General Van der Capellen on 19 July 1825 reporting Pangéran Dipanagara collecting taxes to purchase rice and arms, while deriding Dipanagara's ascetic devotions as periodic insanity (*"tormented by insanity... gives himself up to the most servile devotion"*); records Dipanagara's final letter of 19 July 1825 denying deliberate troop mobilization and refusing to meet the Resident out of fear; reports the loss of 28,600 guilders belonging to the colonial government intercepted by rebels on the road between Magelang and Yogyakarta on 24 July 1825; transcribes Governor-General Van der Capellen's appointment letter to Smissaert (4 Jan 1823) urging aggressive colonial administrative enforcement during the young Sultan's minority; extracts a private letter to Commissioner J.I. van Sevenhoven (14 Dec 1824) questioning the proposed annexation of 19 Jabarangkah enclaves when Banyumas and Bagelen remain under the courts.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Governor-General G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Resident of Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Magelang, Kedu, Banyumas, Bagelen, Jabarangkah enclaves, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Vorstenlanden (VL), Babad Dipanagara (Letter A.62), Colonial treasury ambush, Minority administration.
