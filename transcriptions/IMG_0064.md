# Transcription of IMG_0064.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0064.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Confidential Dispatch to Governor-General)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-19 (Retrospective legal context: 1822-12-09)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Secret and private dispatch (*Kopie Particulier*) written by Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert from Yogyakarta to Governor-General Godert van der Capellen in Buitenzorg on 19 July 1825—the exact day prior to the armed attack on Tegalrejo; Smissaert reports that Pangéran Dipanagara, officially appointed co-guardian of child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V under the supposed Decree of 9 December 1822 No. 1 (which Peter Carey notes does not actually exist in the colonial statute registers), had surrendered himself completely to religious fanaticism (*dweepzucht*); reveals that Dipanagara had ordered his retainers to arm themselves, stock up large reserves of rice, and remitted village taxes so rural communities could purchase weaponry; admits that Dipanagara's ultimate objective remains a complete mystery not only to himself, but also to Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Rijksbestuurder*), senior co-guardian Pangéran Mangkubumi (*P. MB*), the Queen Mothers (*Ratus / Vorstinnen*), and Paku Alam I; confesses that he has not personally met Dipanagara to ascertain his grievances; attempts to downplay the crisis by pathologizing Dipanagara's conduct, diagnosing the prince as an erratic eccentric tormented by periodic bouts of madness (*gekweld door krankzinnigheid*), in which irrational states he was known to commit foolish acts and perform extreme, driven religious asceticisms (*dwaze stappen en doorgedrevene godsdienstige oefeningen*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (Governor-General), Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Rijksbestuurder*), Pangéran Mangkubumi, Paku Alam I, Queen Mothers (Ratu Ageng & Ratu Kencono), Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta Residency, Tegalrejo, Buitenzorg (Bogor).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Geheim Besluit / Secret Government Decree (9 Dec 1822 No. 1), Co-guardianship council, Religious fanaticism (*dweepzucht*), Tax remissions for arms purchases, Rice stockpiling, Pathologization of resistance (*gekweld door dwaze stappen / doorgedrevene godsdienstige oefeningen*).
