# Transcription of IMG_0082.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0082.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA [Nationaal Archief, The Hague] Collectie Smissaert No. 4132: *Debts to Ngabèhi Ratawidjaya*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-04-01 to 1825-10-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** A foundational fiscal document illuminating the systemic indebtedness of the Yogyakarta ruling elite on the eve of the Java War. While Ngabèhi Ratawidjaya borrowed heavily from European officials like Chevallier and Wiesemann (see `IMG_0081.md`), he simultaneously acted as a primary creditor and intermediary to the highest echelons of the Yogyakarta Kraton, holding appanage lands (*jungs*) and personal heirlooms as pawn collateral. The ledger records that Patih Danureja IV owed 2,300 Dutch guilders and Major Tumenggung Wiranagara owed 500 Spanish Dollars (both contracted during the fasting month of Puasa), with Wiranagara pledging appanage land where tax revenues were diverted directly to pay loan interest. Senior prince Pangéran Mangkubumi owed an initial 2,600 Sp. D. without collateral, followed by a secondary loan of 650 Sp. D., and ultimately pawned Desa Jolmaki, 12 Javanese manuscript books, and two diamond rings (valued at 900 Sp. D.), bringing his total debt to 4,150 Sp. D. Regional bupatis similarly mortgaged their territorial bases: Radèn Tumenggung Nitinegara owed 1,225 Sp. D. and pawned 5 jungs of land for 600 Sp. D.; Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma II owed 1,000 Sp. D. against 5 jungs of land; Radèn Tumenggung Wandakusuma pledged 2 jungs against 300 Sp. D.; and Radèn Tumenggung Mangkudirja pawned 1 jung against 260 Sp. D. Additional debts were held by Radèn Panji Suréngrana (450 Sp. D. secured by land), Radèn Tumenggung Prawirakusuma (100 Sp. D.), Darmawijaya (120 Sp. D.), and Radèn Juda (100 Sp. D.), bringing the general total of outstanding debts owed to Ratawidjaya to 9,550 Spanish Dollars.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Ngabèhi Ratawidjaya, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Major Tumenggung Wiranagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Tumenggung Nitinegara, Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma II, Radèn Tumenggung Wandakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Mangkudirja, Radèn Panji Suréngrana, Radèn Tumenggung Prawirakusuma, Darmawijaya, Radèn Juda.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Desa Jolmaki.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ARA Collectie Smissaert No. 4132, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Dutch guilders, Jung (appanage land unit), Puasa (fasting month debts), land pawn mortgages, Javanese manuscript books (12 volumes pledged), diamond rings.
