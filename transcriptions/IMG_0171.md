# Transcription of IMG_0171.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0171.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-08-01 to 1825-08-09`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Governor-General Van der Capellen reviews initial August developments of the Java War; praises Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry's handling of Surakarta under critical conditions and resolves to promote him to full Resident to invest him with greater executive authority; stabilizes the Banten military command by replacing an untrustworthy officer newly arrived from Europe with his own trusted military adjutant, Major Ferrié; monitors the exiled Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II) in Surabaya (dismissed as a fanatic); analyzes Pangeran Mangkubumi's psychology, noting his European reputation as intelligent yet unenterprising and attributing his defection to Resident Smissaert's provocative demand holding him personally accountable for Dipanagara's submission; orders De Kock to conduct a thorough investigation into the deep causes of the rebellion; De Kock reports firm Surakarta loyalty, disciplined fallback of royal tumenggungs to Klaten, and planned advances toward the south coast via Pasar Gede; dispatches from Yogyakarta deteriorate as Mangkunagaran troops under Colonel Cochius prepare an advance while Mangkubumi mobilizes irregular *franc-tireurs* guerrilla forces.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry [acting 2nd Resident / Resident of Surakarta], Major Ferrié [military adjutant to Van der Capellen / military commander of Banten], Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh) [exiled Sultan of Yogyakarta at Surabaya], Pangéran Mangkubumi [uncle of Dipanagara / co-leader of the uprising], Pangéran Dipanagara [leader of the Java War], Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock [Commander-in-Chief / Commissioner for the Principalities], Colonel Frans David Cochius [military commander / artillery officer], Anthony Hendrik Smissaert [Resident of Yogyakarta].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Banten, Surabaya, Klathèn, Pasar Gede (Kotagede), Yogyakarta (Djokja), South Coast of Java.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Resident / Second Resident, Military Adjutant, Ex-Sultan, Guarantor / Fellow Guardian (*curator*), *Franc-tireurs* [irregular guerrilla light infantry], Tumenggung, Mangkunagaran Legion.
