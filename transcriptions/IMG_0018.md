### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier** (`P.F.H. Chevallier`): Assistant Resident of Yogyakarta.
  * **Pangéran Abubakar (Dipawiyana I)** (`PRINCE ABU BAKAR`): Son of HB I, head of court *priyayi pradikan* (religious tax-exempt nobility), who defected to Diponegoro on 6 August 1825 and was killed in 1826 (line 88).
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Reksadiningrat** (`Rekso Deningrat`): Senior Yogya court official wounded in combat against insurgent forces.
  * **Pangéran Dipanagara [Diponegoro]** (`DP`): Listed under line 10.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Kraton**: The royal palace complex of Yogyakarta.
  * **Mosque [Mesjid Ageng / Mesjid Gedhe Kauman]**: The grand sultanate mosque facing the northern alun-alun.
  * **Djogja [Yogyakarta]**: Sultanate capital.
