# Transcription of IMG_0095.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0095.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Collectie De Kock* / *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *Smissaert to General De Kock on Royal Impotence and the Capture of Kota Gede, 30 July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey transcribes the continuation of Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert's dispatch to Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock on 30 July 1825, underscoring the absolute paralysis of the colonial and royal administrative apparatus in Yogyakarta. While Dipanagara and Panembahan Mangkubumi direct operations from Selarong and Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara mobilizes rural levies around the perimeter of the capital, Smissaert admits that neither the court bupatis nor Patih Danureja IV command any residual authority over the Javanese population (*"anything could happen"*). All remaining princes of suspect loyalty in the capital are placed under physical arrest inside Fort Vredeburg. In a telling confession that earned De Kock's scathing marginal note—*"sic!"*—Smissaert acknowledges his utter blindness: *"The reasons for the present uprising I still do not fully understand,"* lamenting that he had never been so thoroughly deceived by mankind. Rumors that the colonial government contemplates returning the exiled Old Sultan (Hamengkubuwana II / Sultan Sepuh) from Ambon to Surabaya inject further revolutionary momentum into the populace. Crucially, Dipanagara's troops, supported by skilled slingers (*bandring* units), capture both the Surakarta and Yogyakarta demangs of **Kota Gede** (*Pasar Gèdhé*), transferring the sacred ancestral tombs and dynastic cradle of Mataram into insurgent hands. The lower half of the page is a ruled blank leaf.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Selarong (Vilarong), Fort Vredeburg, Kota Gede (Pasar Gèdhé), Surabaya, Ambon, Mataram.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ARA Collectie De Kock, De Kock's ironic marginalium (*sic!*), total administrative collapse, slinger skirmishers (*bandring*), capture of Kota Gede demangs, return of Sultan Sepuh, Fort Vredeburg detention.
