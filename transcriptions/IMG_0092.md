## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0092.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *State Prisoners, Fort Vredeburg, and the Outbreak of War, 25 July – 2 August 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-25 to 1825-08-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles vital operational dispatches from *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54 recording the near-total isolation of Yogyakarta and the desperate preventive incarceration of suspected Kraton princes. Between Klaten and Yogyakarta, virtually every village has declared for Dipanagara. On 25 July, Pangéran Surya (son of Mangkunagara II) is overwhelmed and ambushed by insurgents at Kalasan, with initial reports fearing him dead (an engagement confirmed in the *Babad Purwaredja*, canto XII stanza 26). In response, Colonel Frans David Cochius is ordered to organize a mobile shock column to keep the Surakarta highway open. On 30 July, Resident Smissaert issues emergency orders to the commandant of Fort Vredeburg to seize 12 royal princes and noblemen as "State prisoners" to forestall their flight to Selarong: Pangéran Mangkukusuma, Pangéran Adikusuma, Pangéran Panular, Pangéran Suryabranta, Pangéran Selarong, Pangéran Suryawijaya, Purwakusuma, Tepasanta, Adiwijaya, Mertasana, Ariakusuma, and Natabardja. Significantly imprisoned alongside them is **Bengali Abdul Kadir**, an Indian blacksmith (*tukang besi*) from Wanabasa and prominent figure from the Bengali Sepoy community settled in the Principalities. On 1 August, Smissaert reports to Kedu that all roads are unusable following a direct rebel attack on the *Witte Paal* (Tugu monument) just one league (5 km) north of the Kraton, and on 2 August 1825, he issues an all-Java circular letter warning all regional Residents that Dipanagara and Mangkubumi have been in open armed rebellion since 18 July.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Surya, Mangkunagara II, Colonel Frans David Cochius, Pangéran Mangkukusuma, Pangéran Adikusuma, Pangéran Panular, Pangéran Suryabranta, Pangéran Selarong, Pangéran Suryawijaya, Bengali Abdul Kadir.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Klaten, Kalasan, Wanabasa (Wana Basa), Witte Paal (Tugu), Fort Vredeburg, Surakarta (Solo), Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, Babad Purwaredja (canto XII), State prisoners (Fort Vredeburg), Bengali community (Sepoy blacksmiths / tukang besi), mobile column (*vliegende colonne*), all-Java circular letter (*circulaire*), Witte Paal attack.
