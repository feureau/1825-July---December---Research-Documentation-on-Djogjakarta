- **Date Range:** 1825-09-27 to 1825-10-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey compiles official residency dispatches documenting the command transition in Yogyakarta and the intense military operations around Selarong in September and October 1825. On 27 September 1825, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry assumes direction of the Yogyakarta Residency following Smissaert's dismissal. On 30 September, reports detail an attack on a colonial convoy between Banturan and Prambanan; despite being ambushed, the unit (comprising a European sergeant, two hussars, and Madurese policemen) cuts its way through to Klaten. Simultaneously, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock attempts written overtures to Dipanagara at Selarong; receiving no compliant answer, De Kock mounts an assault on Selarong on the morning of 30 September aiming to secure food supplies and stabilize the environs of Yogyakarta. On 24 October, De Kock launches another major offensive against Selarong to disrupt Dipanagara's scheduled celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (*Garebeg Mulud*); colonial forces seize the positions at 11:20 (accompanied by an earthquake), but Dipanagara successfully withdraws. On 26 October, De Kock reports pursuing the insurgents southwest to Desa Banting, where Dipanagara's elite priestly regiments (*Suranatan* / religious troops) put up a tenacious and disciplined rearguard defense (*"tenaciously and strongly en field"*), enabling Dipanagara and Mangkubumi to make a clean crossing of the Progo River (*Kali Praga*), after which all contact is lost. Meanwhile, Commissioner Jan Isaäk van Sevenhoven reports catastrophic urban mortality in Yogyakarta and Surakarta: hundreds die daily in the streets, officially blamed on cholera but realistically caused by acute famine, necessitating urgent appeals for soldiers, money, and provisions.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Jan Isaäk van Sevenhoven, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Banturan, Prambanan, Klaten, Selarong (Vilarong), Desa Banting, Progo River (Praga), Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Residency of Yogyakarta, Garebeg Mulud festival, Priestly Regiments (Suranatan), Progo River crossing, earthquake of 24 October 1825, urban famine and cholera epidemic, Madurese police, hussars.
