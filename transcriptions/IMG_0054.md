# Transcription of IMG_0054.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0054.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698 b; ANRI 'Archief van Jogja' [AvJ] 1825 / *Translaat Brieven: Beginning of the War*)
- **Date Range:** 1824-12-16 to 1825-07
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatch regarding the request to repatriate the body of the deceased exiled royal Prince Pangéran Mangkudiningrat from Surabaya to Yogyakarta, requiring permission from the exiled Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sepuh); Kraton security crisis on 25 February 1825 when Ratu Kencono (widow of HB IV) wounded another woman in the palace with a keris; subsequent flight of Ratu Ageng and the infant Sultan Hamengkubuwana V to the residence of her brother Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat, taking with them the sacred Majapahit dynastic heirloom keris *Kyai Pendawa Gajah*; appointment of Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier to the boundary commission for Jabarangkah (Besluit 16 Dec 1824 No. 1); forensic accounting records from translated intercepted court letters (*Translaat Brieven*) detailing usurious loans and land pawning in July 1825, where Yogyakarta nobles (Radèn Tumenggung Nitinegara, Danukusuma, Wangsakusuma, Mangundirja, Suryengrana, and Prawirakusuma) mortgaged their appanage lands (*jonks*) for cash in Spanish silver round reals (*Rond Realen*) borrowed from Patih Danureja IV and Ngabehi Ratawijaya.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Mangkudiningrat, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sepuh), Ratu Kencono, Ratu Ageng, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat, Dr. Lucien Adam, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Ngabehi Ratawijaya, Radèn Tumenggung Nitinegara, Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Wangsakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Mangundirja, Radèn Panji Suryengrana, Radèn Tumenggung Prawirakusuma.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogya), Surabaya, Kraton Yogyakarta, Jabarangkah enclaves, Majapahit.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Archief van Jogja (AvJ 1825), KITLV H 698 b, Translaat Brieven, Kris Kyai Pendawa Gajah (Majapahit heirloom), Garebeg Puasa (18 May 1825), Jonk (land measure), Rond Realen (Spanish silver dollars), Pawn / Gadai (mortgaging appanage lands).

### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Pangéran Mangkudiningrat**: Son of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; exiled by the British to Penang and Ambon; died in exile.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II`): Listed under line 12. Exiled ruler residing in Surabaya in early 1825.
  * **Ratu Kencono (`Ratu Kencana`)**: Listed under line 32. Official wife of HB IV and mother of HB V; central figure in palace keris incident.
  * **Ratu Ageng Tegalreja**: Listed under line 29. Widow of HB I; fled palace with infant Sultan and Majapahit pusaka.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana V**: Listed under line 15. Child Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1822–1826).
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat**: Son of Danureja II and brother of Ratu Ageng; sheltered royal family.
  * **Chevallier, Petrus Franciscus Henricus**: Listed under line 134. Member of Jabarangkah boundary commission.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja IV** (`DR IV`): Listed under *Patihs of Yogyakarta* (line 99). Grand Vizier and principal creditor/usurer in land-mortgage transactions.
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Nitinegara, Danukusuma, Wangsakusuma, Mangundirja, Suryengrana, Prawirakusuma**: Senior bupatis and court dignitaries forced into debt; all subsequently joined Dipanagara in July 1825.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Jogya [Yogyakarta]**: Listed under line 427.
  * **Surabaya**: Listed under lines 437, 471. Site of royal exile.
  * **Kraton**: Listed under line 430. Royal Palace of Yogyakarta.
  * **Djabarangkah**: Listed under line 433. Disputed enclave territories.
  * **Majapahit**: Classical empire; source of dynastic legitimacy.
* **Terms:**
  * **AvJ 1825**: Archief van Jogja (Residency Archives of Yogyakarta).
  * **Translaat Brieven**: Seized Javanese court letters translated into Dutch.
  * **Kris Kyai Pendawa Gajah**: Sacred heirloom weapon (*pusaka*) dating from the Majapahit era.
  * **Jonk**: Javanese unit of land measurement and revenue appraisal (approx. 4 bau or 2.8 hectares).
  * **Rond Realen (R.R.)**: Spanish silver pillar dollars (reales de a ocho) circulating in Java.
  * **Garebeg Puasa**: Major Islamic royal court festival marking the end of Ramadan (held 18 May 1825).
