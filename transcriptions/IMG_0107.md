# Transcription of IMG_0107.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0107.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54 & ARA 4132, 26 September 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-03 to 1825-09-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Smissaert reports on predatory financial activities by European and Chinese moneylenders in Surakarta and Yogyakarta who amassed considerable fortunes during the panic of the early war months through pawned articles (*pand*-taking); Smissaert insists on regulations requiring pawned collateral to be returned upon liquidation of interest rather than being permanently alienated into private European or Chinese estates. The administration addresses the strategic management of the opium monopoly (*candhu / madat*): opium dens were initially shut down to prevent insurgent plundering and deny drugs to auxiliary corps (Paku Alam, Kraton bodyguard, and Mangkunagaran Legion), but because troops suffered severe withdrawal and sickness without opium, Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier is ordered to release 2 catties of opium directly to loyal troops. An emergency municipal loan ledger documents forced/voluntary financial advances to the colonial treasury to keep the residency afloat: Paku Alam (6,600 guilders), Chinese Been (308 guilders), Capitan Cina Qué Wi Kong (1,750 guilders), Med. Dirck (660 guilders), Mevrouw E.C. Sahtrie (330 guilders), Nyai Israël (300 guilders), and Mevrouw Brouwerius van Nideck (2,100 guilders). On 26 September 1825 (Disptach No. 213), Lieutenant-General De Kock officially suspends Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert from office (a measure De Kock intended to enact upon reaching Solo on 3 August, deferred only because Yogyakarta was under siege); Smissaert is ordered to proceed immediately to Semarang to face a formal commission of inquiry by the Governor-General, with Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry installed as acting Resident of Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, P.F.H. Chevallier, Paku Alam I, Qué Wi Kong (Capitan Cina), Chinese Been, Med. Dirck, Mevrouw E.C. Sahtrie, Nyai Israël, Mevrouw Brouwerius van Nideck, Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Pawnshops / pawned collateral (*pand*), opium monopoly (*candhu*), opium distribution to auxiliary troops, emergency municipal loans, official suspension of Resident Smissaert (De Kock Dispatch No. 213), Commission of Inquiry.
