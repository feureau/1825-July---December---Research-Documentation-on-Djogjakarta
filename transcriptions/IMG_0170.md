# Transcription of IMG_0170.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0170.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Diary of Van der Capellen, July 1825 – Jan 1826*; *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], new series, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-29 to 1825-08-01
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official journal entries of Governor-General Van der Capellen recording strategic decisions, troop redeployments, and administrative overhauls between 29 July and 1 August 1825. Van der Capellen notes with deep regret that Chinese tax-farmers (*bandar*) and their households, from whom the agrarian populace had suffered severe economic exactions, become the primary targets of vengeful rebel massacres, concluding that the sorrowful state of affairs demands immediate and formidable countermeasures. Naval orders are dispatched redirecting military transports returning from Borneo to sail directly to Semarang. Lieutenant-Governor-General De Kock arrives at Semarang on 29 July and Surakarta on 30 July, immediately convening a general conference and formally proclaiming supreme government authority across the Vorstenlanden. In Surakarta, certain tumenggungs assigned to oppose the rebels march out carrying the Sunan's royal payungs. In Kedu, Resident Le Clercq and Captain Duperron successfully check the rebel advance east along the Elo River, bolstered by a relief detachment from Semarang of 50 soldiers and two cannon, while further reinforcements are mobilized from Surabaya. Carey appends biographical data on Colonel Frederick von Jett (born Ludwigsburg 1777; served under Daendels; Yogyakarta 1810; commandant of the 2nd Military District 1816; dismissed 1827). Upon arriving in Yogyakarta, De Kock executes his paramount initial administrative duty: the immediate and summary dismissal of Resident A.H. Smissaert, whose ill-advised policies, bungled negotiations, and haughty, undiplomatic dealings with the Javanese nobility are condemned as the principal cause of the rebellion.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Sunan Pakubuwana VI, P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Captain Duperron, Colonel Frederick von Jett, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Herman Willem Daendels, P.J.F. Louw, Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Kedu, Kali Elo (Elo River), Surabaya, Borneo, Banten (Bantam), Ludwigsburg.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Anti-Chinese Massacres, Chinese Tax-Farmers (*bandar*), Naval Transports, Supreme Government Authority (*Vorstenlanden Proclamation*), *Payung* (royal parasols), Elo River Defense, 2nd Military District Command, Summary Dismissal of Resident Smissaert, Undiplomatic Court Conduct.
