# Transcription of IMG_0109.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0109.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54 [Oct–Dec 1825], MacGillavry to De Kock, 15 October 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-10-09 to 1825-10-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** MacGillavry transmits a detailed intelligence report on the military uprising in Pacitan (*Padjitan*). At the outbreak of hostilities, the population of Pacitan and Sambeng was mobilized into a defensive cordon along the Van Boeijen frontier under European Inspector Wormser and R.J. Deux (a volunteer officer from Solo), cooperating with the pensioned Bupati Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya, while the former Bupati Jayanegara remained at Pacitan. On 9 October, a band of 25 armed insurgents raids Pacitan town, setting fire to the government coffee and pepper warehouses, burning down Inspector Wormser's house and residency office, reportedly murdering former Bupati Jayanegara and his retainers, and driving out the inhabitants with a large part of Pacitan destroyed. On 10 October, R.J. Deux, reinforced by Jagakarya and Wormser at Pinangsonang, counterattacks the insurgents, scattering them and capturing a mortally wounded insurgent chief in a pepper garden—identified as Wira Rana (Wirontani), the former *bekel* of Gedangan. Prior to dying, Wira Rana confesses he was commissioned as Bupati of Pacitan by Dipanagara's son Radèn Dipa Atmaja (Pangéran Dipakusuma) to seize the districts of Ngili, Nglurah, and Panggul—a claim corroborated by the Surakarta-appointed Bupati of Ngili, Kerta Diwirya. However, the Resident dismisses Wira Rana as an opportunistic adventurer exploiting Dipanagara's name, confirming that Radèn Dipa Atmaja remained strictly loyal inside Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Inspector Wormser, R.J. Deux (Solo volunteer), Radèn Tumenggung Jagakarya (pensioned bupati of Pacitan), Radèn Tumenggung Jayanegara (former bupati), Wira Rana / Wirontani (former bekel of Gedangan), Radèn Dipa Atmaja (Pangéran Dipakusuma, son of DP), Kerta Diwirya (Solo bupati of Ngili).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pacitan (Padjitan), Sambeng, Van Boeijen frontier, Pinangsonang, Gedangan, Ngili, Nglurah, Panggul, Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Coffee and pepper warehouses (*pakhuizen*), arson, *bekel*, bupati appointment, opportunist revolt, loyalty of royal princes.
