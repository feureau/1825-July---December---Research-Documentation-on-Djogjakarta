# Transcription of IMG_0158.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0158.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Kedu Letters, Appendix I; Collectie De Kock, Nationaal Archief, The Hague).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Contemporary Malay translation and verbatim English transcription of an intercepted royal summons (*surat undang*) issued jointly by Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi to the chiefs, headmen, and inhabitants of Kedu, intercepted from courier Madan Besari (Hasan Besari) and his six companions upon their capture by the Resident of Kedu. The proclamation addresses all friends and subjects throughout Kedu—men and women, eminent and humble—commanding that upon receiving this summons, they must immediately prepare their weapons to rise in holy rebellion (*ribut negari*), purify and re-establish the Islamic faith (*Agama Rasul* / *Agama Islam*), and unite in common resistance. The letter enforces absolute obedience through draconian sanctions: any individual who refuses or lacks the courage to believe and act upon this decree is to be summarily executed by decapitation (*"mesti gua potong dia punya léhér"* / their heads must be severed).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara (*Kandjeng Gusti Pangéran Dipa Negara*), Pangéran Mangkubumi, Madan Besari (Hasan Besari, royal courier), P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu (*Negeri Kedu*), Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Royal Summons (*Surat Undang*), State Rebellion (*Ribut Negari*), *Agama Rasul* (Religion of the Prophet / Holy War), Malay Intercepted Translation, Capital Punishment / Decapitation Mandate (*potong leher*), Courier Interception.
