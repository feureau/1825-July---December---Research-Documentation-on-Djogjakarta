## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0089.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI / ARA *Algemeen Verslag Djogja Letters* No. 54: *Outbreak of the Java War, 25–26 July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-25 to 1825-07-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey details the rapid tactical encirclement of Yogyakarta and the catastrophic defection of the Kraton princes between 25 and 26 July 1825. Resident Smissaert resorts to dispatching duplicate letters via native runners using unfrequented mountain paths to evade insurgent patrols. He urgently requests 50 hussars and two artillery guns from the Mangkunagaran Legion to secure arterial roads, with detachments of 50 cavalry stationed at Klaten and Kartasura. Rebel scouts and advance units are sighted directly at the *Witte Paal* (the White Pillar / Tugu monument) in northern Yogyakarta. Surakarta royal enclaves around Gondang rise under Demang Wangsadikrama and his son Nitikrama, while Dipanagara's personal appanage village of Sambilegi is fortified into a major rebel bastion, with roadways trenched and studded with sharpened bamboo stakes (*ranjau*). On 26 July, Smissaert notifies Klaten that logistics from Yogyakarta are severed, mandating supply requisitions from Kartasura. Skirmishes erupt within musket range of Fort Vredeburg and the Kraton walls, killing an escort hussar and shooting dead a lieutenant's mount, while Sunan loyalist troops suffer a severe defeat at Sambilegi. Simultaneously, a mass midnight exodus of princes abandons the Kraton to join Dipanagara at Selarong, led by Radèn Adisurya (brother of Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara) and joined by Pangéran Panular, Pangéran Suryabrangta, Pangéran Adikusuma, Pangéran Mangkukusuma, Pangéran Suryawijaya, Tumenggung Mertasana, Adiwijaya, Prawiradiningrat, and Dipanagara's eldest son Radèn Dipa Atmaja (Dipanagara II).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adisurya, Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara, Pangéran Panular, Pangéran Suryabrangta, Pangéran Adikusuma, Pangéran Mangkukusuma, Pangéran Suryawijaya, Tumenggung Mertasana, Radèn Dipa Atmaja (Dipanagara II), Demang Wangsadikrama, Nitikrama.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Witte Paal (Tugu), Sambilegi (Sambi Legi), Gondang (Rondang), Klaten (Klatten), Kartasura, Fort Vredeburg, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** AV Djogja Letters No. 54, Witte Paal (Tugu landmark), bamboo caltrops (*ranjau*), Mangkunagaran Legion hussars, princely defection wave, duplicate dispatches, dalem escapes.
