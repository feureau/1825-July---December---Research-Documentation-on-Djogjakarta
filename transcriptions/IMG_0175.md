# Transcription of IMG_0175.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0175.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-09-28 to 1825-10-25`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Escalation of the northern campaign and dramatic relief of Yogyakarta in October 1825; Pangéran Sèrang and Radèn Ayu Sèrang win a major tactical victory over Captain Buschkens' colonial detachment (comprising Javanese Jayèng-Sekar cavalry and armed naval sailors from the frigate *Javaan*), inflicting heavy casualties; acute alarm in Semarang over an impending rebel assault on Demak; Major Dollewijn arrives with 500 troops to reinforce Semarang; Major-General Joseph van Geen organizes three mobile offensive columns under Lieutenant-Colonel Smitzinzer [Smitsinger], Major Ernout, and Major Dollewijn; Column 1 under Smitzinzer is ambushed outside Demak and falls back southward, where Smitzinzer succumbs to cholera; Van Geen launches a direct assault on Demak and sends clearing patrols toward Purwodadi; Pangéran Sèrang's family residence is put to the torch by Assistant Resident Van Rheede van Oudtshoorn; political crisis erupts within the Semarang elite involving the sons of the late Kyai Adipati Sura-adimanggala IV: the younger son Raden Sukur defects to the rebellion with the foreknowledge of his eldest brother Raden Salèh [Raden Panji Suryawinata], leading to the summary arrest and imprisonment of the family, causing widespread unrest among the Javanese population; in Kedu, pacification columns commanded by Lieutenant-Colonel Cleerens in the north and Major De Bast in the south, coordinated with Kendal Assistant Resident Holmberg de Beckfelt, clear rebel forces; Lieutenant-General De Kock assembles an 8,000-man relief army advancing in three columns under General Van Geen to break the siege of Yogyakarta; relief convoys and supplies reach Yogyakarta in the nick of time, ending catastrophic wartime starvation where rice had spiked to 500 guilders per picul.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Pangéran Sèrang, Radèn Ayu Sèrang, Captain Buschkens, Major Dollewijn, Major-General Joseph van Geen, Lieutenant-Colonel Smitzinzer [Smitsinger], Major Ernout, J.F. van Rheede van Oudtshoorn [Assistant Resident of Salatiga], Kyai Adipati Sura-adimanggala IV [late enlightened Bupati of Semarang], Raden Sukur [younger son of Adimanggala], Raden Salèh [Raden Panji Suryawinata / eldest son], Lieutenant-Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens, Major De Bast, H.G. Baron Nahuys van Burgst / Holmberg de Beckfelt [Assistant Resident of Kendal], Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Demak, Semarang, Purwodadi, Grobogan, Kedu (Kedhu), Kendal, Yogyakarta (Djokja).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Frigate *Javaan*, Mobile Columns (*Mobiele Colonnes*), Cholera Epidemic, Jayèng-Sekar [armed cavalry], Semarang Regency Elite, Famine Price (500 guilders / picul of rice), Relief Column.
