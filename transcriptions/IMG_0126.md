# Transcription of IMG_0126.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0126.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nahuys van Burgst Archive, Leiden University Library, B.P.L. 616, Portfolio 9, No. 2: *Solo - Djogja Correspondence, July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-16 to 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical diplomatic and intelligence dispatches between Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (acting 2nd Resident of Surakarta) and Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta) from 16 to 20 July 1825 preserved in the Nahuys van Burgst Archive (B.P.L. 616, Portf. 9, No. 2), recording the immediate buildup to the outbreak of the Java War. On 16 July 1825, MacGillavry relays alarming reports from the head *bekel* of Sragen (7 to 8 leagues from Solo): at the start of the Javanese month of Bedaar (Dulkangidah), an organized rebel force was formed for Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*) across the Sukowati (*Sokawati*) region; for three to four months, villagers in Sragen and Desa Babedan (Yogyakarta) have withheld all land rent and tax payments, redirecting the revenues to purchase firearms, ammunition, and rice provisions; MacGillavry explicitly notes the militant aims of this insurgent party (*kraman-partij*). On 18 July 1825, Smissaert replies that these warnings are corroborated by intelligence from court *bupatis* and Patih Danureja IV (*Danuredjo*), appealing for mutual military assistance to safeguard public order in both principalities; remarkably, Smissaert notes that Yogyakarta court regents dismiss Dipanagara as mentally unstable (*"someone who is not right in his head"*). On 20 July 1825, Smissaert dispatches his fateful report to Governor-General Van der Capellen: Dipanagara has flatly refused summonses to explain the armed peasant gathering at Tegalrejo or attend conferences at the Kraton; Pangéran Mangkubumi (*MB*), terrified by Resident threats, has joined Dipanagara; urgent letters from court regents and the Queen Mother Ratu Ageng (*Ratoe Agoeng*) demand immediate military intervention against Dipanagara to protect the Crown; and Smissaert makes a final futile diplomatic effort by dispatching Patih Danureja IV with an ultimatum.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Mangkubumi (*MB*), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Danuredjo*), Ratu Ageng (*Ratoe Agoeng*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (*Solo*), Yogyakarta (*Djogja*), Sragen, Sukowati (*Sokawati*), Desa Babedan, Tegalrejo, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Nahuys van Burgst Archive (B.P.L. 616), Solo-Djogja Correspondence, Head Bekel, Tax Boycott (*arms & rice stockpiles*), Kraman-Partij (Rebel Party), Regency Court Perceptions (*"not right in his head"*), Protection of the Crown (*Kroon*), Summons to Tegalrejo.
