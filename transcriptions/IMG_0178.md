# Transcription of IMG_0178.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0178.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-11-25 to 1825-12-17`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Widespread regional maneuvering, fiscal devastation, and imperial diplomacy in late 1825; most eastern *mancanagara* regents swear renewed loyalty to the colonial government, with the defected Regent of Kertasana returning to Dutch allegiance; Captain Theunissen is posted at Ngawi on the Solo River to maintain armed riverine watch; between Kedu and Jabarangkah, insurgents regain lost ground, leaving Lieutenant-Colonel Cleerens hard pressed; colonial revenues suffer disastrous collapse as opium tax-farmers (specifically the opium contractor of the 21st and 3rd posts) beg to be relieved of their farms, while interior tollgate (*bandar*) receipts are obliterated; General Van Geen and the Panembahan of Sumenep (Madura) travel to Buitenzorg; on 17 December 1825, the Panembahan of Sumenep is officially elevated to the title and rank of Sultan (Sultan Pakunataningrat) while the Sultan of Madura (Bangkalan) receives a gold medal of honor for vital auxiliary military services; west of the Progo River, Dipanagara conducts spirited offensive sorties despite the heavy monsoon rains; Cleerens and colonial forces in Banyumas launch an assault on 8 December at Diele, capturing 80 rebel prisoners and inflicting heavy casualties; the Surakarta enclave district of Blora remains loyal to the government; Lieutenant-General De Kock travels to Buitenzorg (Bogor) to bid farewell to the departing Governor-General Van der Capellen, relinquishing senior field command to General Van Geen; following De Kock's withdrawal, Dipanagara issues a celebrated circular manifesto denouncing De Kock as *"a robber from a strange, foreign part of the world"*.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Regent of Kertasana, Captain Theunissen, Lieutenant-Colonel Jan Baptist Cleerens, Major-General Joseph van Geen, Panembahan of Sumenep [elevated to Sultan Pakunataningrat of Sumenep], Sultan of Madura (Bangkalan), Pangéran Dipanagara, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Mancanagara lands, Kertasana, Ngawi, Solo River (Bengawan Solo), Kedu (Kedhu), Jabarangkah enclaves, Buitenzorg (Bogor), Sumenep (Madura), Madura, Progo River (Kali Praga), Banyumas (Diele), Blora, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Riverine Post, Opium Farm Ruin, Tollgate (*Bandar*) Destruction, Elevation to Sultanate, Gold Medal of Honor, Monsoon Offensive, Anti-Colonial Royal Circular (*"Robber from a Foreign World"*).
