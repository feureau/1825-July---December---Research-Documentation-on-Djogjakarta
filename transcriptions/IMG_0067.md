# Transcription of IMG_0067.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0067.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Kopie No. 5)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official ultimatum issued by Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert from Yogyakarta to Pangéran Dipanagara at Tegalrejo on the morning of Wednesday, 20 July 1825; Smissaert formally accuses Dipanagara of having gathered armed men at his estate (*dalem*) from diverse territories across Kedu, the Sunanate of Surakarta, and the Sultanate of Yogyakarta; charges that Dipanagara had circulated written circular orders (*rondgaande orders*) instructing rural populations to prepare for war, procure weapons, and buy up grain supplies; Smissaert peremptorily summons Dipanagara to appear immediately at the Residency to answer for actions threatening the peace and security of the Kingdom of Mataram; the ultimatum is delivered by two high-ranking court emissaries, Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara and Mas Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat, with orders to demand an immediate and categorical reply.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Pangéran Dipanagara, Mas Tumenggung Sindunegara (court emissary), Radèn Tumenggung Wiryadiningrat (court emissary), Sunan Pakubuwana VI (the Sunan / Emperor), Sultan Hamengkubuwana V.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta Residency, Tegalrejo, Kedu (*Kêdhu*), Surakarta, Kingdom of Mataram.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 Kopie No. 5), Formal colonial summons / Ultimatum, Princely dalem as military assembly point, Circular military orders (*rondgaande orders*), Rice stockpiling for siege operations, Court emissaries (*gecommitteerden*).
