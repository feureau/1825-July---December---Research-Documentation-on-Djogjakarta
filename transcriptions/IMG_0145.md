## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0145.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Solo Letters 1825–1826 / Rebellion in Eastern Mancanagara)
- **Date Range:** `1825-09-16` to `1825-09-26`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Emergency reports dispatched by Acting Resident A.D. Cornets de Groot to Governor-General Van der Capellen and General De Kock on 26 September 1825 regarding the massive expansion of the rebellion across the Eastern Mancanagara (*Mancanagara Wétan* / Madiun region). Reports from Madiun *bupatis* (Radèn Mas Tumenggung Prawiradirja / Prawiradiningrat and Radèn Tumenggung Rangga Prawirasentika) received by Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II indicate that all *tumenggungs* east of Madiun are joining the insurrection, instigated by Radèn Adipati Kertadirja following his defeat in Sukawati. The Chinese population residing throughout the eastern *mancanagara* suffers catastrophic violence and massacres; Carey highlights with double red marginal lines that captive Chinese women had their hair shorn off. At the strategic river junction of Ngawi on the Solo River, an insurgent army commanded by the aristocratic leader Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma (sister of Sultan Sepuh / HB II, married to the Bupati of Munèng, Radèn Tumenggung Yudakusuma) storms the town on 16/17 September 1825, winning over the garrison and putting Chinese tollgate keepers to the sword across all posts. At least 14 to 15 regional regents have turned renegade, including Tumenggung Yudaprawira (Bupati of Maospati), Sasraprawira (Bupati of Magetan), Sasrakusuma (Bupati of Grobogan), Juda Prawira (Bupati of Porong/Sareng), and Sasranegara (Bupati of Godean; identified as Sasrawiria, son of HB III). Although summoned by General De Kock to submit in Surakarta, colonial authorities harbor acute anxiety that the two senior joint *bupati wedana* of Madiun—Prawiradiningrat and Rangga Prawirasentika—will openly defect to Dipanagara.

### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[26 Sept 1825]**<br>*(News from Madiun)* | **Insurrection of Eastern Mancanagara Regencies:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Intelligence from Madiun:* Reports received by Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II from Madiun regents: Radèn Mas Tumenggung Prawiradirja [Prawiradiningrat] and Radèn Tumenggung Rangga Prawirasentika.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *General Uprising East of Madiun:* All *tumenggungs* east of Madiun rise in revolt at the instigation of Bupati Kertadirja [Radèn Adipati Kertadirja], who fled to Madiun after being defeated in Sukawati.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Anti-Chinese Violence:* Most Chinese residing in the Eastern Mancanagara fall victim to the uprising; Chinese women are subjected to humiliation and have their hair cut off (`Women cut off their hair`). |
| **[Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma at Ngawi]**<br>*(Red Box / Underline)* | **Fall of Ngawi & Regency Renegades:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Attack on Ngawi:* Djogja royal aristocrats (*bangsawan*) at the junction of the Solo River attacked and won over on 16/17 September 1825 by Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma [daughter of HB I / sister of Sultan Sepuh, wife of the Bupati of Munèng].<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Destruction of Toll Posts:* Chinese tollgate keepers and residents killed across all regional posts.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Roster of Renegade Regents:* 14 or 15 regents defect to the rebellion:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– Tumenggung Juda Prawira (Bupati of Maospati)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– Tumenggung Sasra Prawira (Bupati of Magetan)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– Tumenggung Sasra Kusuma (Bupati of Grobogan)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– Tumenggung Juda Prawira (Bupati of Porong Sareng)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– Tumenggung Sasra Negara [Sasrawiria, son of HB III] (Bupati of Godean).<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Fear of Total Madiun Defection:* While these regents respond to General De Kock's summons to come to Surakarta, grave fears remain that the two senior joint *bupati wedanas*—Prawiradiningrat and Rangga Prawirasentika—will join the insurrection. |
