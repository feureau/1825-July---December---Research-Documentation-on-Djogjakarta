# Transcription of IMG_0081.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0081.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA [Nationaal Archief, The Hague] Collectie Smissaert No. 4132: *Unmutilated Letters / Debts in Djogja 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1822-04-28 to 1825-10-05
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey analyzes primary documentation from the Smissaert collection concerning illicit financial dealings and usurious private moneylending conducted by Residency Assistant Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier and military Captain Wiesemann in Yogyakarta on the eve of the Java War. The document records formal complaints that Chevallier illegally levied a 1.5% personal commission on coffee transactions throughout the Principalities (*Vorstenlanden* / VL), operating in direct defiance of Governor-General Godert van der Capellen's decree of 28 April 1822 (*Verbaal* No. 28) which strictly barred colonial civil administrators from engaging in commercial trade. It inventories the heavy private debts contracted by Javanese courtiers, detailing the account of Ngabèhi Ratawidjaya, who owed Chevallier 4,500 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) disbursed in installments between January and May 1825 at a monthly interest rate of 1.5% (*"Owenton per 100"*). To secure these advances, Ratawidjaya pawned luxurious family heirlooms including five diamond rings valued at 2,600 Sp. D., paired Chinese gold-and-diamond earrings (700 Sp. D.), and individual jewel allotments bringing total pledged liabilities to 5,800 Sp. D. Ratawidjaya also incurred a 600 Sp. D. debt to Captain Wiesemann secured by two rings (valued at 1,100 Sp. D.), while his wife pledged domestic heirlooms to Mrs. Fredericks, Mrs. Wiehana, and merchant Smark (gold belt buckle, two gold *sirih* pots, and three diamond rings for 700 guilders).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, Ngabèhi Ratawidjaya, Captain Wiesemann, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Mrs. Fredericks, Mrs. Wiehana, Smark.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Vorstenlanden (VL), The Hague (ARA).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ARA Collectie Smissaert No. 4132, Governor-General's Regulation of 28 April 1822 (VB No. 28), coffee monopoly commission (1.5%), Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), Dutch guilders (paper/silver), usury (*renten* / 1.5% monthly), pawned collateral (diamond rings, gold sirih pots, belt buckles).
