# Transcription of IMG_0123.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0123.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 55)
- **Date Range:** 1826-11-05 to 1827-03-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official intelligence dispatches, socio-military surveys, and operational reports of acting Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*) from November 1826 through March 1827 preserved in ANRI *Jogja Letters* No. 55. On 5 November 1826, Van Sevenhoven warns Baron A.M.Th. de Salis (Assistant Resident at Wonosobo) of imminent intelligence that Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*) plans an offensive against Surakarta (*Solo*). On 9 November 1826, writing to student Javanese translator (*élève in de Javaansche taal*) Wormer at Kletek, he reports significant aristocratic submissions following the restoration of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh): Pangéran Blitar, Tumenggung Kusumaredja (*Tum Kusumaregja*), and Tumenggung Sasrawega (*Tung Se Wega*) defect from the rebellion and submit to Sultan Sepuh. On 19 November 1826, Van Sevenhoven notifies the Director-General of Finances that no state revenue from the edible birds' nest monopoly (*vogelnestjes*) could be collected during the 1825/1826 season, as rebel forces occupied the coastal caves of Karangbolong and systematically devastated the swallow habitats. On 18 December 1826, Van Sevenhoven forwards a reimbursement petition to Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock from interpreter Wormer, requesting over 200 guilders expended while on secret mission in Pacitan (*Pacitan* circled in red) for bribing local Javanese headmen and officials. On 20 January 1827, royalist forces return to Solo accompanied by over 140 porters/coolies. Finally, an extensive dispatch dated 18 March (1826/1827) to the military commander at Klaten assesses the social dynamics of Pajang (*Padjang*): while local villagers desire Dutch protection to preserve their property, the stationing of two Yogyakarta princes at Klaten is vital to prevent direct rebel raids; furthermore, Van Sevenhoven highlights the grave danger posed by aristocratic military turncoats such as Tumenggung Suradilaga (*Sura Dilaga*), who briefly defected to the Dutch with his troop detachment before rejoining Dipanagara, noting that such trained, armed soldiers represent a vastly more perilous threat than unarmed village peasants.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven (*Van S*), Baron A.M.Th. de Salis, Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*), Wormer (*élève in Javanese taal*), Pangéran Blitar, Tumenggung Kusumaredja (*Tum Kusumaregja*), Tumenggung Sasrawega (*Tung Se Wega*), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (*Sultan Sepuh*), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Tumenggung Suradilaga (*Sura Dilaga*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Jogja*), Surakarta (*Solo*), Wonosobo, Kletek, Karangbolong, Pacitan, Klaten, Pajang (*Padjang*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Military Intelligence, Aristocratic Submissions to Sultan Sepuh, Edible Birds' Nest Monopoly (*vogelnestjes, Karangbolong destruction*), Secret Service Bribes in Pacitan, Military Porters / Coolies, Social Conditions in Pajang, Armed Rebel Turncoats (*Suradilaga case*).
