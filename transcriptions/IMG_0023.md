## Contextual Notes & Analysis
- **Infrastructure & Edible Birds' Nests:** The road to Gunungkidul and the royal bird's-nest cliffs of **Karangbolong** (on the south coast) were vital revenue sources for both Yogyakarta and Surakarta courts and the colonial government.
- **Epidemics (11 Jan 1825):** Cholera (*cholera morbus*) was spreading inland from Salatiga; the colonial administration distributed cajeput oil (*minyak kayu putih*) and brandy as emergency palliatives. Simultaneously, smallpox vaccination was compromised by lack of lymph vaccine.
- **Pacitan Administration & Wormser:** C.F.E. Wormser served as colonial administrator/inspector in Pacitan supervising royal cash crops (coffee and pepper). He collaborated with **Pangéran Aria Mangkunegara** to arrange porterage and overland transport into Yogyakarta.
- **Toll Resistance along the Bengawan Solo (12 Jan 1825):** The tax farmer (*pachter*) at Rangka and the *begalan* (market/transit gate) at Jerenan faced active resistance from Javanese merchants and boatmen. Smissaert dispatched Grand Vizier Danureja IV to clarify jurisdictional boundaries—specifically that while riverborne transit on the Bengawan Solo enjoyed certain treaty exemptions, overland road transit remained fully taxable.
