# Transcription of IMG_0103.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0103.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54, August 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-19 to 1825-08-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Insurgents cut off water conduits supplying Fort Vredeburg and the adjacent Chinese quarter (*Kampung Cina*), causing acute water shortages. On 19 August, a severe night curfew is imposed across the city of Yogyakarta. Smissaert reports to the Resident of Magelang (Kedu) on 23 August that rebels have begun attacking northern regional sectors around Probolinggo / Purbalingga. Rampant hyperinflation strikes the besieged city: 125 lbs of rice (c. 62.5 kg) skyrockets to 60 guilders. On 22 August, a general coordinated rebel assault hits Yogyakarta: large formations of insurgent cavalry and infantry advance flying war flags and royal umbrellas (*payungs*), the fighters uniformly dressed in white religious robes (*kain putih*) and multi-colored turbans. During fierce combat, a Dutch Hussar cavalryman is struck down and killed by a kris blow, his blood drunk and his decapitated head paraded aloft by rebels. On 26 August, Smissaert reports to the Residents of Pekalongan and Semarang that Post Utarang along the vital northern post-road to Semarang has been torched to the ground, and that the Kabumen district bordering Pekalongan is in full insurrection.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Resident of Magelang (Kedu), Resident of Pekalongan, Resident of Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Fort Vredeburg, Kampung Cina, Yogyakarta, Probolinggo / Purbalingga, Post Utarang, Semarang road, Pekalongan, Kabumen (Kebumen).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Water supply sabotage, curfew, hyperinflation (rice at 60 guilders), white religious battle vestments (*kain putih*), turbans, payungs (state umbrellas), decapitation ritual, Dutch Hussars, kris.
