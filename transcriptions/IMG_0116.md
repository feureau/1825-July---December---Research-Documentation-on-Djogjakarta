# Transcription of IMG_0116.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0116.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* No. 54 [Oct–Dec 1825 & Jan–Aug 1826], December 1825 – January 1826).
- **Date Range:** 1825-12-12 to 1826-01-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On 15 December 1825, Resident Van Sevenhoven informs General De Kock of his administrative decision to appoint new *bekels* (village headmen) to replace those who had defected to Dipanagara, particularly across the Kalibeber district. On 12 December, the tollgate customs farmer of Madiun petitions for relief, unable to meet contracted revenue quotas due to war devastation. On 2 January 1826, Van Sevenhoven urgently warns the Resident of Surakarta of a total salt famine in Yogyakarta, pleading for immediate salt convoys. The same day, a municipal loan remittance of f. 8,145 in silver specie arrives from Semarang, accompanied by requisitions for dried fish supplies. On 4 January, Chinese tax-farmer Kwa Kui-Djie and Johannes are officially confirmed in the opium tax monopoly across intermediate garrison posts—Klaten, Prambanan, Borongan, Kaloran, and Jenu—amid desperate cries for unhusked rice (*padi*). That same day, Van Sevenhoven notifies the Director of Finances that compiling a survey of agricultural paddy fields is impossible under wartime conditions, but endorses a proposal that private plantation proprietors at Sérang, Sasakan, and Setas furnish rice and salt reserves from their estates to provision the city—a policy supported by Patih Danureja IV. On 9 January, Van Sevenhoven communicates with the Director of Schools in Semarang emphasizing the pressing civil necessity of establishing a primary school in Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. van der Capellen, Pangéran Dipanagara, Kwa Kui-Djie (Chinese tax-farmer), Johannes (opium farmer), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Director of Schools in Semarang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Kalibeber, Madiun, Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Klaten, Prambanan, Borongan, Kaloran, Jenu, Sérang, Sasakan, Setas.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Replacement of rebel *bekels*, customs tollgate (*bandar*), salt famine, silver loan (f. 8,145), opium monopoly lease (*candhu*), estate food requisitions (*padi* and salt), primary school establishment.
