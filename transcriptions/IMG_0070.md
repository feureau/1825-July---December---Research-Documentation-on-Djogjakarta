# Transcription of IMG_0070.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0070.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Kopie No. 12)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Urgent evening dispatch written by Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert from Yogyakarta to Governor-General Godert van der Capellen in Buitenzorg on Wednesday, 20 July 1825, detailing the catastrophic collapse of negotiations and the outbreak of open rebellion; Smissaert reports that armed villagers from both Yogyakarta and Surakarta territories continue pouring into Tegalrejo despite colonial remonstrances; acknowledges that Dipanagara refused to come to the Kraton or Mangkubumi's residence because he feared being arrested, and sought to openly overthrow Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV; describes sending Pangéran Mangkubumi on a second conciliation mission on the morning of 20 July (following an initial visit on 19 July) to demand the demobilization of Dipanagara's retainers; reveals the devastating shock when Mangkubumi defected, notifying the Residency that he had permanently joined Dipanagara at Tegalrejo; notes that the two rebel princes had begun issuing circular letters to court *tumenggungs* and regional regents commanding them to join the Holy War; describes total panic gripping Yogyakarta—city bazaars and markets deserted, looting breaking out, and terrified merchants locking goods indoors as war rumors (*prang*) spread; concludes with Smissaert convening an emergency war council comprising Ratu Ageng, Patih Danureja IV, and Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Godert Alexander Gerard Philip van der Capellen (Governor-General), Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), Paku Alam I, Ratu Ageng (Queen Mother).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Tegalrejo, Dalem Mangkubumen, Kraton Yogyakarta, Surakarta enclaves.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 Kopie No. 12), Evening dispatch to Governor-General, Defection of Pangéran Mangkubumi, Urban panic / Market collapse (*bazaars empty*), Rumors of war (*prang*), Intercepted insurgent circulars to Tumenggungs, Emergency council of state.
