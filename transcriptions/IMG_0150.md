# Transcription of IMG_0150.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0150.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Solo Letters 1825–1826 / Rebellion in Eastern Mancanagara, January 1826)
- **Date Range:** `1825-12-22` to `1826-01-04`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical intelligence dispatches from Collectie De Kock No. 183 sent to Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II detailing the rout, casualties, and desperate conditions of the rebel remnants on Mount Lawu. On 4 January 1826, reports confirm that rebel commanders Pangéran Serang and Radèn Adipati Kertadirja have escaped higher into the mountain forests. Several senior rebel commanders are killed in action: Tumenggung Kenitèn, Tumenggung Suradirja, and Ngabèhi Ranamenggala of Sela. High-ranking captives taken by colonial troops include the mother and eldest brother of Pangéran Serang (Carey notes: *"Mother of Serang / ? R.A. Serang. See Louw III, p. 573"*), Ngabèhi Prajasentana of Ramé, and Ngabèhi Bangsajuda of Sela. In addition, numerous local village headmen from Grobogan and Kudus involved in the uprisings across Demak, Serang, and Madiun are captured. Fleeing insurgent soldiers and horses die in large numbers from hypothermia and starvation in the freezing high-altitude terrain, forced to subsist on mountain grass and roots. The son-in-law of Radèn Adipati Kertadirja (appointed by him as rebel Bupati of Magetan, and biological son of Madiun Tumenggung Sorang Sareng) surrenders unconditionally. An earlier report of 22 December 1825 notes 500 to 600 rebel cavalrymen still operating on horseback under the Patih of Pangéran Serang, named as Brawijaya.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Serang, Radèn Adipati Kertadirja, Radèn Ayu Serang (Mother of Pangéran Serang), Eldest Brother of Pangéran Serang, Tumenggung Kenitèn, Tumenggung Suradirja, Ngabèhi Ranamenggala, Ngabèhi Prajasentana, Ngabèhi Bangsajuda, Son-in-Law of Kertadirja (Bupati of Magetan), Tumenggung Sorang Sareng, Brawijaya (Patih of Pangéran Serang), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Gunung Lawu (Mount Lawu), Magetan, Madiun, Grobogan, Kudus, Demak, Serang, Sela (Sesela), Ramé, Surakarta (Solo)
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Eastern Mancanagara Insurrection, High-Altitude Famine and Cold, Living on Grass and Roots, Surrender of Rebel Regents, Rebel Cavalry, Patih of Pangéran Serang
