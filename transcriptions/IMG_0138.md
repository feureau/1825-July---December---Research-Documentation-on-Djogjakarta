## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0138.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Surakarta Letters / Intercepted Dipanagara Piagem)
- **Date Range:** `1825-07-28` to `1825-07-28`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's transcription and translation of an intercepted original Javanese *piagem* (official charter / commission of authority) issued on the direct authority of Pangéran Dipanagara and executed by Radèn Ngabèhi Wirsa Dikrama. Dated in the Javanese lunar month of Besar, Year Bé 1752 (corresponding to late July 1825, intercepted and forwarded by Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry on 28 July 1825), the document elevates and appoints a prominent local brigand leader, Biesahi [Besari?] Ngabèhi Mangga [Rongga] Witsana, under the official title of Maas Ngabèhi Rongga Witsana as commander over Dipanagara's irregular subjects, specifically identified as rovers and *ketjus* (*kècu* / bandits / social outlaws). The charter lays down the foundational moral, religious, and tactical code governing insurgent forces during the Java War: troops are expressly commanded to do no harm whatsoever to anyone following the Muslim religion (the Islamic faithful), but to direct their armed attacks exclusively against the Dutch, Chinese, Arabs, and all overseas foreign nations (*wong sabrang*). In an extraordinary clause outlining postwar social rehabilitation, the charter promises that once victory is achieved and peace is restored to the Javanese kingdom, all *ketjus* and bandits shall receive grants of land and return to their home villages to support themselves through honest agriculture and husbandry.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Ngabèhi Wirsa Dikrama, Maas Ngabèhi Rongga Witsana (Biesahi Ngabèhi Mangga Witsana), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Mataram
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Piagem (Letter of Appointment / Charter), Kècu (*Ketjus* / Bandits / Outlaws), Holy War Code (*Perang Sabil*), Overseas Nations (*Wong Sabrang*), Year Bé 1752 (AJ) / Month Besar, Postwar Resettlement / Agriculture
