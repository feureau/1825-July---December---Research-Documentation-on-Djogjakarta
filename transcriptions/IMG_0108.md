# Transcription of IMG_0108.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0108.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ARA 4132 [*Jan's Bijlage*], *Yogya Letters*, MacGillavry dispatches, September–October 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-09-27 to 1825-10-14
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On 27 September, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry assumes duties as acting Resident of Yogyakarta under orders from Lieutenant-General De Kock. After initial attempts to win over Dipanagara and Mangkubumi through peaceful persuasion fail, De Kock resolves to launch an all-out assault on the rebel headquarters at Selarong scheduled for 30 September. On 8 October, MacGillavry coordinates with the military commandant at Prambanan, instructing Tumenggung Danunegara and Tumenggung Mertanegara to secure villages and pacify the Pajang district. On 10 October, MacGillavry reports to De Kock regarding the accession arrangements for the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V: all personal royal property is to be held in trust by the guardians, alongside 100,000 Spanish Dollars derived from lands administered by Patih Danureja IV. On 14 October, MacGillavry alerts the Resident of Cirebon that government areas in Karangbolong have fallen to rebel control, where the European edible birds'-nest overseer A. Portier is captured; in Rawa, Islamic law and religion are formally instituted for all inhabitants, and colonial authorities dispatch military detachments from Nusa Kambangan and Cirebon to safeguard the valuable edible birds'-nest cliffs (Carey annotates the eventual capture of Radèn Ayu Sérang in January 1826). The same day, MacGillavry warns the Resident of Rembang that intelligence reports indicate an imminent rebel assault on Rembang, advising a policy of negotiating to win over rebellious local bupatis.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (Acting Resident), Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. van der Capellen, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Tumenggung Danunegara, Tumenggung Mertanegara, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, A. Portier (European overseer), Radèn Ayu Sérang, Chaviel van Rabok (Resident of Rembang).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Selarong, Prambanan, Pajang, Karangbolong, Rawa, Nusa Kambangan, Cirebon, Rembang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Accession treaty of HB V, royal guardianship, 100,000 Spanish Dollars, edible birds'-nest monopoly (*sarang burung*), Islamic law instituted, pacification of Pajang.
