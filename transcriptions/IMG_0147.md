# Transcription of IMG_0147.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0147.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Rebellion in Eastern Mancanagara / Attack on Ngawi Eyewitness Statement)
- **Date Range:** `1825-05-01` to `1825-09-27`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official deposition and eyewitness intelligence taken from Collectie De Kock No. 183, submitted by Ngabèhi Trunamenggala recording the sworn statement of his subordinate Ranudongso (from Desa Kademangan) on 27 September 1825 regarding the devastating rebel storming of Ngawi. Having traveled to Ngawi in May 1825 to purchase rice and remaining there over the summer, Ranudongso witnesses an insurgent army of 2,000 fighters launching a dawn assault at 3:00 AM on Saturday, 17 September 1825. The attack is spearheaded by Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma of Munèng, Radèn Tumenggung Purwadadi, and Radèn Ngabèhi Granitèn (with Carey adding that Radèn Tumenggung Suradirja was subsequently killed in action). The insurgents descend upon the Chinese quarter and lucrative customs tollgate posts located along the riverbank (cross-referenced with Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier's earlier investigative reports). Approximately 40 Chinese residents mount a desperate defense, barricading themselves inside their homes and firing down upon the attackers from second-story attics. The insurgent forces systematically set fire to the houses; 12 Chinese defenders are killed on the spot, the remaining survivors are captured and marched off as prisoners to Purwodadi, and the entire Chinese settlement is reduced to ashes.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Ngabèhi Trunamenggala, Ranudongso, Radèn Ayu Yudakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Purwadadi, Radèn Ngabèhi Granitèn, Radèn Tumenggung Suradirja, P.F.H. Chevallier
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Ngawi, Desa Kademangan, Purwodadi, Munèng, Solo Riverbank
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Eastern Mancanagara Rebellion, Assault on Ngawi Chinese Quarter, River Tollgate (*Bandar*), Arson and Attic Defense, Chinese Prisoners, Rice Trade
