## Contextual Notes & Analysis
- **Sultan's Guardianship & Family Politics (13 Feb 1825):** The official guardianship (*wali*) of child-sultan Hamengkubuwana V comprised his great-grandmother Ratu Ageng, mother Ratu Kencana, grand-uncle Pangéran Mangkubumi, and Pangéran Diponegoro. Facing destitute pleas from the exiled Sultan Sepuh (HB II) in Surabaya, the court guardians sanctioned a remittance of 5,000 guilders. Peter Carey references this directly in the *Babad Dipanagara*.
- **Death of Pangéran Mangkudiningrat:** Eldest son of HB II, exiled to Ambon following the British storming of the Kraton (1812), died in Ambon in 1824. His mortal remains were transported back to Java in 1825 for interment in the royal cemetery at **Imogiri**.
- **The Kraton Stabbing Crisis (28 Feb 1825):** A sensational harem incident. Ratu Kencana (daughter of Radèn Adipati Danureja II and mother of the young HB V), suffering severe insomnia, fever, and hysteria while reading wayang texts, attacked Radèn Ayu Puspitasari (a former secondary wife/*garwa ampeyan* of Hamengkubuwana III) with an heirloom dagger/keris. The child sultan Hamengkubuwana V and dowager queen Ratu Ageng fled the palace in terror to the nearby residence of Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat.
