# Transcription of IMG_0086.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0086.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA [Nationaal Archief, The Hague] *Collectie De Kock* No. 181 / No. 111: *Letters of Resident Smissaert to Governor-General Van der Capellen, 24 July 1825 – 7 Jan. 1826*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-24 to 1825-11-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey abstracts high-level dispatches from the De Kock Collection recording Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert's reports to Governor-General Godert van der Capellen on 24 July 1825, alongside retrospective notes on Smissaert's subsequent dismissal. Smissaert reports that the primary punitive sortie directed against Dipanagara and Mangkubumi ended in total failure (*"is vruchteloos afgeloopen"*), as every single village along the march route had risen in spontaneous rebellion, with blocked roads rendering colonial cavalry and artillery ineffective. Simultaneously, devastating news arrives regarding the Pisangan disaster: rebel leaders Dipadirja (Desa Pisangan), Naïpe (Desa Logorok in Surakarta territory), and Yuda Kertajaya (Desa Kalibodo in Yogyakarta territory), acting under the supreme leadership of Princes Dipanagara, Mangkubumi, Adinegara, and Suryadipura, ambushed the colonial silver treasure convoy from Kedu. The insurgents captured 28,000 guilders and annihilated the 40-man European escort, massacring commanding officer Second Lieutenant Boogaard and his troops in a gruesome manner (*"op een schrikkelijke wijze"*). Reconnaissance cavalry sent to locate the plunder returned empty-handed, and with the direct Kedu highway completely severed, dispatches had to be forwarded in duplicate via Surakarta. Archival annotations document that Lieutenant-General De Kock issued a formal decree dismissing Smissaert from the Residency of Yogyakarta on 24 September 1825, concluding with his final departure on 20 November 1825.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Tumenggung Adinegara, Pangéran Suryadipura, Dipadirja (Dipa Drana), Naïpe (Nampi), Yuda Kertajaya (Kerta Yuda), Second Lieutenant Boogaard, Colonel von Jett.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Kedu, Pisangan, Desa Logorok, Desa Kalibodo, Surakarta (Solo), Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ARA Collectie De Kock No. 181, fruitless expedition (*vruchteloos afgeloopen*), treasure convoy robbery (f. 28,000), barbaric massacre (*op een schrikkelijke wijze*), duplicate communications, dismissal of Smissaert (24 Sept. 1825).
