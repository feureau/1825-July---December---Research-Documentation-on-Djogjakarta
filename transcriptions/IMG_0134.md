# Transcription of IMG_0134.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0134.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 183 - Solo Letters 1825 / Nahuys van Burgst Papers No. 95)
- **Date Range:** `1825-07-28` to `1825-09-04`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Dramatic nocturnal revelation of royal treachery and conspiracy in Surakarta at the outbreak of the Java War. At 2:30 AM on 28 July 1825, Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry receives an alarming message from First Lieutenant Schlösser (commanding European and Surakarta forces on the Klaten road), disclosing that Major Tirta Diwirya, commander of the Sunan's auxiliary detachment, received secret verbal orders directly from Sunan Pakubuwana VI stipulating that should Pangéran Dipanagara and his rebel army reach Delanggu, they must be allowed to march unhindered into Surakarta. MacGillavry immediately convenes a crisis conference at 6:00 AM with Pangéran Buminata, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), and Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, and sends an urgent courier imploring Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock to accelerate his march to Surakarta to prevent the court from formally joining the rebellion. Carey's notes cross-reference these findings with Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst's confidential memorandum (No. 95, 2–4 September 1828 / 1825) detailing Sunan Pakubuwana VI's complicity, including a circular letter bearing the code name *"OBAT"* (*obat* / gunpowder / medicine) distributed to court nobles encouraging them to join Dipanagara's *brandals*, explicitly implicating Pangéran Purbaya (the future Pakubuwana VII) and Radèn Ngabéhi Natakusuma.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, First Lieutenant Schlösser, Major Tirta Diwirya, Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Buminata, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Purbaya (Sunan Pakubuwana VII), Radèn Ngabéhi Natakusuma
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Delanggu, Yogyakarta, Klaten, Semarang
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock No. 183, Nahuys van Burgst Papers No. 95, Sunan's Kraton Detachment, Secret Code *"OBAT"*, *Brandals* (Rebels / Insurgents), Mangkunagaran Legion
