# Transcription of IMG_0166.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0166.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Diary of Van der Capellen, July 1825 – Jan 1826*; *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], 1860, vol. 11, p. 367).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-01 to 1825-08-15
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Journal entries of Governor-General Van der Capellen recording political intrigue and military developments across Surakarta, Kedu, and Yogyakarta during August 1825. In Surakarta, the situation deteriorates into acute uncertainty: the erratic young Sunan Pakubuwana VI's orders are openly flouted as he attempts to appease rebel princes, prompting serious fears that Surakarta court troops might defect. Pangéran Mangkunagara II remains exceptionally resolute in demanding rigorous compliance with Resident MacGillavry's instructions, while the *Rijksbestuurder* (Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II) and Pangéran Buminata maintain their alignment with the colonial government. All direct communications with Yogyakarta remain severed; rural uprisings surge across the countryside with Chinese tollgate and tax-farmers (*bandar*) slaughtered without mercy alongside their households. In early August, Lieutenant-General De Kock arrives at Semarang and Surakarta, sending encouraging reports (*"goede demenan"*) confirming the loyalty of senior Surakarta princes and issuing a proclamation placing governance in the Vorstenlanden under an extraordinary government council. On the Kedu front, three rebel tumenggungs brandishing red and yellow royal payungs advance, but conditions stabilize as a relief detachment of 60 infantry and two field artillery pieces from Semarang successfully reaches Magelang. Finally, Van der Capellen records De Kock's decisive dismissal of Yogyakarta Resident A.H. Smissaert, whose catastrophic mishandling, ill-advised negotiations, and criminal indifference had provoked the conflagration; military adjutant Major Ferré is appointed acting Resident because of his proven soldierly capability and extensive experience dealing with the Javanese nobility.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Pangéran Mangkunagara II (Prangwedana), Panembahan Buminata, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II (Rijksbestuurder / Patih of Surakarta), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, P. le Clercq (Resident of Kedu), Captain Duperron, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Major Ferré (Acting Resident of Yogyakarta), Pangéran Dipanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Yogyakarta, Semarang, Kedu, Magelang, Makassar (Celebes).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sunanate Court Politics, Royal Vacillation, Mangkunagaran Loyalty, Anti-Chinese Violence / Slaughter of Tax-Farmers (*bandar*), Colonial Specie / Field Artillery, *Payung* (red and yellow status parasols), Official Proclamation (Government in Council), Dismissal of Resident Smissaert, Military Adjutant / Soldier-Resident Appointment.
