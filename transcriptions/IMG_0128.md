- **Date Range:** 1825-07-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historiographical and archival documentation compiled from H.F. Aukes' *Het Legioen van Mangkoe-Negoro* (1935, pp. 61–63) recounting the immediate catalysts of the Java War in July 1825. Patih Danureja IV's highway project connecting Yogyakarta to Magelang traverses Pangéran Dipanagara's estate at Tegalrejo without prior notification, passing directly through his residential property; peasant corvée laborers conscripted from Sleman and Bantul side with Dipanagara, and when surveyors' stakes are replanted, Dipanagara summons his armed retainers to Tegalrejo. Secret mobilization circulars are dispatched across North Coast regencies and the Eastern Mancanagara (Madiun, Kediri, Rembang, Bagelen, Sukowati) ordering: *"When the cannons sound, at all times, by day and night you must stand prepared."* Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert remains ignorant of developments until 19 July 1825, when 2nd Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry of Surakarta informs him that Dipanagara has remitted four *puasas* of taxes to two Surakarta chiefs and is organizing a rebel *"Kraman party"*. Smissaert complacently dismisses the danger, assuming Dipanagara will soon acknowledge his errors. Carey transcribes MacGillavry's secret letter of 19 July 1825 to Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier (a decorated Waterloo cavalry veteran who died in Yogyakarta on 11 November 1825): MacGillavry warns that Demang Grojogan is marching toward Yogyakarta with 100 followers; intelligence reveals rebel plans to fall first upon Pacitan before overcoming Yogyakarta; bazaar rumors (*merae nugae*) report that war (*prang*) is imminent, the common people have hidden and pawned their possessions (*geborgen*), and Patih Danureja IV has ascended Mount Merapi to make solemn pledges for war. Aukes notes that colonial officials in Surakarta had firm intelligence of the rebellion well before authorities in Yogyakarta itself.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, P.F.H. Chevallier, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Demang Grojogan, H.F. Aukes.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Jogja*), Magelang, Tegalrejo (*Tegalredja*), Sleman, Bantul, Madiun, Kediri, Rembang, Bagelen, Sukowati, Surakarta (*Solo*), Desa Grojogan, Pacitan (*Patjitan*), Mount Merapi.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Het Legioen van Mangkoe-Negoro* (H.F. Aukes, 1935), Road Stake Dispute, Mobilization Circulars, Kraman Party, Puasa Tax Remissions, Demang, Military Order of William (MWO 4th Class, Waterloo), Rumors of War (*prang*), Pledged Possessions (*geborgen*), Merapi Vows.
