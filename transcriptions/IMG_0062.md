# Transcription of IMG_0062.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0062.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / Official Dispatches)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official letter from Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert addressed directly to Pangéran Dipanagara (*"co-guardian of His Highness the Sultan"*) dated 18 July 1825, written just forty-eight hours before the armed assault on Tegalrejo; Smissaert expresses shock and sorrow that Dipanagara had sent an ultimatum to Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV expressing fears that the Dutch and Danureja intended to harm him; Smissaert attempts to mollify the prince with paternalistic assurances of European esteem, reminding Dipanagara that the Dutch government demonstrated supreme confidence in his loyalty by formally investing him as co-guardian (*wali*) of the child sovereign Sultan Hamengkubuwana V; urges Dipanagara to abandon all suspicions and to bypass the Patih by communicating complaints directly to the Resident himself, hypocritically professing profound reverence for the Yogyakarta royal lineage and Dipanagara's personal dignity.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Rijksbestuurder*), Sultan Hamengkubuwana V (*the young Sultan*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta Residency, Tegalrejo, Kraton Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Royal co-guardianship (*wali*), Resident's summons / Conciliatory ultimatum, Dutch-court alliance vs. princely opposition.
