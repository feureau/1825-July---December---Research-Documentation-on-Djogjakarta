# Transcription of IMG_0053.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0053.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, Collectie Smissaert, ARA 4132: *Opgave der personen dewelke in dit land ambtenaren zijn geworden*)
- **Date Range:** 1823 to 1825
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's transcription of confidential official conduct evaluations of European colonial personnel serving in the Yogyakarta Residency under Resident A.H. Smissaert; records a damning appraisal of Assistant Resident P.F.H. Chevallier, noting that while possessing great talent he suffers from a corrupt heart and unprincipled wickedness (*"great ability but with a corrupt heart... I have found out many times his wickedness"*); characterizes clerk and commission official J.G. van den Berg as awkward and uncooperative (*ongemakkelijk*), noting that despite understanding Javanese he speaks it haltingly and poorly, while having a surprisingly defective command of Dutch; evaluates senior clerk J. Roos (1st Commis) as having demonstrated genuine capability and fitness for his post.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, J.G. van den Berg, J. Roos, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Heer van Maak.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogjakarta).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Ambtenaren (colonial civil servants), 1st Commis / 1st Klasse, Confidential conduct registers (Opgave der personen).
