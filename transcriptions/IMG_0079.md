- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0079.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81: *Letters concerning Dipanagara, July 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-22 to 1825-07-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** In late July 1825, Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert coordinates emergency military responses as the uprising led by Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi erupts across the Yogyakarta environs and spreads rapidly into surrounding districts. Smissaert awaits the arrival of Colonel von Jett with reinforcements from Semarang while reporting that Dipanagara and Mangkubumi have concentrated their followers at Selarong, with widespread attacks by *wong durjana* (brigands/rebels) making vital arterial roads impassable. The Residency communication lifeline to Magelang is cut at the old Kemlaka tollgate (*bandar*). On 23 July 1825, Smissaert urgently warns Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry in Surakarta and the Resident of Kedu that insurgent bands commanded by Dipadirja (Desa Pisangan), Nampi (Desa Logorok), and Kartajaya (Desa Kalibodo) have severed the high road around Pisangan, ambushing a detachment of 125 men escorting a colonial treasure convoy of 28,000 Dutch guilders sent from Magelang. The convoy is shattered, its commanding officer Second Lieutenant Boogaard and 40 soldiers killed, and the f. 28,000 captured. Smissaert frantically requests immediate military support from Surakarta (including 12,000 live infantry cartridges and 1,000 pistol cartridges under heavy escort) and urges Kedu to deploy bupati contingents to scour the borderlands for the *wong brandhal*, indifferent to jurisdictional boundaries between Yogyakarta and Surakarta territory.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Colonel von Jett, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Dipadirja, Nampi, Kartajaya, Second Lieutenant Boogaard.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Semarang, Selarong (Nilarong), Kedu (Kêdhu), Magelang, Kemlaka, Pisangan, Desa Logorok, Desa Kalibodo, Surakarta (Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven Bundel 81, wong durjana (outlaws / robbers), wong brandhal (rebels / bandits), bandar (tollgate), treasury convoy (f. 28,000), scherpe patronen (ball cartridges), Sunan's troops.
