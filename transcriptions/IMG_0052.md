# Transcription of IMG_0052.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0052.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nationaal Archief, The Hague, Collectie Smissaert, ARA 4132: *Van Sevenhoven Private Correspondence*, continuation)
- **Date Range:** 1824-12-14 to 1825-01-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of private confidential correspondence to Commissioner J.I. van Sevenhoven regarding the disputed Jabarangkah enclaves; criticizes the proposed annexation of Yogyakarta lands (specifically naming Goktook [Gagak], Wadas Wari, and Kertanegara) to Pekalongan, stressing they do not lie along colonial communication lines and that stripping Yogyakarta of territory is blatantly unfair; reminds Batavia that Yogyakarta territory is already far smaller than Surakarta's due to disproportionate annexations under Daendels (1811) and Raffles (1812); urges the colonial government to make an immediate decision regarding compensation (*indemnificatie*) for cancelled land leases, warning of extreme danger arising from peasant destitution and fiscal uncertainty; records a dispatch from Batavia dated 29 January 1825 revealing that high-level policy decisions regarding the Principalities were frozen until the return of Dutch military forces deployed on the expedition to Bone / South Sulawesi (*Makassar*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Thomas Stamford Raffles, Governor-General G.A.G.P. Baron van der Capellen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Jabarangkah (Djabarangkah), Pekalongan, Wadas Wari, Kertanegara, Goktook, Yogyakarta (Djogja), Surakarta (Solo), Batavia, Makassar (Bone).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Vorstenlanden (VL), Landhuur abolition / indemnity uncertainty, Peasant poverty, Makassar military expedition.
