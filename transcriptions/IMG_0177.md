# Transcription of IMG_0177.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0177.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1825-11-20 to 1825-11-24`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Governor-General Van der Capellen records an improving strategic outlook in late November 1825; all government-administered residencies remain at peace while the Surakarta court renders substantial military assistance; Sunan Pakubuwana VI dispatches Surakarta princes (presumably Panembahan Buminata) to campaign against Dipanagara; General Van Geen drives Dipanagara's forces west across the Progo River; General De Kock institutes radical reforms in Yogyakarta's regency administration for 6-year-old Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, appointing Pangeran Panular (brother of HB II) and Pangeran Murdaningrat (son of HB II) as new royal guardians (*curatoren*); acting Resident Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven invests the new guardians, establishing strict colonial oversight over internal Kraton governance which had completely disintegrated under Smissaert; the Resident assumes executive authority in the child Sultan's name and schedules regular administrative meetings with Patih Danureja IV and senior court dignitaries to detach rebel partisans; imperative orders issued to complete all defensive fortifications prior to the impending rainy season; sudden demise of Assistant Resident Pieter Frits Chevallier; Van der Capellen laments Chevallier's death as a profound loss of a diligent civil servant, vigorously defending him against public accusations that he and Smissaert provoked the Java War; in Grobogan, Pangéran Sèrang experiences successive military defeats.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Panembahan Buminata, Pangéran Dipanagara, Major-General Joseph van Geen, Pangéran Panular [uncle of HB V / brother of HB II], Pangéran Murdaningrat [uncle of HB V / son of HB II], Sultan Hamengkubuwana V [child Sultan of Yogyakarta], Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven [acting Resident of Yogyakarta], Anthony Hendrik Smissaert [former Resident], Radèn Adipati Danureja IV [Patih of Yogyakarta], Pieter Frits Chevallier [Assistant Resident of Yogyakarta, died November 1825], Pangéran Sèrang.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo), Progo River (Kali Praga), Yogyakarta (Djokja), Kraton Yogyakarta, Grobogan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Guardians of the Sultan (*Curatoren*), Kraton Internal Administration, Direct Resident Rule, Fortifications, Rainy Season (Monsoon), Official Eulogy / Colonial Justification.
