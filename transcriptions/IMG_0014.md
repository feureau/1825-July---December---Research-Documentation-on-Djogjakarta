### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Pangéran Dipanagara (Diponegoro)** (`DP`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 10). Resided at Tegalrejo.
  * **Pangéran Mangkubumi** (`Mangkoeboemie / MB`): Listed under line 63. Son of HB I, uncle of Dipanagara, fellow guardian of HB V.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja IV** (`PATIH / Danurejo`): Listed under line 184. Pro-Dutch Patih of Yogyakarta (1813–1847).
  * **Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert** (`Res / v`): Listed under *Residents of Yogyakarta* (line 345). Dutch Resident of Yogyakarta (1823–1825) whose complacency contributed to the escalation at Tegalrejo.
  * **Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier** (`Chevallier / ASST RES`): Assistant Resident of Yogyakarta under Smissaert.
  * **Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry** (`Macgillavry`): Listed under lines 319, 346.
  * **Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst** (`Nahuys van Burgst`): Listed under line 341.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Tegalreja** (`TR / Prince's KRATON`): Listed under *Places* (line 462). Dipanagara's country estate outside Yogyakarta.
  * **Kraton**: The royal court / palace of Yogyakarta.
  * **Solo**: Surakarta Sunanate.
  * **Djogja**: Yogyakarta Sultanate.
* **Terms:**
  * **Patih**: Chief Minister of the Sultanate.
  * **Poasa / Pasa**: The Islamic fasting month of Ramadan (in 1825, Ramadan ended in mid-May; the text refers to the Garebeg / post-fasting court gatherings).
