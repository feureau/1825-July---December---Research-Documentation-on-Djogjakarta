## Contextual Notes & Analysis
- **Pusaka Lineage (Majapahit Keris):** The dagger used by Ratu Kencana in the Kraton attack was identified as an heirloom (*pusaka*) tracing back to the Majapahit empire, inherited from her grandfather Radèn Adipati Danureja I (first Grand Vizier of Yogyakarta, who served under Sultan Hamengkubuwana I from 1755 to 1799). In Javanese belief, ancient heirloom weapons possessed spiritual agency and could induce trance-like frenzy if mishandled.
- **Jabarangkah Commission (16 Dec 1824):** The colonial administration formed a joint boundary commission to delineate and annex the Jabarangkah enclaves (isolated princely enclaves situated in northern and western Java surrounded by Dutch territories). Chevallier's appointment reflects his rising administrative prominence, though he had to be recalled to Yogyakarta to manage the Residency during Resident Smissaert's frequent leaves.
- **Pekalongan Border Unrest (15 Mar 1825):** The territorial restructuring and tax pressures surrounding the Jabarangkah annexations provoked widespread unrest along the border zone of Pekalongan, necessitating emergency inspections by colonial officials.
