# Transcription of IMG_0151.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0151.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief, ARA 2791, No. 14, Part 3 & 9 / Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade, 31 Dec. 1825)
- **Date Range:** `1824-10-22` to `1825-12-31`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official deliberations of the Council of the Indies (*Raad van Indië*) under Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. baron van der Capellen in Batavia on 31 December 1825, evaluating secret residency dispatches concerning the alarming behavior and political reliability of Sunan Pakubuwana VI. The Council examines a confidential dispatch from the Resident of Surakarta describing the state of the Surakarta court and the Sunan's erratic, extravagant conduct, which severely imperils peace. Council member Hendrik Jacob van de Graaff issues a severe memorandum asserting that the Sunan must be compelled to submit unconditionally to all previous treaties and contracts concluded with the Dutch government; so long as the monarch fails to demonstrate strict obedience, the safety and authority of the colonial state remain in jeopardy. Van de Graaff notes that six months have elapsed without any improvement in the Sunan's posture following the appointment of Resident J.I. van Sevenhoven. The minutes also outline long-term colonial fiscal ambitions to lease expansive agricultural domains from the Javanese princes, focusing on strategic border enclaves to the west/north toward Cirebon and Tegal, and to the east toward Pasuruan and Surabaya. In Part 9, the dossier addresses the domestic and political crisis triggered by the Sunan's unilateral demand to divorce his official royal consort Ratu Mas (daughter of Pangéran Ngabèhi). The Council concludes that the Sunan's conduct is incompatible with sovereign authority and actively considers divesting him of governmental control until he demonstrates the capacity to govern responsibly.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana VI, G.A.G.Ph. baron van der Capellen, Hendrik Jacob van de Graaff, Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Ratu Mas, Pangéran Aria Ngabèhi
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Batavia, Cirebon, Tegal, Pasuruan, Surabaya
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Algemeen Rijksarchief (ARA 2791 No. 14), Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade, Raad van Indië, Land Leases from Javanese Princes (*Landverhuur*), Border Enclaves (*Grensdistricten*), Royal Divorce, Proposed Regency Deposition

### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[31 Dec 1825]**<br>*(Part 3: Attitude of the Sunan)* | **Council of the Indies on Sunan Pakubuwana VI:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Secret Dispatch:* Concerning confidential letter of Resident of Surakarta (22 October 1824/1825) regarding the critical state and political condition of the Surakarta Kraton.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Van de Graaff's Opinion:* Council member H.J. van de Graaff replies that the Sunan must submit himself strictly to previous contracts concluded with the Dutch.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Threat to Colonial Security:* As long as the Sunan's behavior lacks obedience to previous treaties, peace is liable to be disrupted and government security placed in grave jeopardy.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Six Months Without Reform:* Notes that six months have passed with zero improvement in the Sunan's posture since the appointment of Resident J.I. van Sevenhoven.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Agrarian Leases in Border Districts:* Colonial government resolves to secure leases on expansive tracts of land from Javanese princes, prioritizing border enclaves: in the West/North toward Cirebon and Tegal, and in the East toward Pasuruan and Surabaya. |
| **[Part 9: Proposed Deposition]**<br>*(Divorce from Ratu Mas)* | **Extravagance, Domestic Crisis & Proposed Regency:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Rash & Extravagant Conduct:* Secret dispatch of 22 October describes the court of Solo endangered by the Sunan's reckless, extravagant behavior, which imperils regional stability.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Royal Divorce Scandal:* Crisis exacerbated by the Sunan's abrupt demand for a divorce from his consort Ratu Mas, daughter of Pangéran Aria Ngabèhi.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Disqualification from Rule:* The Sunan's erratic behavior is deemed incompatible with sovereign rule, rendering him unfit to retain operational control of the government.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Contemplated Deposition:* Recommends that sovereign power be removed from the Sunan and placed in hands of a regency for as long a period as necessary until he demonstrates the capability to rule responsibly. |
