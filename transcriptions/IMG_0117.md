# Transcription of IMG_0117.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0117.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief / ANRI *Djogja Letters* [Dec 1825 – Feb 1826], January–February 1826).
- **Date Range:** 1826-01-09 to 1826-02-03
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** On 9 January 1826, Resident Van Sevenhoven writes to Surakarta emphasizing the critical need for an immediate certified translator in the Javanese language to replace the deceased Chevallier. The same day, while rebel detachments remain active in the immediate environs of Yogyakarta, positive intelligence is received regarding the progress of the colonial expedition to pacify Madiun. On 14 January, Van Sevenhoven reports to General De Kock on competitive auction bids for the river customs tollgate (*bandar*) of Pantonary, where bids dramatically surge from f. 586 to f. 3,540 per month. On 18 January, Yogyakarta authorities discuss an unusual colonial resettlement project with the Resident of Kedu: a proposal to receive Dutch families displaced by the catastrophic 1825 floods in the Netherlands (*Holland*) and settle them upon newly allocated agricultural lands in the Principalities (*Vorstenlanden*). On 25 January, military disbursements record Pangéran Suryaningprang (son of Paku Alam I) commanding the Pakualaman Dragoon corps, alongside Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing), who receives f. 415 per month for his troops. On 31 January, Van Sevenhoven issues a general police proclamation strictly forbidding citizens from discharging firearms or shooting in the vicinity of Yogyakarta. On 3 February 1826, official transition of supreme colonial authority takes place as Governor-General Van der Capellen transfers administration to Commissioner-General Leonard Pierre Joseph burggraaf Du Bus de Gisignies.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Jan Izaäk van Sevenhoven, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Suryaningprang (Radèn Mas Salya / Pakualam II), Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat (Tan Jin Sing), Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. van der Capellen, Commissioner-General Leonard Pierre Joseph burggraaf Du Bus de Gisignies, Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Solo), Semarang, Madiun, Pantonary (river tollgate), Netherlands (Holland), Vorstenlanden, Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Javanese language translator, Madiun military expedition, river tollgate (*bandar*) leasing auctions, Dutch flood victim resettlement scheme, Pakualaman Dragoons, weapons discharge prohibition, Commissioner-General transfer of power.
