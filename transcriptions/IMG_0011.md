### Main Text

> For some time in Djogja Princes DP [Diponegoro] and MB [Mangkubumi], both guardians of the young Sultan H.B. [Hamengkubuwana] V, have left the Capital and have RESISTED the Dutch government, and have with ever exciting population by inflammatory writings (letters) and have perpetrated Robbery and Murder, and have worked for general Resistance.
>
> In view of the fact that the Dutch government wants nothing else than the peace and welfare of the Javanese princes as well as their subjects, Has therefore sent me here with full authority and power. After having come to an agreement with the court of Solo [Surakarta], who have once more showed quite clearly their industrious faith in the Dutch govnt. The following resolutions have hereby been determined:
>
> **1.** That supreme authority vested in de Kock during period of REBELLION.  
> **2.** All officials of both courts to follow orders given by de K [de Kock] via Patihs.  
> **3.** All officials who have attached themselves to the causes of DP [Diponegoro] and MB [Mangkubumi] can hope for continued clemency from their princes if they give themselves up.  
> **4.** All those who persist in REBELLION will be most heavily punished.  
> **5.** In order that no one may plead IGNORANCE this order will be sent to all officials at the courts and countryside.  
>
> **[given at Solo 1st August 1825.]**
