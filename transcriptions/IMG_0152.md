# Transcription of IMG_0152.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0152.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Algemeen Rijksarchief, ARA 2791, No. 14, Part 3 / Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade, 31 Dec. 1825, Continuation)
- **Date Range:** `1819-11-25` to `1825-12-31`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of the secret policy deliberations of the Council of the Indies (*Raad van Indië*) dated Batavia, 31 December 1825, addressing the governance of Surakarta and fundamental fiscal reform across the Principalities (*Vorstenlanden*). The Council resolves that should Sunan Pakubuwana VI be formally suspended from royal executive powers, governance over the Surakarta Kraton will be entrusted to a tripartite Regency council comprising the Resident of Surakarta, Panembahan Buminata, and Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II. The Council then undertakes an exhaustive examination of the proposal to abolish internal transit tollgates (*bandar*) across the Vorstenlanden. While recognizing that the total destruction of the tollgate system is indispensable for rural pacification, the Council highlights that these customs posts generate an indispensable annual revenue of approximately 1,000,000 guilders (f. 1,000,000) for the colonial state treasury. Consequently, the Council determines that the tollgates cannot be abolished until an alternative revenue source is secured, proposing instead a sweeping policy of territorial annexation across the Principalities if the Sultan and Sunan grant consent. The notes reference earlier administrative measures (25 November 1819) establishing fixed tariffs for tollgates in Surabaya and Semarang to suppress corruption, and conclude with deliberations regarding a general decree banning ethnic Chinese from residing or leasing farms within the Vorstenlanden.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Resident of Surakarta, Panembahan Buminata, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, G.A.G.Ph. baron van der Capellen
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Yogyakarta, Vorstenlanden (Principalities), Surabaya, Semarang, Batavia
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Algemeen Rijksarchief (ARA 2791 No. 14), Besluiten van den Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade, Tripartite Regency Council, Abolition of Tollgates (*Bandar*), 1 Million Guilders Revenue, Territorial Annexation (*Annexatie*), Fixed Tariffs (1819), Banning Chinese from Vorstenlanden
