## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0141.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (A.D. Cornets de Groot Papers, pt. 6, IXe, Koninklijke Bibliotheek, The Hague)
- **Date Range:** `1825-09-22` to `1825-09-23`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official directives and emergency administrative instructions issued in Surakarta as colonial high command mobilizes for the major military expedition against Dipanagara's rebel headquarters at Selarong. On 22 September 1825, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock instructs Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry to march on 23 September toward Klaten and Yogyakarta, transferring the direct civil administration of the Surakarta residency to Assistant Resident A.D. Cornets de Groot. De Kock directs Cornets de Groot to preserve harmonious diplomatic ties with the Sunan's Kraton, confer continuously with Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) and Lieutenant Portier regarding military security, maintain strict surveillance over the volatile Sukawati region, and secure logistic provisions for advancing army columns. On 23 September 1825, MacGillavry issues a comprehensive memorandum (pp. 25–34) delineating Cornets de Groot's wartime duties: maintaining liaison with Pangéran Buminata, Mangkunagara II, and Patih Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II; handling sensitive affairs through Buminata and Sasradiningrat rather than disturbing the Sunan; preparing mutual military defense with Mangkunagara and Portier in case of insurrection; packing all available treasury silver in small transport cases for immediate dispatch to Yogyakarta; preserving meat and salt stocks with Lieutenant Portier to manufacture *dèngdhèng* (dried field rations) for combat troops; and maintaining close intelligence watch over outlying hotspots, specifically Gagatan, Serang, Kalangbret, Rawa, and Madiun.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, A.D. Cornets de Groot, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), Lieutenant Portier, Pangéran Buminata, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Sunan Pakubuwana VI
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Solo / Sala), Klaten, Yogyakarta (Yogya), Sukawati (Sragen), Gagatan, Serang, Kalangbret, Rawa, Madiun
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Koninklijke Bibliotheek (The Hague), Cornets de Groot Papers, Acting Resident of Surakarta, Kraton Surakarta, Mangkunagaran Legion, Military Logistics (*Dèngdhèng* / Rations), Treasury Silver Specie
