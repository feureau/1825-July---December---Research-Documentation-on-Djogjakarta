## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0163.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Diary of Van der Capellen, July 1825 – Jan 1826*; published as *"Aanteekeningen van den Gouverneur-Generaal Baron van der Capellen over den opstand van Dipanegara in 1825"*, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], 1860, vol. 11, p. 367).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-01 to 1826-01-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Critical scholarly analysis and extracts from the personal diary (*aanteekeningen*) of Governor-General Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen during the latter half of 1825 at Buitenzorg (Bogor), recording the colonial government's response to Pangéran Dipanagara's outbreak. The notes shed striking light on Van der Capellen's character: an amiable, well-intentioned administrator who was overly influenced by subordinates and lacked thorough, independent judgment, missing opportunities to de-escalate the crisis. Van der Capellen's reflections at Buitenzorg in July 1825 confront the existential threat to Dutch supremacy across Java. Deliberations center on root causes and radical diplomatic countermeasures, including the proposed restoration of Sultan Sepuh (Hamengkubuwana II)—deposed by the British in 1812 and exiled to Ambon—to pacify Yogyakarta. Carey charts the royal lineage: Sultan Sepuh was the grandfather of Dipanagara and great-grandfather of the child monarch Hamengkubuwana V (then six years old). Van der Capellen evaluates the chaotic reports from Yogya Resident A.H. Smissaert following the botched expedition to Tegalrejo, noting with alarm that Smissaert completely misunderstood Dipanagara as mere religious fanaticism and that Smissaert's disordered dispatches revealed a panic-stricken state of mind requiring a calming spirit. Concurrently, Surakarta Resident H.M. MacGillavry reports that Dipanagara had assembled in quiet border villages near Solo, purchasing armaments and mobilizing banditti and rural outlaws.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry (Resident of Surakarta), Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Buitenzorg (Bogor), Yogyakarta, Tegalrejo, Surakarta, Ambon (Moluccas), Java.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Governor-General (*Gouverneur-Generaal*), Personal Diary (*Aanteekeningen*), *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* (TNI 1860), *Kasepuhan* Dynasty, British Interregnum (*Tusschenbestuur* 1811–1816), Tegalrejo Expedition, Disordered Dispatches, Border Villages (*Désa*), Outlaw Levies.
