# Transcription of IMG_0055.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0055.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia, ANRI 'Archief van Jogja' [AvJ] 1825 / *Translaat Brieven: Beginning of the War*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-15 to 1825-08 (Javanese calendar: Vrijdag, 6 Besar Bé 1752 / Friday, 22 July 1825)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's notes extracting intercepted intelligence reports and letters addressed to Patih Danureja IV (*DR IV*) in mid-July to early August 1825; records Tua Guru / Tyagaragas (spiritual teachers) active around Mt. Majasta near Tembayat; Majali reports on 18 July that Pangéran Suryadipura (*Suria DP*) plans to leave Yogyakarta for Majasta; Pusparéja reports that Dipanagara appointed Semarawijaya as a Tumenggung to mobilize followers across Tembayat; Ngabehi Mertadiwiria reports Dipanagara and Mangkubumi staying at Téligong west of the Progo river in the residence of the head of the Tyagaragas; Paku Alam I reports on lands in Selarong belonging to Radèn Tumenggung Rajaniti (Surakarta bupati) and Pangéran Adinegara; MacGillavry (Resident of Surakarta) issues emergency orders on Friday, 6 Besar (22 July 1825) to Surakarta bupatis in Banyumas (Radèn Tumenggung Cakrawidana and Radèn Tumenggung Bratadiningrat) to capture Dipanagara and Mangkubumi if they enter their districts; records rebel movements at Karangkajen along the Imogiri road and Radèn Tumenggung Umararija sent to Kojoran to rally armed irregulars (*brandals*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Pangéran Suryadipura, Semarawijaya, Pusparéja, Ngabehi Mertadiwiria, Paku Alam I, Pangéran Adinegara, Radèn Tumenggung Rajaniti, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II, Radèn Tumenggung Cakrawidana, Radèn Tumenggung Bratadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Umararija.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Mt. Majasta, Tembayat, Téligong (west of Progo River), Selarong, Banyumas, Dimoyo (Kedu), Karangkajen (Imogiri road), Kojoran.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Translaat Brieven, Tua Guru / Tyagaragas (ascetic holy men / hermits), 6 Besar Bé 1752 (22 July 1825), Brandals (rebels / guerrilla fighters), Mobilization of religious networks.
