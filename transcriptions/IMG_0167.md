# Transcription of IMG_0167.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0167.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Aanteekeningen van den G.G. Baron van der Capellen over den opstand van Dipo Negoro in 1825*, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* [TNI], new series, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387).
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-24 to 1825-07-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official journal entries of Governor-General Van der Capellen recording the arrival at Buitenzorg on 24 July 1825 of the sudden and completely unexpected news (*"op het aller onverwachtst"*) of Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi's open rebellion, coupled with Resident Smissaert and Assistant Resident Chevallier's botched military assault on Tegalrejo. Smissaert provides no political or administrative rationale for Dipanagara's flight other than blind religious fanaticism, while his panicked dispatches reveal an agitated temperament entirely unequal to the crisis. Simultaneously, Acting Resident of Surakarta H.M. MacGillavry reports that Dipanagara had already been covertly purchasing armaments and enlisting bandits and rural outlaws in border villages within Yogyakarta territory, while providing categorical assurances that the Surakarta Court, the Sunan's troops, and the Mangkunagaran Legion under Pangéran Mangkunagara II would remain steadfastly loyal and could march to Yogyakarta's relief. On 26 July, the Council of the Indies in Batavia approves the immediate appointment of Lieutenant-Governor-General De Kock to assume supreme civil and military command at Semarang and the Princely Courts. Van der Capellen records his bewilderment: *"Such an uprising in the midst of the calm and peaceful island of Java was an occurrence which I had not envisaged, and from which the consequences were incalculable the more so because the causes were totally unknown to me."* Van der Capellen resolves that while he must remain at the administrative center in Buitenzorg, his second-in-command and designated successor must take charge in the field.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Godert Alexander Gerard Philip baron van der Capellen, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, P.F.H. Chevallier, Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Pangéran Mangkunagara II (Prangwedana), Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Buitenzorg (Bogor), Yogyakarta, Tegalrejo, Surakarta, Semarang, Batavia, Java.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Governor-General (*Gouverneur-Generaal*), *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië* (TNI 1860), Council of the Indies (*Raad van Indië*), Supreme Command / Plenipotentiary (*Volmacht*), Tegalrejo Assault, Fanaticism, Border Désas, Mangkunagaran Legion, Princely Courts (*Vorstenlanden*).
