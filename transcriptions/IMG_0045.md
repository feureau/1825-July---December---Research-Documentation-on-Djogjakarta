# Transcription of IMG_0045.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0045.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Nahuys van Burgst Collection, B.L.P. 616, Port. No. 9 / No. 2, pt. 2: *Procès-Verbal van Raden Mas Alip, 3 Augustus 1825*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-03 (Context: 1822 to 1825-08-03)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Peter Carey's analytical extraction and summary of the 3 August 1825 official deposition (*Procès-Verbal*) given by Radèn Mas Alip (eldest son of Pangéran Dipanagara) to Lt.-Gen. Hendrik Merkus de Kock; documents Dipanagara's persistent pressure on the late Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV to take up arms against the Dutch and retake alienated territories such as Kedu; records Hamengkubuwana IV's refusal based on his friendship with Resident Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst; details Dipanagara's undisguised satisfaction upon the sudden death of Hamengkubuwana IV in December 1822 ("Thank God... now everything will go better"); records princely recruitment, noting Pangéran Adisuryo was the first to join while Pangéran Suryobronto remained loyal to the regency; identifies Dutch officials marked for assassination upon the outbreak of war (P.F.H. Chevallier, J.G. van den Berg, D.W.J. Bouwens); establishes Dipanagara's inner advisory council, comprising Demang Buro Resso of Poonghoongan (Pajang), two Arab religious scholars (Sheikh Ahmad and his son-in-law Achmat) as chief advisers, and intimate personal retainers (Moedo, Wiriodikromo, and Djomohamad).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Radèn Mas Alip, Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV, Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst, Pangéran Adisuryo, Pangéran Suryobronto, Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier, J.G. van den Berg, D.W.J. Bouwens, Demang Buro Resso, Sheikh Ahmad (Achmat), Achmat (son-in-law), Moedo, Wiriodikromo, Djomohamad, Hendrik Merkus de Kock.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Kedu (Kedoe), Pajang (Padjang), Poonghoongan (Pungungan), Tegalrejo.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Procès-Verbal (sworn deposition), Causes of War dossier (No. 2), Arab ulama advisers, Demang mobilization, Assassination hit-list.

### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Dipanagara, Prince (Pangéran Dipanagara)** (`DP`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 10). Primary leader of the Java War.
  * **Radèn Mas Alip** (`R.M. ALIP / Jong Pangeran Diponagoro`): Listed under *Sons of Dipanagara* (line 48). Eldest son of Dipanagara; gave sworn testimony to General De Kock on 3 August 1825.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana IV** (`H.B. IV`): Listed under line 14. Younger half-brother of Dipanagara (reigned 1814–1822).
  * **Pangéran Adisuryo** (`PRINCE ADI SOERJO`): Listed under *Royal Princes* (line 85). Son of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; joined Dipanagara's rebellion.
  * **Pangéran Suryobronto** (`SOERIA BRANTA`): Listed under line 88. Son of Sultan Hamengkubuwana III; father-in-law of RM Alip; Kraton loyalist.
  * **Nahuys van Burgst, Huibert Gerard** (`Nahuys`): Listed under *Dutch Officials* (line 125). Resident of Yogyakarta (1816–1822).
  * **Chevallier, Petrus Franciscus Henricus** (`Chevallier`): Listed under line 134. Assistant Resident and Secretary of Yogyakarta.
  * **Van den Berg, J.G.**: Listed under line 140. Colonial official and commission member.
  * **Bouwens, D.W.J.**: Listed under line 137. Resident of Semarang and commissioner.
  * **Demang Buro Resso** (`BOURA RESSO`): Demang of Poonghoongan in Pajang; principal rural troop organizer.
  * **Sheikh Ahmad** (`Scheikh Ahmad`): Arab ulama and key religious councillor to Dipanagara.
  * **Moedo**: Listed under line 175. Trusted scout and retainer who warned Dipanagara of Dutch plots to capture him.
  * **Wiriodikromo & Djomohamad**: Intimate personal retainers (*panakawan/sentana*) of Dipanagara.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Djogja [Yogyakarta]**: Listed under line 427. Capital of the sultanate.
  * **Kedoe [Kedu]**: Listed under lines 435, 461. Annexed residency demanded back by Dipanagara.
  * **Padjang [Pajang]**: Listed under line 434. Fertile agricultural domain between Yogyakarta and Surakarta.
  * **Tegalrejo**: Listed under lines 431, 460. Dipanagara's country residence and headquarters of mobilization.
* **Terms:**
  * **Procès-Verbal**: Sworn official judicial deposition / interrogation report.
  * **Demang**: Rural administrative official / head of subdistrict.
  * **Causes of War**: Archival dossier title in the Nahuys van Burgst collection.
