### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana VII (Pangéran Purbaya)** (`PURBAJA`): Reigned as Sunan of Surakarta (1830–1858) (line 21). Heavily indebted to European estate renters in the early 1820s.
  * **Johannes Augustinus Dezentjé** (`HBM Dezentjé`): Wealthy Indo-European estate renter, indigo/sugar planter of Ampel (Boyolali), and major royal creditor (line 385).
  * **William Stavers**: Former British officer and prominent European land renter in the Vorstenlanden (line 385).
  * **Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry**: Resident of Surakarta and acting Resident of Yogyakarta (line 319, 346).
* **Institutions & Economics:**
  * **Land Rentals / Debts**: Heavy debt burdens accumulated by Javanese princes to European land renters, a primary economic grievance leading to the Java War following Van der Capellen's 1823 land-lease cancellation decree (*Besluit* of 6 May 1823).
  * **Guilders**: Netherlands Indies currency.
