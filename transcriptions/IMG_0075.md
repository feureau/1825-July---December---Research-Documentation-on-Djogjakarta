# Transcription of IMG_0075.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0075.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters, July 1825 Synthesis)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-18 to 1825-07-19
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Scholarly analytical synthesis of the Smissaert archival collection (ARA 4132) detailing the diplomatic breakdown immediately preceding the outbreak of the Java War (18–19 July 1825); records the verbatim texts of the joint declaration dispatched by royal co-guardians Pangéran Dipanagara and Pangéran Mangkubumi following their first consultation on 18 July; Dipanagara affirms that his sole objective is the dismissal of Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*DR IV*), swearing loyalty to his lawful monarch (*lawful prince*), while Mangkubumi declares he shares Dipanagara's demand for the Patih's dismissal while fearing to meet Resident Smissaert again; summarizes Acting Resident Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry's urgent letter from Surakarta warning that the Demang of Desa Grojogan has marched with 100 armed men under Dipanagara's orders, and that spies reveal an insurgent battle plan to strike Pacitan (*Pattitan*) on the south coast before launching a coordinated assault on Yogyakarta; tracks the reports of Mantri Wiraprana sent to Danureja IV during his rural tour, confirming Dipanagara's military mobilization at Tegalrejo—directing villagers to gird long sarongs as combat belts, mobilizing royal tiger hunters (*Tuwa Buru*), and receiving the tax submission of Demang Nitikrama of Sampar.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih / DR*), Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry, Mantri Wiraprana, Demang of Desa Grojogan, Demang Nitikrama of Sampar, Sultan Hamengkubuwana V (*lawful prince*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Tegalrejo, Surakarta, Desa Grojogan, Pacitan (*Pattitan*), Sampar.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132), Co-guardians' joint ultimatum, Dismissal of Patih Danureja IV, Planned southern campaign against Pacitan, Evacuation of civilian property (*small people hidden goods*), Tiger hunters (*Tuwa Buru*), Combat waistcloths (*kain sarong tapih / pinjung*).
