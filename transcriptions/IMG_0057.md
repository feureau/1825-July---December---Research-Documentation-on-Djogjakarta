# Transcription of IMG_0057.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0057.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (KITLV H 698 b; ANRI 'Archief van Jogja' [AvJ] 1825: May/June 1825)
- **Date Range:** 1825-05-24 to 1825-06-23
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official dispatches from Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert to Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*DR IV*) demonstrating colonial administrative detachment and the provocative road-building program in the weeks immediately preceding the outbreak of the Java War; on 24 May 1825, Smissaert rebukes Kraton nobles for failing to maintain road works and shade trees around the palace, demanding the riding path to Kota Gede be widened into a carriage highway extending to Plered and Tanjung Tirto (Peter Carey queries whether this was to facilitate a sugar factory); orders Pangéran Mangkubumi and the Kauman religious scholars (*priests / pradikan*) to urgently repair the impassable road behind Kauman and Mangkubumen; mandates immediate repairs on the lime-transport road (*kalk*) to Gamping, and the highway to Sultan Hamengkubuwana I's ancestral *pesanggrahan* at Ambarketawang passing Java Jewug / Java Pakis; projects future road works linking Ambarketawang to Kasihan and Gamping to Warak; on 18 June 1825, Smissaert notifies Danureja IV that he is vacating Yogyakarta to reside permanently with his family at his country estate in Bedoyo (*Bèdaya*) on the lower slopes of Mount Merapi, intending to visit Yogyakarta only twice a week (Mondays and Thursdays) to conduct government business; on 23 June 1825, Smissaert requests extensive road and bridge repairs across the Eastern Mancanagara (*Mancanegara regencies*) to enable a private holiday tour to climb Mount Lawu, inspect the Candi Sukuh antiquities, and travel through East Java.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), Pangéran Mangkubumi, P.J.F. Louw (military historian/cartographer cited in Carey note).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta Kraton, Kota Gede, Plered, Tanjung Tirto, Kauman, Mangkubumen, Gamping, Ambarketawang, Kasihan, Warak, Bedoyo (*Bèdaya / Ngabean Bedoyo*), Candi Sukuh, Mount Lawu, Eastern Mancanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Resident dispatch (*Res -> Patih*), Road building / forced corvée labor (*heerendiensten*), Carriage roads (*riding path / carriage way*), Sugar factory speculation, Royal pleasure retreats (*pesanggrahan*), Country residence (Bedoyo estate), Antiquarian tourism vs. administrative neglect.
