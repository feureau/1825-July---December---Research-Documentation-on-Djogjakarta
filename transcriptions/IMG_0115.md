- **Date Range:** 1822-12-28 to 1825-12-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resolution of the Governor-General in Council (*Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*) dated 31 December 1825, extracting determinations based on the Vorstenlanden Commission's report of 25 May 1825 regarding frontier land leases and unliquidated planter debts. The strategic incorporation of Jabarangkah and Karangkobar is ratified to buffer the borders of Kedu, Semarang, and Pekalongan Residencies and establish rationalized fiscal land rents. Because coffee cultivation across these frontier estates proved relatively poor, aggregate capital reimbursement owed to former European leaseholders is calculated at f. 268,921, scheduled for reimbursement in staggered government installments of f. 50,000. An official audit enumerates the private leased estates whose indemnity accounts remain unliquidated as of December 1825: Ampel, Asinan, Keranbang, Jempu, Setasuan (Setas), and Plaor. Among these, the estate of Jempu-Mandera Burug is ordered retained under direct government sequestration pending recovery of f. 5,500; while the Kadisana-Kabastan estate, owned by British planter W. Cotes, is approved for reimbursement of f. 6,600 pursuant to the initial government resolution of 28 December 1822.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. van der Capellen, W. Cotes (British planter / estate lessee).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Jabarangkah, Karangkobar, Kedu, Semarang, Pekalongan, Ampel, Asinan, Keranbang, Jempu, Setasuan, Plaor, Mandera Burug, Kadisana, Kabastan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Handelingen en Resolutiën van de Gouverneur-Generaal in Rade*, frontier border defense, unliquidated private estates (*landhuur*), estate sequestration, capital restitution installments.
