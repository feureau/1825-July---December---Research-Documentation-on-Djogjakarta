# Transcription of IMG_0100.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0100.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Collectie De Kock No. 181 / ANRI *Djogja Letters* [1825], dispatch from Smissaert to De Kock, 13 August 1825).
- **Date Range:** 1825-08-13
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Smissaert sends an urgent secret dispatch to Lieutenant-General De Kock reporting the findings of a messenger sent to Selarong bearing a letter from De Kock. Dipanagara could not be found at Selarong, having departed on the road to Pacitan to recruit soldiers for a general offensive against Yogyakarta fixed for 6 Sura (20 August 1825). Pangéran Mangkubumi receives De Kock's letter but tears it up in defiance after taking counsel with Pangéran Abu Bakar (cross-referenced to *Babad Dipanagara*); both declare they will await the Dutch and Paku Alam at Selarong. The intelligence courier observes massive numbers of armed rebels at Selarong, equipped mostly with pikes and 13 rifles. Construction is actively underway on a man-high defensive perimeter wall enclosing Dipanagara's garden retreat at Selarong, while the cave of Guwa Secang remains solitary. All main communication roads to Selarong are barricaded and closed, notably at Jejeran / Jara-an, Sumber, and Bantul, where rebels are heavily armed with slings and pikes. The rebel leadership formalizes sovereign wartime titles: Pangéran Mangkubumi is designated *Pangéran Singalaga*, Dipanagara is acclaimed *Panatagama*, and Pangéran Adinagara assumes the title *Sénapati Ingalaga*. Mangkubumi interrogates Dipanagara's patih (Suryéngalaga / earlier Mas Ngabéhi Mangunreja) as to whether five captured servants of Patih Danureja IV have been executed yet, and boldly declares to the messenger that the insurgents will wrest control over the child Sultan Hamengkubuwana V "at any cost."
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Pangéran Abu Bakar (Dipawiyana I), Pangéran Adinagara (Suryéngalaga), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Suryéngalaga (Mas Ngabéhi Mangunreja, patih to DP), Sultan Hamengkubuwana V, Paku Alam I.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Selarong, Pacitan, Yogyakarta, Guwa Secang, Jara-an (Jejeran), Sumber, Bantul.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie De Kock, secret duplicate letters, *Panatagama*, *Pangéran Singalaga*, *Sénapati Ingalaga*, pikes, rifles, slings (*bandring*), Sura (Muharram), garden retreat wall, council of war, *Babad Dipanagara*.
