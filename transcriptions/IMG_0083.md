## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0083.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Leiden University Library BPL 616: *Collectie Nahuys van Burgst* Port. 9 No. 9: *Djogja Correspondence / Letters of Ngabèhi Wiraprana to Patih Danureja IV*)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-15 to 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Carey abstracts primary intelligence reports and escalating diplomatic correspondence from the Nahuys van Burgst collection (BPL 616 No. 9) during the fateful days leading to the outbreak of hostilities at Tegalrejo. On 15 July 1825, court informant Ngabèhi Wiraprana reports to Patih Danureja IV regarding Dipanagara's radical shift in demeanor: the Prince has instructed rural supporters to wear long white priestly vestments (*kain putih*) and ordered the professional tiger hunters (*Tuwa Buru*) of Kramatdesa and Mangir to mobilize the rural underworld south of Yogyakarta. An attempt by Pangéran Mangkubumi on Thursday to counsel caution fails because Dipanagara's thoughts appear deeply agitated, and when Demang Nitikrama delivers village tax revenues, Dipanagara returns the cash with direct instructions to purchase paddy to feed assembling insurgents. On 18 July, Resident Smissaert sends a private letter to Danureja IV assuring the Patih of absolute Dutch backing and trust. On the evening of 19 July, Dipanagara dispatches his famous, deceptively cordial letter to "his brother" Resident Smissaert, acknowledging past marks of esteem, insisting that if he harbored grievances he would express them openly and frankly (*rondborstig*), and professing continued respect for the government. On 20 July, Smissaert responds with an uncompromising ultimatum, charging that Dipanagara has gathered armed levies from Kedu, Sultan's appanages, and Gunung Kidul (*Gudhang Punuk*) inside his dalem, accumulated weapons and grain, and directly threatened the peace and public order of Mataram.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Ngabèhi Wiraprana, Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Demang Nitikrama, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert, Major Huibert Gerard Nahuys van Burgst.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Tegalrejo (Tegalredja), Kramatdesa, Mangir, Kedu (Kedoe), Gunung Kidul (Gudhang Punuk), Mataram.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Leiden Univ. BPL 616 No. 9, Tuwa Buru (tiger hunters), white kain (priestly robes), padi procurement, royal dalem, peace and orderliness of Mataram (*rust en orde*).
