# Transcription of IMG_0179.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0179.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes on the Java War (1825–1830) and Yogyakarta History (Extracts from Governor-General G.A.G.Ph. Baron van der Capellen's Private Diary, *Tijdschrift voor Neêrland's Indië*, 1860, vol. 11, pp. 360–387)
- **Date Range:** `1826-01-01 to 1826-01-04`
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Final entries and strategic summary from Governor-General Van der Capellen's private diary on the eve of his departure in early January 1826; the strategic stalemate persists with no decisive change in the military situation; widespread disease, malaria, and tropical dysentery severely incapacitate colonial troops garrisoned in Yogyakarta; Pangéran Arya Kusumodilaga of Surakarta is pushed back to Diele in Banyumas; noble casualties among Madurese allies include sons of the Prince (Panembahan) of Pamekasan killed in combat; an insurrection erupts in Banten, exhibiting clandestine religious and political connections to the Yogyakarta holy war; imperial diplomacy regarding exiled Surakarta princes: Pangéran Pringgakusuma and Pangéran Mangkubumi (uncles of Sunan Pakubuwana VI), who were banished to the Moluccas (Ambon) following the 1817 British/Sepoy conspiracy and permitted to return to Java in 1824 under close police surveillance in Batavia, are sent back to Surakarta in January 1826 to help stabilize royal court loyalties; senior colonial administrative shake-up: Adriaan Mauritz Theodorus Baron de Salis is appointed Resident of Kedu to re-establish civil administration, while Assistant Resident of Kendal Holmberg van Beckfelt is promoted to full Resident of Pekalongan.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Baron van der Capellen [Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies], Pangéran Arya Kusumodilaga [Surakarta prince], Panembahan / Prince of Pamekasan [Madura ally], Pangéran Pringgakusuma [uncle of Sunan PB VI / 1817 exile], Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta) [uncle of Sunan PB VI / 1817 exile], Sunan Pakubuwana VI, Adriaan Mauritz Theodorus Baron de Salis [Resident of Kedu], Holmberg van Beckfelt [Assistant Resident of Kendal / Resident of Pekalongan].
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djokja), Surakarta (Solo), Diele, Banyumas, Pamekasan (Madura), Banten, Batavia (Jakarta), Ambon, Kedu (Kedhu), Kendal, Pekalongan.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Garrison Epidemics, 1817 Sepoy Conspiracy Exiles, Return of Exiled Dynasts, Administrative Appointments (Resident of Kedu / Pekalongan), Banten Sympathy Rising.
