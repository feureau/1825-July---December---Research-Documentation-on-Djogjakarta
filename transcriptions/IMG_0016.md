### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Petrus Franciscus Henricus Chevallier** (`Chevallier`): Assistant Resident of Yogyakarta.
  * **Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert** (`SMISSAERT / Res`): Dutch Resident of Yogyakarta (line 345).
  * **Pangéran Suryabrangta [Purwadiningrat]** (`JOENA BRANTA`): Son of HB III, brother of Dipanagara (line 73).
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradiningrat**: Brother of Ratu Ageng (line 257).
  * **Radèn Mas Tumenggung Brotodiningrat** (`BROTO`): Kinsman of Ratu Kencana (lines 31, 217).
  * **Major Radèn Tumenggung Wiranagara** (`WIRO NEGORO`): Commander of the Sultan's bodyguard troops (line 281).
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana [Mangkunagara II]** (`Mangko Negoro / MN`): Ruler of Mangkunagaran principality in Surakarta (lines 27, 360).
* **Places & Military Installations:**
  * **Fort Vredeburg [FORT]**: Dutch fortress located immediately north of the Kraton of Yogyakarta.
  * **Kraton**: The royal palace of Yogyakarta.
  * **Bantul [Bantool]**: District south of Yogyakarta on the route to Diponegoro's base at Selarong.
* **Currencies & Terms:**
  * **Guilders [gs.]**: Dutch Indies currency (line 532).
  * **Mangkunagaran Legion [MN TROOPS]**: Elite auxiliary corps established under Daendels (line 544).
