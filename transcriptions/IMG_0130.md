- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20 to 1830-03-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Historiographical and tactical analysis compiled from H.F. Aukes' *Het Legioen van Mangkoe-Negoro* (1935, pp. 68–72) contrasting colonial and insurgent military doctrine during the Java War. Colonial field tactics (p. 68) depend heavily on infantry fire discipline, utilizing alternating lines delivering salvos and rapid fire, while Central Java operations (p. 69) deploy mobile flying columns of 350 to 400 men (typically 100–120 Europeans and 30–40 cavalry). In contrast, an anonymous military commentator assesses Dipanagara's irregular warfare (p. 70): initially commanding few regular soldiers in 1825, Dipanagara mobilizes rural villages by directing armed peasants to assemble before local *lurahs* or *demangs*; contingents are deployed to destroy bridges and launch formless guerrilla ambushes using slings (*bandring*), pikes, and rifles, avoiding pitched bayonet combat. Leveraging terrain familiarity (p. 71), rebel forces retreat into villages fortified with sharpened bamboo caltrops and stakes (*ranjau*) to neutralize colonial cavalry charges. To overcome the unreliability of transient village levies (*who often fled during battle*), Dipanagara and his chief religious adviser Kyai Maja (*Kjahi Madja*) reorganize their forces into disciplined standing regiments (*Bulkios, Turkios, Arkios*) recruited from South Kedu, Banyumas, Bagelen, Yogyakarta, and Surakarta, receiving regular food rations and pay; fighting in flexible open order, they repeatedly inflict tactical defeats on Dutch columns. Finally (p. 72), the introduction of De Kock's *bèntèng stelsel* (fortified redoubt system) isolates Dipanagara from rural levies, noting that only once throughout the five-year conflict did Dipanagara's army defend a fixed fortified position—at the ancient Kraton of Plered (*Plérèd*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*), Kyai Maja (*Kjahi Madja*), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, H.F. Aukes.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Central Java, South Kedu, Banyumas, Bagelen, Yogyakarta (*Jogja*), Surakarta (*Solo*), Plered (*Plérèd*).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Het Legioen van Mangkoe-Negoro* (1935), Colonial Flying Columns (350–400 men), Salvo & Rapid Fire, Guerrilla Warfare (*bridge demolition, ambush*), Slings (*bandring*), Bamboo Caltrops (*ranjau*), Regular Standing Regiments (*Bulkio / Bulkiya, Turkio, Arkio*), Open-Order Formations, Bèntèng Stelsel, Fortified Defense of Plered.
