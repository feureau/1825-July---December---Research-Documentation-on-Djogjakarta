# Transcription of IMG_0069.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0069.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ARA 4132 / Collectie Smissaert; Yogya Letters 1825 / No. 7)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official Dutch translation of the joint, coordinated ultimatums dispatched from Tegalrejo by Pangéran Dipanagara and senior royal co-guardian Pangéran Mangkubumi to Resident Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert on the morning of Wednesday, 20 July 1825, immediately following Mangkubumi's defection; Dipanagara informs the Resident that although requested to meet at Mangkubumi's dalem, he cannot appear due to well-founded fears of betrayal and arrest; Dipanagara explicitly defines his sole war objective: *"I demand nothing more than that Danurêja should be dismissed and I never had the intention to set myself against the Government or against my King"*; Pangéran Mangkubumi follows with an extraordinary personal declaration explaining that he cannot return to the Residency because his nephew/son Dipanagara cannot be persuaded (*niet mee te overhalen is*) to abandon his demand for the dismissal of Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja IV, and that Mangkubumi now shares Dipanagara's exact conviction, while swearing he harbors no treasonous designs against the colonial government.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pangéran Dipanagara, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Anthonië Hendrik Smissaert (Resident of Yogyakarta), Radèn Adipati Danureja IV (*Patih*), Sultan Hamengkubuwana V (*my King*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Tegalrejo, Dalem Mangkubumen, Yogyakarta Residency.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Collectie Smissaert (ARA 4132 No. 7), Defection of Pangéran Mangkubumi, Princely solidarity, Demand for dismissal of Patih Danureja IV (*Danurêja should be dismissed*), Loyalty to child Sultan (*against my King*), Fear of treacherous arrest.
