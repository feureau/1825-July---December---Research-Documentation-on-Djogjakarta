# Transcription of IMG_0132.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0132.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Research Notes (Typewritten transcript of Radèn Mas Suwangsa's report to Lieutenant-General De Kock in ARA Collectie De Kock / H.F. Aukes, *Het Legioen van Mangkoe-Negoro*, Bandung 1935, p. 81)
- **Date Range:** 1825-07-28 to 1825-07-31
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Concluding section of the firsthand debriefing report submitted by Captain Radèn Mas Suwangsa (Mangkunagaran Legion) to Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock detailing rebel war aims and tactical plans formulated at Selarong in late July 1825 (Aukes 1935, p. 81; ARA Collectie De Kock). Suwangsa concludes his testimony regarding the rebel princes: having abandoned Yogyakarta, they resolved that reconciliation was impossible; in particular, they explicitly targeted the total destruction and expulsion of all Europeans from Java (*"the destruction of all the Europeans"*); furthermore, following Pangéran Dipanagara's strategic injunctions, they were ordered to refrain from large-scale combat until the sacred Islamic month of Sura (Muharram), when they intended to inaugurate their general offensive with a coordinated assault on Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Suwangsa, Pangéran Dipanagara (*DP*), Lieutenant-General Hendrik Merkus de Kock, H.F. Aukes.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (*Jogja*), Selarong.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Anti-Colonial War Aims (*destruction of Europeans*), Sacred Month of Sura (Muharram), General Offensive against Yogyakarta, Mangkunagaran Legion, Collectie De Kock.
